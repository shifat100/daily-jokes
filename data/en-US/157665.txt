Lesbians And Sunflower Seeds
So me and my friend were talking and the topic somehow moved on how to properly eat sunflower seeds.

"So yeah, I just crack it with my teeth and then use my tongue to get the seed out." I say, and she looks at me strangely, "Really? I just eat the whole thing." She says. Now I cannot help but give her a look as if she is crazy. "What the hell? You're not supposed to eat the shell." I say and she frowns and glares at me, saying:

"Well sorry but I never learned to spit shit out of my mouth."
