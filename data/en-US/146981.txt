Two women are waiting to get into Heaven

Carol takes some time before she answers. "Well, I had been married for five years and my husband started to act strange over the past month. I find out that he was cheating on me and since he worked at home, I decided to catch him in the act by coming home early. I get there and I can smell a perfume that is not mine going up the stairs to our bedroom and see him in bed in nothing but his underwear. I open the closet, the bathroom, and I searched everywhere while cussing him out when I felt a sharp pain in my arm and died from a stroke."
Jill takes in all that Carol has said and says " If you would have checked the garage freezer first, he would be here instead of us."
