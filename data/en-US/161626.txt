Who's My Daddy (slightly NSFW)
A newborn baby is crawling around the waiting room, looking for his father. 

He crawls towards a group of men sitting down at the corner of the room.

The baby starts asking each person "Are you papa?", but "No." was the common reply.

Before he could leave, one man approaches the baby, and recognizes his face.

"Are you papa?", the baby asks,

"Yes, I am, Little One"

The baby starts to poke his father's forehead with force, then says, 

"How does that feel?"
