Muhammad was feeling down.
He goes to his wife and says "I feel bad that you are all covered up and cannot show off your true beauty to the world". The wife replies "Do not worry, my beauty is only for you and Allah to know".

Still feeling down, he goes to his other wife and says "I am sad that you are so young and already married, for you have not had a chance to develop your own identity". She replies, "Do not worry for my identity is to give my youth to you and Allah".

Muhammad then goes to his third wife and says "I am melancholy that you must share me with the other wives, you must get lonely". The wife replies "So you don't find me beautiful or young anymore!".
