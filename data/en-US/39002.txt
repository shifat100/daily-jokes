This post is to tell the tale of my foray into the world of experimental and cutting edge comedy to serve as an example and warning to those brave souls who continue, day in day out, in the salt mines of original material. 

What j did was blend too long standing comedic methods into one which I had expected to produce a neat sum of their two parts. Instead there was an adverse reaction that creates a sort of exponential comedic product 

Simply combine "that's what she said" jokes with "yo momma" jokes and suddenly you have "that's what your mom said" as a quick response to any sort of statements possible 

What you will find is people are caught completely off guard and the usual shields in place for yo momma jokes we all acquire naturally at a young age in he public school systems somehow sont respond in time and so people get angry before they realize it, I fact this joke isn't even that funny just powerful. Whenever you would normally say "that's what she said" try to go ahead and replace it with "that's what your mom said" and let me know how it goes for you.
