A woman fails her driving test for the third time.
The examination conductor questions her, "You are driving a bike. There's a straight narrow road ahead with a dead end. An old man and a young man are ahead. What do you do?"

"Obviously, I would run over the old man. The young guy has a long life to live. Why kill a young man?"

And again, she failed. She asked the conductor, "Why am I wrong?"

The conductor said, "The correct answer was "Apply the brakes of the vehicle."
