There was this bar and blondes were celebrating in the corner, with beers shouting, "41 days, 41 days!!!!" The bartender watched as they more came in and joined the celebration. He was finally so curious that he went over and said, "Why are you celebrating shouting, '41 days, 41 days!!!'?" And a blonde held up a 4 piece puzzle box and said, "The box said 4 to 6 years, and it only took us 41 days!!!!!!!!!!!!"
