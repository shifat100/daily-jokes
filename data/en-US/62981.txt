A guy is getting ready for prom...
He goes to get a tux but when he gets there, he has to wait in line. And wait. And wait. And wait...

Finally he gets the tux and goes to get a corsage. But yet again, he waits in line. And waits. And waits some more...

He picks up his date and they get to the prom but they see a line at the door. So they wait for what feels like forever...

Finally, they are at the prom when his date asks him to get some punch. There is no punch line.
