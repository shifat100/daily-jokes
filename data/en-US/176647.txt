Months after months of fighting, freeing slaves and doing justice, the king finally returns home, to see his beautiful wife.  That night when the king unlocked the belt, he found that the guillotine was used quite a few times.

All the knights took off their pants.  All but one of their knights have had their manhood chopped off.  The king ordered all 11 injured knights to the gallows. He promised the cheating knights a slow and painful death and the last of his faithful, royal knight an appropriate reward.

"Speak knight" said King Arthur. "How should I reward thee for thy faithful service?  I can give thee mountains of gold, a hundred virgin girls, or half the land of my kingdom.  Speak! And receive thy reward!"

The knight kept his mouth shut tight.  He shook his head, and walked away, refusing the handsome reward the king promised, in tears.

And hence began the tale of the silent knight, who never opened his mouth in public, especially in front of the king.
