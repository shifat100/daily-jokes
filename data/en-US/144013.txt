Cherry Hill
A High school class in Nevada is missing two of their students, the teacher after already half way through the class becomes angry that they had still not shown up.  She runs to the door to go find them, when a girl walks in, the teachers says, "Where have you been all this time?!?". The girl replies, "I've been on top of Cherry Hill." She tells her to go to her seat and says," I've never heard of Cherry Hill before." Suddenly there is a knock on the door the teacher rushes over and opens it. "The young boy says sorry I'm late." The teacher replies,"What is your name?" He proudly says, "My name is Cherry Hill."
