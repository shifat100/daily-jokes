L. Ron Hubbard walks into a comic book convention
L. Ron Hubbard walks into a comic book convention. He gets in line to get a comic book signed by Stan Lee, and a little girl asks him: "Aren't you too old for comic books?" Hubbard smiles and replies: "Comic books are for everyone, not just for kids."
After a few minutes Hubbard reaches Stan Lee and hands him the comic book and asks him to sign it.
Stan Lee then asks Hubbard: "When is the point of this joke going to come?" Hubbard answers: "I don't know. I didn't ask to be in this joke, some guy on Reddit just included me in it. All I wanted to do was to get my book signed and then go home and perhaps watch some television." Hubbard then 
took his newly signed comic book and went home. At home, he turned on his television and proceeded to watch a few episodes of Dallas.
