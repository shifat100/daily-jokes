A woman walks into a hair salon...
And she tells a receptionist "Make me look beautiful". The receptionist then hands her a business card.  Incredulously, the woman asks the receptionist "I did not know Father John could cut hair".
The receptionist then responds: "He doesn't. You come to us if you want a haircut. You go to Father John if you need a miracle". 

Everyone talks about dad jokes, my mom just told me this one.
