A Mexican immigrant goes to Walmart.
A few days after entering the United States, and after days of walking, a mexican immigrant who does not speak English goes to WalMart in order to get some new clothes. He looks confused so an employee tries to help him out. First they go to the shirts, but the Mexican, shakes his head; then they go over to the pants and he again shakes his head. They move on to the next section and he gets very excited: "¡Esso, si que es!".
