An Englishman, a Frenchman, a Spaniard and a German...
An Englishman, a Frenchman, a Spaniard and a German are all standing watching a street performer do some excellent juggling. The juggle notices that the four gentlemen have very poor view, so he stands up on a large wooden box and calls out:

"Can you all see me now?"

 "Yes."

 "Oui. "

 "Sí."

 "Ja."
