I was in the mall looking for a specific shoe store...
... And got tired of walking around. So I went up to the information counter and asked this old man at the counter. 

"Excuse me sir? How do I get to this shoe store?" while pointing at a brochure I had. 

"Alright kid, you go down here and turn left. You take the escalator up a floor. Coming out, you head straight ahead. You'll see another information counter just like this."

"Alright, then? "

"Ask the lady at the counter how to get there."
