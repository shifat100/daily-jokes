Numerical Marriage Counseling
Two married mathematical units, Pi and i, are in couple's counseling.  Immediately, i tears into Pi.

"We always argue and he won't let me get a word in," i says to the counselor.  "He just goes on and on and acts like I don't exist."

"Oh, get real!" Pi fires back.

"See what I mean?!" she exclaims.  "He's just being irrational!"
