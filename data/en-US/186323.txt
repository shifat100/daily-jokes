A chinese man walks into a pet shop.
One of the employees goes up to him and says "Welcome sir! How can I help you?"

The chinese man says "Yes, actually. Do you have any dogs?"

"Of course sir! Anything in particular?"

"What do you have?"

"We have some chihuahuas, a few Jack Russells, and a Boxer. What would you like?"

"I'll take a chihuahua"

"Are you sure?"
