A guy walks into a bar with a frog on his forehead and says "Bartender give me a scotch on the rocks."
The bartender returns with the drink, and being kind of curious about the pair, asks:
"If you don't mind my asking, how'd you end up with that thing on you?" 

Immediately the frog replies, "I don't know, it started out as a wart on my ass!".
