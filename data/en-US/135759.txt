A fire breaks out in a kindergarten...
A fire breaks out in a kindergarten on the 5th floor of a building and firefighters come at the place to help save the children. They stretch a safety net to catch the kids with it. The teachers start throwing the kids down on the net... 

They throw a white kid - the firefighters save it. They throw another - also saved. Then the teachers throw a black kid and the firefighters move away, the kid falls on the ground and dies. They throw another - also falls to the ground.
One of the firefighters starts yelling at the teachers - "Stop throw the burnt ones!"
