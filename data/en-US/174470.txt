Another costume
A guy goes into a costume shop.


He says, "I'm going to a costume party, I want to
go as Adam."


The girl brings out a fig leaf. He says, "Not big
enough."


She brings out a bigger one. He says, "Still not big
enough."


She brings out a huge fig leaf. He says, "Still not
big enough."


She says, "Listen, Ace, why don't you just throw it
over your shoulder and go as a gasoline pump?"
