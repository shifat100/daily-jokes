Another Polish joke, translated
At JFK Airport in New York, a guy walks into an elevator and sees a woman in uniform, mini skirt, jacket - some stewardess. The guy intrigued by this situation says:

\- Hello, you fly USA Airways?

A woman does not say anything, just looks at him in surprise. The guy he thought so what, he'll try again:

\- Flugen Sie Lufthansa, ja? [You fly Lufthansa]

More confused, the woman looks at him and says nothing. Oh well, he thought the guy ... I'll try again:

\- Volare sinora Alitalia? [You fly Alitalia?]

Then the woman says:

\- A w mordę chcesz palancie?! [Do you want me to smack you in the mouth, jerk?!]

\- Aha, LOT Polish Airlines!

Edit: added German and Italian translation for the unilinguals.
