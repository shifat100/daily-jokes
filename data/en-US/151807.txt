There was a beet farmer...
There was a beet farmer in his field with his Son harvesting crops. He said to his Son, "Take this basket and bring it into the house." The Son said, "Okay", and started walking to the house. The Son then tripped on a vine and spilled the beets all over the ground. The Father looked at his Son and said, "What did you do?!" The Son looked at his Dad and then said, "I'M DROPPIN' BEETS!"
