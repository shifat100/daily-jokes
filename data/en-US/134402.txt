Bob Dylan's tour bus
....breaks down outside the Arkansas state fair. He and his crew decide to visit the fair while the bus is being repaired, and they walk around all the tractors and chickens and tools and such. At the center of the expo, they find a small tent with a pretty girl outside. Bob introduces himself and asks her what's in the tent. She tells him the fair is sponsored by Durango Overalls, and inside the tent is a game where you can remove your clothes, put on a pair of Durango Overalls, and win a prize if you're fast enough! Bob doesn't understand the game, but she ushers him inside, excited at having a celebrity play the game for the sponsor. He is met by the VP of Marketing for Durango Overalls, a loud woman who aggressively leads him up to a small stage. She tells the audience that "THE Bob Dylan himself will be taking the Durango Overall Challenge!" The crowd applauds. She turns to Bob, "Are you ready?" Bob does not know what to be ready for and doesn't respond, so she waits an awkward second and yells, "GO!". Bob stands there and does nothing. The woman tells him to remove his clothes, "start with your shirt Bobby!", but he won't do it. She gets right up in Bob's face, points to the judges and says, "Change! They are a timin'!"
