An American, Frenchman, Mexican, and German are all attending a performance.
The group is having a tough time seeing the performer.  He is just about to start the show and they are in the back looking for an opening.  The performer sees this and stalls a few minutes as he watches the group.  Just when they've nearly given up hope, they find a platform to hop onto and now have a clear view of the performer.  He asks "Can everyone see me now?"
The group answers, "Yes, Oui, ,Sí, Ja!"
