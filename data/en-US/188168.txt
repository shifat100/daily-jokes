Band Names as Descriptions of Bowel Movements
I haven't seen this game on here before, but it's one of my favorite games to play with friends, and I want to see what you guys come up with!



The way you play is basically in the title: You say the (actual) name of a band/songwriter/musician, and think of the bowel movement it describes. I'll give a few examples to get started:



Muddy Waters

The Rolling Stones

My Bloody Valentine

Cream



You get the idea. So, /r/jokes, what are some of your favorite bowel movements?
