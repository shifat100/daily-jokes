A black guy is walking home from the store
His paper bag bursts and one of those large round cheeses falls and rolls down the steep hill. A Mexican sees the cheese grabs it and runs home to his hungry family. He tells his wife to make nachos with the cheese. His wife disagrees and says she should make something more substantial like tacos. He tells his wife that the cheese was provided by the Lord and she must make nachos and tells her he heard the Lords voice all the way home saying "hey man that's nacho cheese"
