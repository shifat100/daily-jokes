A Christian priest and a Jewish rabbi sit by a lake, asking religious questions.
The priest asks "What's your depiction of hell?" The rabbi sighs and looks over the lake. "Well, we believe that hell is more of a feeling than a place.. Any time we feel so ashamed that we are so far from God, that is our own personal hell, and we believe no feeling could be worse than that." They both become lost in thought, staring over the lake for a brief moment, before the rabbi looks over and asks "What's your depiction of hell?" The priest looks over and replies "Fact."
