Production meeting
One day, there was a production meeting for a film which I was a part of (sound department) and everyone involved sat around in a circle and introduced each other. 

Little did we know all know, a special guest by the name of Sade, who was a co-executive of the film, joined us and sat in the background under her request. So the director got up and said his name, as did the writers, actors, etc. It would eventually be my turn to introduce myself, so I got up and prepared to utter the words. 

I mean I was ready for this moment; I made sure I had a good rest the night before, recited my lines, binged on the entire 3rd season of the Steve Harvey show, but I digress. 

And before I could get a single peep, Sade quickly got up and sang out loud, "He's a boom operatorrrrr, booooooom operatorrrrrr!"
