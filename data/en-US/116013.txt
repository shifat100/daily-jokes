God and Satan were walking along a beach
They were conversing over their differing opinions on humanity when they came upon a smooth, round pebble in the middle of the beach, seemingly out of place as there were no other pebbles on the entire expanse of the beach.

Satan bent over, picked up the pebble and asked "My Lord...what is this?"

To this God replied "My child, this is religion."

Satan turned the pebble over and over in his hand while pondering God's statement....then a sly grin spread across Satan's face as he looked at the pebble almost lovingly and said....

"Let's organize it."
