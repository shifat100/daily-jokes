A blonde, a brunette, and a redhead are stuck in a room
... when a gunman breaks in. He demands for all the money they have.  
Desperately, the brunette points out the window and shouts, "Look! A tornado!" The gunman glances back and the brunette escapes.  
The redhead sees that this works, and points at the ceiling. "Oh my god, a tarantula!" Once again, the gunman is caught off guard and the redhead escapes.  
Finally, the blonde points at the wall and says, "Fire! Fire!"
