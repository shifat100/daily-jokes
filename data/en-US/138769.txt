Something weird is happening to the stars
they begin moving around the night sky, some go out, some light up. Eventually the stars form the words: "Who are you?"

People of Earth gather all the light sources they can, torches, flashlights, etc., go to a desert and form the words: "We are people of Earth".

Stars begin moving again, and after a while form new words: "People of Earth, fuck off, we're not asking you!"
