A letter to someone of great importance
Dear love,
  
  
It's almost 3 am here and I can't sleep.  
I want to sleep.  
But I can't.  
    
I've been procrastinating.  
Now I have less than 3 hours to whip up a presentation, a lab report and take care of other stuff.  
Please, time, please.  
Stop for me.  
Stay.  
I need you.  
I can see you packing up your stuff and leaving as I type this.  
I know you must despise me, after all these years of neglect and ignorance.  
I also know that there's nothing I can ever do to make it up for you.  
But let me tell you this, and I'll never, ever bother you again — I want you to know that I finally, finally, realized how important you are to me, when I looked up from my computer after staring at it for hours and saw the clock - 3:00 am, and remembering that I had homework.  
  
  
Love,  
A procrastinator who just realized your importance.
