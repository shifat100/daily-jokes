I Don't Think He is Crazy...
Inside an insane asylum, a few doctors decided to run some tests on a few men that seemed be recovering remarkably fast. They put 7 men inside a room with 1 huge door resembling a garage door. This room was all white with a fake door drawn on the wall. At first the men seemed to just stay to themselfs. However after a couple hours they seemed to belive the door was real, all but one of them. They said "we are going to escape boys!" "We just need to get this dam door open!" After hours of observation the one man in the corner refusing to help, just sat in the corner laughing  at them, almost as if he knew it was a fake door. The docters noticed this and turned their atention to him. He just continued to laugh as the rest of the men failed to open the door. Soon a docter said " I don't think he is crazy... lets get him in another room... lets ask him a few questions.  In a smaller white room the docters ask the man "Why where you laughing at the rest of the guys... did you know?" The man then replies, "I laugh because they think there going to get that "Door" open... but how can they do that when I have the only key!"
