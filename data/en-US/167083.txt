A man takes his son out for his very first beer
Now his son his no ordinary man, he was born with only a head, no torso, no arms, and no legs. But his father loved him anyway, and raised him well. 

When his son turns 18, the father decides to take him out to the pub for his first beer to celebrate. 

He sits his son down on the bar and takes a seat, asking the bartender for two beers, for his sons birthday. 

His son, the head, with the help of his father, drinks down his first beer, and all of a sudden a torso magically pops out ! The bartender is amazed and father and son are ecstatic. 

They order another beer, and again, Two arms pop out of the boys brand new torso, Father and son look at each other, grinning ear to ear, and order a third beer. This one the son is able to drink himself, with his brand new arms. Two legs pop out and his body is now complete. Filled with beer and excitement, the boy hops up on his new legs, And runs out of the pub calling out to everyone in town to look at his brand new body. Just as he steps onto the road, he gets hit by a speeding bus, killed instantly. 

His father begins to cry, and the bartender places a hand on his shoulder and whispers to the father *"He should have quit while he was ahead".*
