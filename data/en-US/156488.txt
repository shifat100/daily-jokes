15
A man is walking down the sidewalk next to an insane asylum. From the other side of a large wall he hears this insane cackling.

"14. Hee hee hee. 14!"

Curious, the man spies a hole in the wall and decides to take a look.

As the man puts his eye to the hole to see what is happening a finger pokes him right in the eye.

"Ha ha ha ha 15. 15!!"
