3 explorers were hiking through unexplored territory...
When they decided this newly discovered expanses needed a name. The first explorer turns to the two behind him and says, "this place needs a new name, we can each come up with a letter and see what we can make." The other two agreed it was a phenomenal idea and proceeded. The first said "C, eh." The second responded "N, eh." While the last explorer finally said "D, eh." That's how the word Canada was born.
