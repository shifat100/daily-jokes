A student asks his teacher a question.
During a lecture, a young male student has the urge to ask his sex education teacher a question.
"Mr. Dickson, I have a question about the male body" he says quietly.
"Well what is it then Carl?"
"I know puberty is when the penis starts growing but.... when does it stop..?"
The class went silent for a few moments.

"I don't know, but I'll let you know!"
