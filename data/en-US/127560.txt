What's a BRT ?
Two U.S. army officers once passed a new recruit who was on cleaning duty...

"Don't forget to clean off the B.R.T." said one of them, pointing to a big round metal object.

"Boy, how do you know all the names of all the different machinery?" marveled a fellow officer.

"And what does B.R.T. stand for, anyway ?"

"Big Round Thing," he answered.
