Apes are very intelligent...
After working very hard with a group of apes their zookeepers seemed to have had some success teaching them to read and write.

The apes begin to make remarkable progress. As they spent most of their budget on basic children's books the staff decides to use texts about animals from the gift shop to help advance the apes' reading abilities. One ape soars above the others, and one morning while being fed the zoo employee notices he is reading Darwin. So he writes a note to the ape asking how he likes Darwin. The ape ponderously writes out a message and returns it to his feeder. 

The note asks "Am I my keeper's brother?"
