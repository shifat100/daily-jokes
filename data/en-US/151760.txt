An American, German, and British soldier are captured by cannibals.  The cannibals say, "We're going to kill you, eat you, use your bones to make tools and your skin to make canoes.  You get to choose the way you want to die."

The British solder asks for a musket from the American revolutionary war.  The cannibals don't have one, so they send off for it and the British solder lives six more months while they wait for the musket.  The British soldier shoots himself and the cannibals eat him, use his bones for tools and his skin for canoes.
