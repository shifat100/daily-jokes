So, it's the 1st intelligence championship for blondes. The stadium is packed with enthusiastic blondes and the runner up who has eliminated all other blonde contestants is ready for the question that will win her the title. The question is: what is the sum of 5 + 8. The countdown starts and we can see she is really struggling and at the end answers 12. The host goes all "Oh, no, that was so close" but the whole stadium starts chanting "give her one more chance". So they do give her one more chance. The question is: what is the sum of 5 + 7.
