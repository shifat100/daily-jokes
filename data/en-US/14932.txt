Harambe and a friend walk into the local watering hole...
After a few drinks, Harambe asks for another mug of the house brew. His friend says, "Dude, you've had too much today. Just get some ice to suck on for a while until we leave." Harambe nods his agreement, and the bartender says, "So, just ice?"

His friend says, "Yep, just ice for Harambe."
