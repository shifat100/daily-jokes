French guy on Everest
There was a french guy climbing Mount Everest. Along with him there were a lot of english speakers who didnt understand french. Since the french guy could not speak english, he couldnt talk to anyone. During the climb they hit bad weather and an avalanche hit them. Most of the people were unharmed but the french guy got hit hard by the rocks. 
As he was about to take his last breath he said something in french and died.
