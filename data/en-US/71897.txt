Chapter 3: The attack of the Man-Ape!
Two weeks have passed since that day at the dojo and each day passed as slowly as the one before it. I'm soooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooooo bored it's not even funny. That butt-chinned boy has been as persistent as a fly; he's so annoying! I wish eh would just shoo off!
