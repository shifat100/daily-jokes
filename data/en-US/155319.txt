An Indian chief was having constipation...
So he went to the doctors, and told him his situation. The doctor said "so you are a trouble having a bowel movement?" When the chief responded with yes, the doctor prescribed medicine for him. And he told the chief to come in in a week. As promised, 7 days later the chief came in. The doctor asked "so have you moved yet?" When the chief responded no, the man gave him stronger medicine. This repeated with the chiefing claiming he had not moved yet, and the doctor prescribing him stronger medicine. On his fourth visit when the doctor asked him if he had moved yet, the man responds by saying  "we didn't have a choice, all the teepees are full of shit."
