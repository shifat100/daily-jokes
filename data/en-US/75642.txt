Nazi Germany
Its 1944 in Nazi Germany. At a concentration camp, a group of jewish prisoners are being led to the most recent building. Not long after its construnction, rumors of dreadful gaschambers had started to spread among the prisoners. Though none of those were confirmed.
As they enter the building, the prisoners are ordered to take of their clothes and enter a hall with faucets on the ceiling, as they will be taking a group shower. When the door of the showerhall closes several people go into hysterics, banging on the door and walls. Women and childeren are crying and some adults start praying, when suddenly the shower faucets start spraying water.
Water! Fear turns to joy, sobs turn to laughter and childeren start playing about when after a few seconds an old rabi notices a young boy crying in the corner. He goes to the boy and asks: "why are you crying? Its only water."
The boy turns and says: "well yeah, but do you see a drain around here?"
