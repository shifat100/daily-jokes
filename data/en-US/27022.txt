A man on a train...
...sticks his head out of the side window and enjoys the breeze blowing through his hair. A fellow passenger warns him - there is a narrow tunnel coming up soon, best pull your head back inside. The guy waves him away and keeps his head in the breeze. Two other passengers pull on his sleeve and urge him to come back inside. He laughs and ignores them too. As the train gets closer and closer to the tunnel the concerned passengers get the ticket inspector to try to get the man to come back inside. The ticket inspector urgently warns the man "Sir, there is a tunnel right ahead and if you don't get back inside you will probably be decapitated." The man is not concerned and replies: "Don't worry! I travel this line often and I'll know when to get back inside. I'll have enough warning because before we get to the tunnel we first pass four PIL-PIL-PIL-PIL-Pillars."
