A Jewish Boy and His Mother
A Jewish boy and his mother get on a bus in Israel. As they're riding to their destination the boy keeps looking out the window and pointing to things and asking his mother questions in Hebrew. She answers him each time in Yiddish. 

After several of these questions that are each time asked in Hebrew first and then answered in Yiddish, another old woman on the bus asks the mother, "Why do you keep answering your child in Yiddish instead of Hebrew?" 

The mother replies, "So he remembers that he's Jewish."
