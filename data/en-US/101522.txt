An elderly couple file for divorce...
... After 60 years of marriage. The lawyer they meet with prepares all the paperwork but stops before handing it to the couple to sign and asks;

"By all means I can let you do this, but please let me know why? Why after over half a century of happy marriage would you want to end it now? What could ruin such a long streak of love?"

"Well," replies the wife, "we wanted to wait until the kids were dead."
