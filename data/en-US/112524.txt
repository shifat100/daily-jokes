A turtle and a centipede
A turtle and a centipede are living together.

"Do we have any milk left?" Asks the centipede.

"No, but I can go buy some." Says the turtle

"No you are much too slow. I am quite faster than you, I will get the milk" Says the centipede.

The centipede walks off to go to the store. After 2 hours, he does not return. Concerned, the turtle gets up and walks to the front door only to find that the centipede is still putting his socks on.
