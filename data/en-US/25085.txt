An Irish Catholic man near death...

They were all shocked when he asked first for an Anglican minister to come to him and convert him to their Protestant faith. When asked why on earth, after a lifetime of fidelity to the Catholic church, would he ever do such a thing, he replied, "Because I'd rather a Protestant man leave this earth today, than a good Catholic!"
