Adele and Beyoncé in a Painting contest
Adele was in a painting contest with her good friend Beyoncé. Beyoncé was painting a realism piece of a glass of lemonade and Adele was painting and abstract piece of the sky falling.  Beyoncé used up all the warm colors because she thought Adele would only need blues and purples, but when it came time to paint the sun Adele had to resort to stealing (this was still a contest after all). So she snuck across the room and grabbed Beyoncé's paint tray but got caught on her way back. Beyoncé got mad and Adele was disqualified. All because Adele stole yellow from the other side.
