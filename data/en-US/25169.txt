A young woman is alone with her mother on her wedding day...
The mother glances over at her daughter, and says "dear, now that you're getting married, I need to tell you the most important thing you must do for your husband."


&nbsp;


The young woman looks at her mother, feeling a bit of trepidation. Swallowing, she finally says, "okay, tell me"


&nbsp;



The mother leans close and whispers, "you need to remind him— to make sure he truly knows—"


&nbsp; 


The young woman's eyes widen, anticipating what her mother is about to say. Her mother continues, "—that paper towels are expensive, and he's using too many of them."


&nbsp;


The young woman says nothing for a few moments, and then with a sigh she says, "I understand. I will tell him everyday."
