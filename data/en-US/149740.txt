The new zoo’s animals start arriving.
Unfortunately, the gnu arrives before the tile is installed in its cage.  Having no choice, they put the gnu in the cage.  The next morning, when feeding the gnu, the keeper discovers the tile has been perfectly installed.  He points this out to the head keeper who asks everyone who laid the tile. No one admits to the work, so on a whim, the head keeper puts the gnu in another cage with the tile stacked in the corner. To his surprise, the next morning the tile in the new gnu’s cage is perfectly installed.
Hearing of this, the owner called the animal supplier to see if there is something strange about this gnu.
After checking his records, the animal supplier says, “No, he is just a typical gnu and tiler too.”
Groans appreciated.
