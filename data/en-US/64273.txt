A man is offered a challenge
A man walks into a bar and the bartender offers him a drinking challenge. The man turns it down, but after a few drinks, he takes it on. The bartender tells the man that he first needs to drink a whole gallon of ghost pepper tequila. Then, in the back there is an alligator with a loose tooth. After that, there is woman upstairs who has never had an orgasm. So the man chugs the drink, crying half the time, then walks out back. After an hour of screaming, clawing, and biting, the Man walks back into the bar, and says, "Now where's that women with a loose tooth?"
