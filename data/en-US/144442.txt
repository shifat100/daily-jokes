Black Panties?

She has her legs spread so far I can see her black panties!"  The other man looked and said, "I see her but you're wrong.  She doesn't even have on panties, she just has a black bush!" They explained their argument and offered the boy $5 to ride by the woman, take a quick look, come back and settle the argument.  When the boy returned they gave him his money and asked who was right.  The boy replied, "You're both wrong, it's flies!"
