Chinese lady got eaten by a tiger - 7/25/2016 (Slightly NSFL)
CNN posted a news story on 7/25/2016 about two Chinese ladies who were driving through one of those drive-thru tiger safaris.  (They even have some frightening video footage.)  Apparently, they get into an argument and one lady gets out of the car.  Unexpectedly, a tiger grabs her and starts mauling her.  The other lady gets out of the car and tries to help the lady who's getting mauled.  Out of nowhere, another tiger grabs the lady (the one who was trying to help) and ends up eating her.  The other lady survived.  

It was very tragic but for me, the saddest part is what happened to the tiger who ate the lady.  That poor tiger... got hungry again after 30 minutes.
