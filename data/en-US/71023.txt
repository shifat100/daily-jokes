A man is put in jail for jaywalking
A man is put in jail for jaywalking and isn't too happy about it.  Immediately, seeing as his crime was stupid, he began searching for ways to break free from the jail.

Soon after entering the jail, he learns that his inmate is one of the greatest bomb makers in the world. They begin to find a way to make a bomb with only jail supplies so they can break out through the many walls of the jail.  It didn't take long for him to figure out how to make multiple bombs out of prison food.

Finally came the jail break day.  They set the first bomb in their cell and blew up that first wall.  Luckily nobody noticed and they continued until they stumbled upon another wall.  They set the bomb and blew up the second wall.

At that point the sirens began going off, so they sprinted until they got to another wall.  They then blew up the third wall as fast as they could.  Then they saw a window leading outside up high on another wall, so naturally, they blew up the final wall.

They saw ran for their freedom, but before they did, the bomb maker turned around and said, "That audience, is how you break the fourth wall."
