Two Old Men Arguing
So these two 80 year old men were eating food until one of them realized that he wanted what the other one was eating.


Old Man 1: Hey give me that oat meal, thats what I want not this cream of wheat.


Old Man 2: Well too bad. You asked for that cream of wheat.


Old Man 1: You give me that cream of wheat now!


Old Man 2: Over my dead body! 


Old Man 1: Fine I can wait five minutes.
