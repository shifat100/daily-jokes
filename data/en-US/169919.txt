5-year-old Loses Reelection
The five-year-old mayor of a small town in Minnesota lost his bid for reelection. The five-year-old mayor made a real effort to drum up support, by shaking hands, and kissing other babies.

…the mayoral race had some pretty stiff competition this year; the 5-year-old was running against a stray dog, a packet of taco mix, and a cardboard cutout of Eminem.
