So there's this killer
who goes around killing wealthy men and stealing their money. Each victim is killed in the same way; slashed throat. The killer claims a few more lives without being caught, with his crimes making national news.

A copycat killer soon emerges, killing wealthy men and stealing their money. The original killer is enraged and soon tracks the copycat down. He watches him as he goes into an alleyway and pounces; takes him by surprise and holds a dagger to his neck. "W-what you doing?" the copycat exclaims, "We can work together!!!"

"Sorry mate," the original killer responds, "It's a *cutthroat* business."
