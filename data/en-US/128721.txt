High IQ
A certain university in the Northeast has a highly regarded school of agriculture. It also has an innovative psychology department. These circumstances resulted in the various animals which were maintained at the school of agriculture being subjected to an extensive battery of tests designed to definitively establish the level of their intelligence. Astonishingly, one of the donkeys tested with an IQ of 186.  Therefore, his daily activities came under close scrutiny. Interestingly, it was observed that the other animals not only wouldn't associate with him, but some were overtly hostile. 
 
Apparently, even in the animal kingdom, no one likes a smart ass.
