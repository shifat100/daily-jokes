The mother-in-law arrives home..
The mother-in-law arrives home from the mall to find 
her son-in-law boiling angry and hurriedly 
packing his suitcase.


"What happened ?" she asks anxiously.


"What happened! I'll tell you what happened. 
I sent an email to my wife telling her 
I was coming home today from 
my business trip. 
I get home, and guess what I found?


Yes, your daughter, my wife, with a guy in 
our marital bed! This is unforgivable, 
the end of our marriage. 
I'm done. I'm leaving forever !"


"Calm down, calm down!" 
says his mother-in-law. "There is something 
very odd going on here. My daughter would 
never do such a thing! There must be a simple 
explanation. I'll go speak to her immediately 
and find out what happened."


Moments later, the mother-in-law 
comes back with a big smile.


"I told you there must be a simple explanation..
she didn't receive your E-mail !"
