Beverage Innovation
A major brewing company which also operates tourist attractions has responded to the governor's urging to expand Florida's non-tourist-based economy by finding a commercial use for the hordes of little green tree frogs which abound in the state.  They have found that these creatures have all of the hops that are needed to brew a fine beer.

Beer drinkers are anxiously awaiting the availability of the new 
product -- Froggy Froggy Brew.

Naturally, its commercial debut will be on next March 17.
