A father and son are playing catch in the backyard...
The son asks his father "Dad, weren't you really good at baseball in college?"

"Sure was.  Even had some scouters asking me about going pro"

"Why didn't you?  That would have been so cool!"

"Oh you know.  Other things in life came up.  You for one..." 

The dad trails off for a few seconds before his eyes light up.

"Though I guess I did end up going pro at something after all." The father says with a grin.

The son looks at his father quizzically "What do you mean?  I don't remember you telling me anything about that."

"I'm sure you of all people would know about this already" says the dad, holding back laughter.

The boy searches his memory for a moment but comes up empty handed.  "Ok dad, I give up.  What are you talking about?"

"I became a pro..."

"Yes?!"

"Creator"
