A redhead, a brunette, and a blonde are trapped in a dungeon.
The only way for them to escape is to walk up some stairs. There are a hundred stairs. Each one has a joke on it. If they don't read the joke, they die. If they laugh at the joke, they die. They confer amongst themselves, and decide that the redhead should go first. She makes it about halfway, then dies.
Next, is the brunette. She makes it a little farther.
Then, the blonde, being the only one still alive, goes. She makes it up 99 steps, then dies. 

Later, in heaven, the three girls meet. The brunette, who was watching from heaven, and asks the blonde,"How'd you make it so far?"

The blonde replies, and says "Well, I didn't get any of the jokes,"
