A man undergoes rigorous training to be a circus performer...
Every night the man comes home to his family physically exhausted, but every morning he gets up eager to get back to his training.

The stress starts to build up, and towards the end of the training no one sees him at all.

Finally he drags himself home after a week long absence and slumps into his favourite chair.

His family ask him what happened. 

He shrugged. "This last week has been in tents."
