Meeting the Parents
A guy and a girl are serious in the relationship, and she decides to bring him home to her parents. Him being on a motorcycle and traveling to a rainy place buys vasoline and a tarp to keep his bike safe from the weather. After a fair ride, the couple arrive at her parents house. Before they go in, she tells her bf that her parents have a funny house rule: the 1st person to speak after dinner is finished has to do the dishes. He laughs and brushes off the comment. The couple take their belongings inside and the bf meets the parents. As dinner is served, the family has a large conversation about everything and goes off without fail. As the food is finished, the bf notices that it's about to rain and goes and grabs the vasoline and the tarp from his bag. The father says "Ok, I'll do the dishes."
