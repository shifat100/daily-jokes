If Dr. Seuss were a convict (poem)
What's this in my hand?  
Behind your back?  
It's soap on a a rope!  
Whack whack whack!  

What's this in my sock?  
Tick tock, knock knock.  
A large steel lock!  
Chock chock chock!  

What's this in my breeches?  
I heard that you blab..  
Snitches get stitches!  
Stab stab stab!!
