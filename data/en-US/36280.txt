A man was watching a baker make pies...
After the baker rolled out the pastry, lined the pie plate, put in the filling he put on the top layer of pastry & carefully trimmed off the excess pastry. The baker then removed his upper dentures & proceeded to use them to tamp down the pastry around the circumference of the pie plate after which the baker reinserted his false teeth back in hi mouth.
The man watching this turned aghast to another man standing beside him & said "My god did you see what I just saw that baker do?"
To which the man replied "That's nothing. You should be here on Thursdays when he makes the donuts."
