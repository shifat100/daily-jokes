In a bus
In a bus, a woman was trying to breastfeed her child, but her child refused to drink. 


Woman: (angrily) If you don't want it, I'll give it to the man beside me!


After 20 minutes, the child still refuses to drink.


Woman: (became more angry) You really don't want it? I'll seriously give it to the man beside me!


Man beside her: Ma'am, you need to decide, I was suppose to be off this bus a while ago.
