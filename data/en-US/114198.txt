Bill Cosby raped me with a pudding pop
This was in 1988, when I had just moved to Hollywood to make it in film. I was just out of Ohio State with a degree in drama, and at the time I was determined to make it big.

I met Bill Cosby at a SAG dinner in West Hollywood. He was standing next to my friend Rebecca, and I edged into their conversation because I grew up loving the Cosby Show. They were just talking about the industry while I listened in on their dialogue. He seemed really friendly and talkative. I liked him because he seemed interesting and also interested in listening to me when I talked. 

Several hours later, and after a few drinks, he invited me back to his hotel room, because he justed to show me an early version of a new show he was working on. Once we got back to his hote, he sat on the bed and pulled out a bag from under the bed. Inside the bag was a VHS cassette tape labeled "The Cosby Mysteries." At that point, he raped me with a pudding pop.
