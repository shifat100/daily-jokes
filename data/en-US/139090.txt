An Englishman, Frenchman, Mexican, and German go to see a parade.
They all arrive together behind a large crowd, and can barely see the performers.  The ringleader is in front of the crowd making a speech when he sees these four men in the back.  He jumps up on a crate to be more visible to the men.  He yells back to them "Hey, can you see me now?"

Then men answer one at a time. "Yes." "Oui." "Sí." "Ja."
