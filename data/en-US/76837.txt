The tombstone
A man was knelt in front of a tombstone crying.  As a young woman walked by,  she heard him sobbing "why did you have to die? Why did you leave me to deal with this? Why, oh why? "  Sympathy welled up in her heart as she saw this broken man.  "I'm sorry was this your wife?" she asked. The man responded with a miserable look on his face. "No this was my wife's first husband!"
