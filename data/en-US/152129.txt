Jokey Joke-Maker
A young man is preparing to take his date to the prom.

First he needs to buy flowers. He arrives at the florist and see's that the line is really long. 

Once he has purchased the flowers, he then has to rent a tuxedo. He arrives at the store and much to his dismay, the line is really long.

After finally renting his tuxedo, he lastly has to rent the limo. He arrives at the rental place only to see that that line, too is really long.

After finally taking care of his pre-prom tasks, he picks up his date and they head to the dance. After much dancing, the young man's date asks him to get her a glass of punch. He heads for the punch-bowl...and there was no punch line...
