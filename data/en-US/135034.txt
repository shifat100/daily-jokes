Man Gets Fired For Prank Postcard Mailed To His Office
The story of Alan who was fired by his company from receiving a realistic prank postcard from an anonymous source can tell us that mail pranks can really ruin someone's life.

An anonymous person who bought one of the mail pranks from http://sendprankmail.com addressed to Alan at his work for revenge. After going through their website the person knew if he did something at Alan’s work place where the next issue with Alan comes up means suspension or no pay.  So the person bought one of the products and had a mail sent to Alan’s office address. The website produced a realistic customized mail envelope and sent it to his work place. 

Upon receiving this mail, his colleague staying in the same cubicle with him saw this and raised an alarm. This ugly incident was reported to the authority at his company and Alan was fired after they saw such envelope that they thought it was real. To a much great surprise, Alan is still trying to convince his work that it was prank mail, but who would believe Alan when he has been behaving badly. Good luck to Alan while he keeps trying to get his job back.

Alan also said he contacted SendPrankMail.com owners to get the information about sender however he is yet to receive a reply from them as it was anonymous mail.
