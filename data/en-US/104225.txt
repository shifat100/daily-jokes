A new app for forestry...
As a pretty techno savvy guy studying forestry in the U.K., I'm always on the lookout for new apps relating to forestry.

Needless to say, I recently found this great app for foresters who need help with felling trees. It's quite simple, they can upload images of trees/woodlands which need felling and other foresters who are looking for work can swipe right or left depending on whether or not they wish to offer some help and do some work.
By swiping right, you are then able to contact the person who uploaded the image about doing forestry work.

The name of the app you ask?

Timber.

Yeah, I'll show myself out.
