Did you hear the one about the neuroscientist who developed a way to use 100% of their brain?
He learned that he might not be using his whole brain, so he developed a special way of meditating and some special drugs that would allow him to use 100% of his brain.  
  
He locked himself away from the world so he had time to experience his new brain without interference.  
  
He spent years meditating and using special drugs to increase the number of connections between his neurons until he thought he had finally connected them all.  
  
He woke up one morning and...  
  
He had a seizure. Using 100% of your brain is called a seizure.
