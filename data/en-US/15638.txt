So a guys car breaks down next to a monastery...
And he realizes this is the famous one with the delicious fish and chip shop.

So he orders his fish and chips and as he's about to bite into one of the fries, he notices it is soggy and rotten.  He goes back to the window and demands to speak to the chef.

"Chef! You are serving rotten potatoes!"  The monk responds, "Ah, you'll need to speak to Brother Irvin.  I am in charge of the fish, he is the chip monk."
