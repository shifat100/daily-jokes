Despite his friends' advice, a young business man goes into a tattoo shop...
He sees another customer waiting to get a tattoo, 
"You know, my friends tell me that getting a tattoo when I'm young is a bad idea because when you get older, your skin gets wrinkly and the tattoo will start to look weird.  But I am a smart business man, and I believe in making smart investments.  That's why I'm here today to get a tattoo on my arm."

The other customer seemed confused, "But sir, based on your friends' advice, getting a tattoo would not be making a smart investment!"

"Ah, one might think that at first, but that's why I'm getting a tattoo of a strip of bacon!"
