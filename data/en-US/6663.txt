A reporter wants to hear people's opinion on the local meat shortage...
The reporter saw a group of women talking to each other on a street corner, and decided to interview them. 

The group consisted of an American, a Chinese woman, an Ethiopian and an Israeli.

"Excuse me, but could each of you give me your opinion on the local meat shortage?" asked the reporter.

"What's a shortage?" said the American.

"What's an opinion?" said the Chinese woman.

"What's meat?" said the Ethiopian woman.

"What the fuck does 'excuse me' mean?" said the Israeli.
