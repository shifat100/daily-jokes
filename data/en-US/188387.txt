Logical Days
Where I work we joke sometimes about "logical" days of the week when people don't have to work the typical Mon-Fri. So if someone has Monday off, their Tuesday becomes their "logical" Monday, and if they have Friday off, Thursday becomes their "logical" Friday.

Today, my co-worker made this joke:

"So if someone has a logical Friday that doesn't match up with their actual Friday, that means the true Friday is an 'illogical' Friday and they have a [complex](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_number) week."

Groan-worthy, I know.
