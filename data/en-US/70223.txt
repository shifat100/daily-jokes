A large man wobbles into a bar
and says to the barman, "A *round* of drinks please."

The barman gives him a few drinks and he gulps down the lot.

"A *round* of drinks again please barman."

So the barman gives him a few more drinks and he gulps down the lot.

"Another *round* of drinks please barman."

The barman, confused asks, "Why do you keep ordering a *round* of drinks, when it's only you that's drinking them?"

The large man replies, "Because I'm *fat*."
