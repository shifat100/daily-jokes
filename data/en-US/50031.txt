"Can you all see me now?"
An Englishman, A Frenchman, A Spaniard and A German are all standing watching a street performer do some excellent juggling. The juggler notices that the four gentlemen have a poor view, so he stands up on a large wooden box an calls out, "Can you all see me now?"
