A man was just coming out of anesthesia after a series of tests in
the hospital, and his wife was sitting at his bedside. His eyes
fluttered open, and he murmured, "You're beautiful." Flattered, the
wife continued her vigil while he drifted back to sleep.

Later, her husband woke up and said, "You're cute."

"What happened to 'beautiful?'" she asked him.

"The drugs are wearing off," he replied.
