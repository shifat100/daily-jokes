This Day in History, 1978
This day in history, June 12,1978, there was a large amount of [Basques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basque_Country_(autonomous_community) who died in a fire at an old local theater. The theater was packed full, and as the fire erupted the entire building's occupants flooded to the only exit in the building. needless to say, with the bottleneck the one door created, many people died in the fire.

That, Kiddies, is why you shouldn't put all Basques in one exit!
