Plenty of Jewish mother jokes, but a "Jewish father" joke? This is as close to one as I've ever heard ...
Haskell and Irving were sitting by the pool and one asks, 'How come you never got married?'

So Irving replies, 'Well, when I met girls I liked, I used to bring them home, but my mother never liked any of them.  This one was too tall, that one was too short, this one was too thin ...  Finally I met a girl that I really thought I'd marry.  I decided that this was it, if this didn't work I was going to give up.'

'But why did you think that your mother wouldn't find something wrong with this one too?'

'Well, this girl was JUST like my mother in all ways.  Same height, same build, same voice, same taste in clothes -- she really resembled my mother perfectly.  So I thought she wouldn't be able to find anything to object to.'

'What happened?'

'Well, my father didn't like her.'
