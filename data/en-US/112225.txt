A man goes to see a fortune teller
He sits down across the crystal ball from the fortune teller, and she begins to tell his fortune. Moments pass, and then the fortune teller begins laughing, first a small giggle, and then gradually building until the man can see tears running down her face.

Miffed, he stands up and slaps her. This sobers her up, so after a moment of massaging her face, she turns to him and says "What did you do that for?!"

And the man says "Well, my mother always told me to strike a happy medium."
