Its all natural
So there are three women at a bar, one with auburn hair, one with brown, and one with green. The bartender at the bar goes up to the auburn hair lady and is taking her order, getting her drink and then he asks her how her hair is so beautiful. She says as she has her hands go through her hair, "Its all natural, its all natural." He then goes over to the brown hair lady and asks her the same question after taking her order. "Its all natural she replies, its all natural." The bartender then goes over to the green hair lady and is taking her order then asks her how she got her beautiful hair so green. She sneezes into her hand then puts her hands through her hair saying "Its all natural, its all natural."
