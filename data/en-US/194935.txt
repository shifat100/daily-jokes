A woman`s garden is growing beautifully but the darn tomatoes won`t
ripen. There`s a limit to the number of uses for green tomatoes and
she`s getting tired of it. So she goes to her neighbor and says,
"Your tomatoes are ripe, mine are green. What can I do about it?"

Her neighbor replies, "Well, it may sound absurd but here`s what to
do. Tonight there`s no moon. After dark go out into your garden and
take all your clothes off. Tomatoes can see in the dark and they`ll
be embarrassed and blush. In the morning they`ll all be red, you`ll
see."

Well, what the heck? She does it. Next day her neighbor asks how it
worked.

"So-so," she answers. "The tomatoes are still green but the
cucumbers are all four inches longer."
