Indian names
An old Indian was telling some children how they were named. 

"After the mother has given birth, when they walk out of the tent, they name you the first thing they see. You, Soaringeagle, we're named after a majestic eagle flying in the sky. You, Standingbear were named after a fierce bear that was greatly feared... and you.... Twodogsfucking..... well...."
