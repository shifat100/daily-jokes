black mans pig
There once was a black man who wanted to have a pig. so he goes to the first farm in the neighbourhood, walks to the owner and tells him he wants to buy a pig. The farmer, who is willing to sell him one, explains him the things needed to keep it as a pet:

- First of all, and very important the pig will need food.

- No problem at all, it will have all the food it needs.

- And secondly the pig will need a place where it can walk freely and sleep comfortably

- That is also no problem. I own a big house.

- Well sir, it is your garden that needs to be big enough.

- No no, this is my new pet and it will live in my house.

- But sir, what about the stench then ? 

- The pig will get used to it.
