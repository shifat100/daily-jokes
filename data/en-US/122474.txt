Another blonde joke to top off the list.
An airplane is flying through the air across the Atlantic when the pilot realizes that there are five women holding on to the wings; four blondes and a brunette. The pilot proceeds to yell at the women and says that the plane can only land if there are two women holding on to each wing. Understanding that a someone would have to let go, the brunette releases her grip on the left wing in order to save the blondes and everyone on the airplane. Amazed by the brunette's noble sacrifice the blondes begin clapping.
