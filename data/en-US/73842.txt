Two catholics join a monastary...
Two catholic men decide to go totally devout and join a monastary. Deciding it looks a bit drab outside, they decide to plant a garden despite the wishes of the others in the monastary. A simple garden, but they know a friend with a nursery with roses, petunias, and some others. Right as they are about to put the seeds in, a chinese man comes along and convinces them not to, for it will go against the simple life and take their time, preventing them from doing service.

The moral of the story: Only Yu can prevent florist friars
