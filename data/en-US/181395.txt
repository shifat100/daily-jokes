The Legend of Toxic Chemical Smoothie
Sit right down and I’ll share the tale

Of how three Texas guys and one crazy female

Came up with the best inside joke there’s ever been

About the greatest band the world’s never seen

It started with a video game late one night

Toxic Chemical Smoothie sounded just right

For their imaginary band and its imaginary deeds

That evening’s laughter contained the joke’s seeds

They’d all talk about it from time to time

Whenever the memory came to mind

Till one Christmas she decided to send

Band logo t-shirts to each of her friends

Before they knew it, a fan club grew

Around this band that nobody knew

Orders poured in for t-shirts and tags

As everyone wanted to be part of the gag

The press picked it up and ran with the story

Launching the band to still greater glory

In less than a year, their fame spread far and wide

The only Rock legends who never even tried.

Now that I’ve told this story to you

There’s only one thing I’d ask you to do

Spread the word and share in the fun

Toxic Chemical Smoothie’s for everyone!
