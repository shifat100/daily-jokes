A boy was jumping on the train tracks
A boy was jumping on the train tracks shouting "24...24...24." When one lad comes along and asks him what he's doing. 
"I'm playing a game to see who can jump on the tracks the longest while shouting 24, come play with me!"
The both start jumping on the tracks with an intense game of "24" when the boy all of a sudden jumps off the tracks.
"Haha I win!" shouts the lad. Prancing in victory, the lad was unaware that a train came by and ran over the lad. The boy jumps back on the tracks and continues jumping "25...25...25."
