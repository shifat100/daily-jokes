Ashamed cow
One day a street cow walking slowly on the road. Her stomach full of grass because plenty of grass on the road side. She eaten a lot. So stomach full, no way to walk because of weight. Anyway she walking slowly. 

One guy was walking along with the cow. The cow looked the guy and continued walking. Again cow looking the guy and again walking straight. Meanwhile the cow shaking her tail, again shaking, again looking the guy and walking. 

The guy noticed the cow is looking his face randomly. Something somewhat the guy is thinking and looking her back. Her tail is shaking left and right. Left and right. Left and right. The guy slowly touched her back and taken hand. The cow got sudden sense, she again looked the guy face, he is walking normally. She had a doubt that something touched or not. 

The guy again touched her back, suddenly the cow looked his face and stopped. The cow's eyes looks like love. She felt shame and her head down, again looked the guy and again head down. The guy stumbled there for some minutes, the cow also.
