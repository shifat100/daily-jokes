A man's job...
A man's job sent him to Kuwait in the late 80's and he had to visit many small villages. He began to observe a strange custom. The men always seemed to be walking 20 or 30 feet in front of their wives. Curiosity got the best of him and he finally asked a pair of men why they did that. The men replied that for thousands of years men had preceded their wives as an expression of their superiority. Ten years later, now a war torn country, the man returned and happened by the same village. He saw the same men but to his amazement, their wives were now walking 10 paces ahead of their husbands. He immediately had his driver pull over and confronted the men asking what could possibly change a millenia old tradition. They replied, "land mines."
