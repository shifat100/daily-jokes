Wilson & Jesus?
So Jesus went to the tailor to get a new robe made.  He loved his new robe so much that he went back to the tailor, a Mr. Wilson, to let him know.  

Wilson, always the business man, told Jesus, "Jesus, if you liked it so much, let's go into business together.  You've got great a great reputation, and I make great robes... together I know we can be a really successful team!" 

Jesus said, "Wow, Wilson, I love the idea of teaming up with Jerusalem's best tailor.  Let's do it...but what should we call ourselves?"

"What about 'Wilson & Jesus'?" suggests Wilson.

"Hmmm," replied Jesus, "That's not a bad idea, but I've got just one change... I think it would do a lot better if we called it 'Lord and Tailor'?"
