On the Oregon Trail...
On the Oregon trail a group of cowboys are on their way to the West. In the group of 20 or so, one of the cowboys has to cook the meals for the group. Once you are chosen to cook you have to cook until someone complains about the food. This one cook eventually gets tired of cooking and starts making the food worse and worse. Day after day the other cowboys pretend to enjoy it. Finally, one cowboy got so fed up and shouted, "IM SICK OF THIS, IT TASTE LIKE SHIT" everyone gasps. He panickly retorted "BUT ITS GOOD THOUGH"
