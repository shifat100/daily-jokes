The magic spring
Once upon a time, There was once a magic spring which could alter your appearance. It is usually done by jumping towards the spring and shouting any character who you want to be. 
A disfigured lady jumped into the spring and shouted "Snow White! Immediately she turned into a beautiful lady who looks like SnowWhite. 
A fat old hag heard about it and decided to try it for herself. As she ran towards the magic spring, she tripped on a rock and as she fell on the spring she accidentally shouted " vagina!".
