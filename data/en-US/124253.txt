The Bikers and the Trucker
A calm trucker is sitting in a diner enjoying a lunch when a group of ten rowdy bikers pull up and walk into the restaurant. One of the bikers eventually leaves his seat and walks over to the trucker and spits on his food. The trucker calmly gets up, pays for his meal, and leaves the restaurant. The one biker says to the other "Huh, some man he was." The waitress then says "Yeah, he's not much of a truck driver either." The biker responds "How do you know that?" The waitress says "Because he just ran over ten motorcycles in the parking lot when he left."
