Good news and a bad news
John the butler went to Sir Richard very worried and told him:

-Sir Richard, someone wrote a dirty word about you in the snow with urine.

Sir Richard ordered John to investigate and soon he returned with the results:

-Sir Richard, I have good news and a bad news for you - which would like to hear first?

-The good news of course- said Sir Richard.

-The good news is that we found that the urine belongs to your neighbor William. The bad news is that it's in your wife's handwriting ...
