The stockbroker's secretary answered his phone one morning. "I'm sorry,"
she said, "Mr. Bradford's on another line."

"This is Mr. Ingram's office," the caller said. "We'd like to know if
he's bullish or bearish right now."

"He's talking to his wife," the secretary replied. "Right now I'd say
he's sheepish."
