Two friends run into each other at the same laundromat...
Two old friends, Paul and Donny, run into each other at the same laundromat. Paul brings a variety of detergent while Donny doesn't. 

Donny asks Paul if he could use one of his detergents and Paul gladly hands him some Tide. Unhappy with the way his clothes came out, Donny asks Paul if he had a different brand of detergent, so he hands him Gain. Still not satisfied, Donny asks for another brand. Paul gives him Downy. 

After going through most of Paul's detergent, Donny asks him for one more. With a deep sigh, Paul hands Donny his last brand of detergent.

He gave him his All.
