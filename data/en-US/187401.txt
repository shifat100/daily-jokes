Consistent thinking
In an elementary school during an art class teacher tries to spark imagination among pupils. He puts a red brick on the table and ask his 2nd grade pupils what do they think looking on the brick.
Ann replied that it would be nice to build a house. Max said that he could use it after school if he would met Tommy, a bully. What about you, the teacher asked the trouble maker Billy. Billy looked at the brick and replied: "I think about girls with boobs". "Why's that" asked the stunned teacher. "I always think about them" was Billy's reply.
