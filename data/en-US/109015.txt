Leader: No, it doesn't say it anywhere that we should kneel.

Young Man: Ah, then it surely says the traditions is we are meant to stand during the prayer. Thank you.

Leader: Well, actually, no it doesn't say that is the tradition, either.

Young Man: But, there must be an answer. All we do, lately, is argue about what to do during the prayer.
