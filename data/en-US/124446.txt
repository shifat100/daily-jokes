A man is cruising trough Hillbilly country
Mr Smith is cruising in his convertible trough HillBilly country. The landscape is barren, large fields and the occasional tree.

Suddently, Mr Smith notices a man on top of a tree: a Hillbilly. He gets closer to ask for directions and he is baffled:

BRAAAMM BEEEMM BEEEEEEEM BEEEEEEEEEMMM BRA BRA BRA BEEEMMMM, shouts the HillBilly while riding a imaginary motorbike.

&nbsp;

Mr Smith is stunned, and just cruises along. A mile away he notices another tree. There is another Hillbilly on the tree, portraying yet another imaginary motorbike

BRAAAAAA BAAAAA BAAAAA BA BA BAA BEEEEEEEMMMM

&nbsp;

"What the hell is going on?" wonders Mr Smith, as he drives off.

Another mile away he notices a Hillbilly sleeping on a tree. He pulls over and asks:

&nbsp;

"Excuse me sir, why are some guys back there pretending to ride motorbikes on top of trees"?

The hillbilly suddenly wakes up very startled

&nbsp;

"Holy shit they are already back there?!?! NO TIME TO LOSE!"
