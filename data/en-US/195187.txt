Seventy-three year old Gus had worked in the garment center all his life, never finding the time to get married. But one day a beautiful seventeen-year-old girl walked into the store and it was love at first sight.

Within a month Gus and Rachel were married and on the way to Florida for their honeymoon.

"So how was it?" asked Gus' friends, on the couple's return.

"Oh, just beautiful," replied a starry-eyed Gus. "The sun, the surf... and we made love almost every night, we--"

"Just a minute," interrupted Gus' Friend. "At your age, forgive me for asking, you made love almost every night?"

"Oh yes," said Gus, "we almost made love Saturday, we almost made love Sunday . . ."
