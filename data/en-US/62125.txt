An Evil Overlord Purchases a Web Server...
For the purposes of building a website where various villains and ne'er do wells could collaborate and show off their evil schemes.

Early on he had some troubles keeping the site up so he bought several backup servers running a clone of the website. 

After that there was no problem. Sure the world governments would occasionally manage to take one or two servers offline for a while, but they could never take down his final forum.
