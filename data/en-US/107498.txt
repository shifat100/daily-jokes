A young boy planted a seed...
One day a young boy planted a seed in his backyard in hopes of growing a big, tall tree- maybe to relax or play under once it was tall enough.  

Each day he went out to water and check on the tree, but to no avail- it didn't seem to be growing.  

He decided that he would ask his school teacher for help the next day at school.  

After asking his teacher for help, he was thrilled to go home and try this new technique recommended by his teacher.  

So he went home and immediately ran to the yard.  As recommended, he took a picture with an old Polaroid camera of where the seed was planted.  

He then took this picture and tried forcing his sister to eat it, to which he explained "Please help me!  Plants can grow better when there are photos in the sis!"
