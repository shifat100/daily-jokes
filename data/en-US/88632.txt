So an Ewok gets a job at a food processing plant...
...One day while going about his job he trips and falls into a vat of honey. The vat is so deep that only the tip of one ear is visible as he's struggling to get out. A few coworkers rush over to help him and the commotion attracts a supervisor, who demands to know what's going on.

"What is that thing sticking out of the honey?" He asks, pointing to the tip of the Ewok's ear. One of the workers turns to answer him.

"Well, sir, it's a bit of a sticky Wicket..."
