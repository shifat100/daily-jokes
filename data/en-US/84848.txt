A man is driving through the country side when he encounters a road block by the police
He stops, a police officer approaches his car, signifies him to roll down the window and asks for his drivers license. The man obeys the order and while doing so asks, whats going on. He is told, that they've closed all the roads, because they are looking for a rapist. The officer waves him through and the man drives off hesitantly.
Just 2 minutes later, he turns around and drives back to the road block. The same officer approaches him and asks him what he is doing here. The man clears his throat and says: "I've thought about what you said, and... I'll be up for the job!"
