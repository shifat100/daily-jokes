Good guy Justin Bieber...
Justin Bieber was standing at the front of a que at a Wal-Mart the one day waiting for the people at the cashier to finish up and pay for their food. Standing at the cashier was a father and his 6 year old son. The cashier had scanned all their items and said, "that will be" when suddenly the boy cut in and said, "eleven dollars and ninety cents". The cashier applauded the boy but the father looked at him incredulously and snapped at him, "don't be so rude boy, know your place!" 

All of a sudden Bieber walks up to the boy, picks him up and says to him, "son, don't listen to your father, one day you will do great things and many people will look up to you". 

That boy grew up and became successful just like Bieber had predicted. His name? Barack Obama.
