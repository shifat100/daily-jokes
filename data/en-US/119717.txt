Two kids in the forest
Two kids are walking in the forest, and happen to be talking about snake bites.  One kid says, "Did you know that if a snake bites you out here, one of the only ways to get rid of the venom is by cutting a hole in the bite and sucking it out".  Later while sleeping, one of the kids gets bitten by a snake and the other kid asks him to show where the bite is.  The kid says, "ITS BACK HERE HURRY".
