Happy...
A long, long time ago, deep in a forest in the woods, the 7 Dwarfs were having a celebration, on the last day of the year.

Suddenly, from out of nowhere, an evil witch cast a spell and poor Happy's ear, was gone.

Fortunately, Doc was able to work some of his own magic and the very next day, it was back!

The 7 Dwarfs celebrated once again and 'till this very day, on 1 January, we celebrate Happy's new ear!
