A man wants to get on his friend's computer...
... when the computer asks for his friend's password, the man turns and asks his friend.

"Figure it out" his friend says.

Upset that his friend didn't give him the password, the man starts typing different words and phrases in the password box. Frustrated, the man turns around and asks for his friend's password again. 

"Figure it out" his friend says.

After trying for another half hour without any success, he goes to his friend's bedroom and threatens to beat him to a bloody pulp if he doesn't give him the password. 

With a calm expression on his face, his friend goes to the computer and starts typing:

"f-i-g-u-r-e-Space-i-t-Space-o-u-t-Enter"

5 seconds later the desktop is displayed.
