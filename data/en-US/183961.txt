Parental advice.
The son of a family was growing up so the mother wanted the father to talk to him about "the flowers and the bees".

Later on, the father and son are out walking when the father suddenly asks: 

\- "Son, do you remember the field over there?". 

\- "Yes." 

\- "Do you remember the girls we met there?" 

\- "Yes." 

\- "Do you remember what we did to them?" 

\- "What of it?"

\- "That's how the bees does it."
