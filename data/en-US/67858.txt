A blind man logs into reddit and find his way to the subreddit "Jokes". After browsing a bit, and sitting there for a while, the blind guy posts, “Hey, you wanna hear a blonde joke?” Reddit immediately becomes absolutely quiet. In a husky, deep, font, the blonde woman in the opposite side of the world replies, "Before you tell that joke, know we have seen it. It was on reddit yesterday and the day before that. The woman on the other other side of the world is a blonde and a pro wrestler. Think about it seriously, mister. You still wanna post that blonde joke?” The blind guy says, “I'll just post it another five times.”
