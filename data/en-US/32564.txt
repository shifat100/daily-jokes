Two mice sneak into a San Francisco Bay Area In-N-Out Burger...
.. Shocked that nobody noticed them, they quietly scurried into the kitchen. It was finally their chance to have an authentic In-N-Out cheeseburger.  However, just before reaching the grill, the two mice fell into a bucket of cream. The first mouse quickly gave up and drowned. The second mouse, wouldn't quit. He struggled so hard that eventually he churned that cream into butter and crawled out. Then he got stepped on by an employee and he died.
