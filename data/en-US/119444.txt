A Southern Baptist, a Catholic, and a Mormon are discussing families...
The Baptist said,  "The LORD did say be fruitful and multiply,  therefore, I have 4 children,  one more and I'd have a basketball team!".  "Well," said the Catholic,  "I'll have you know,  I have ten children.  One more,  and I'd have a football team!".  "That's nothing",  said the Mormon,  "I have 18 wives.  One more,  and I'd have a golf course!"
