A Store Clerk is robbed
A Store Clerk is robbed from a customer at gunpoint from a man with a mask on and the customer gets away. The police call in a bunch of suspects that were close to what the Clerk described.

All the suspects are lined up and the Clerk has to choose which one and the Clerk immediately picks the one on the far right. 
The customer/robber exclaims "How did you know!?"
The Clerk replies "The customer is always right."
