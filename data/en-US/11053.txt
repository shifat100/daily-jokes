New element discovered in USA
Prof. Akunuri V. Ramayya, an alumnus of Andhra University and a nuclear physicist at Vanderbilt University of USA, has discovered a new super-heavy element by combining Hydrogen, Argon, Americium and Beryllium. The breakthrough was made and the element was discovered with the aid of scientists in a Cincinatti research facility. This is an exciting discover and has both answered and raised many questions.
