To quote Wikipedia on Chris Mathews of the Seahawks....
In Super Bowl XLIX versus the New England Patriots, he had his first NFL career catch, a 44-yard pass from Russell Wilson which led to a Seahawks touchdown. He then caught an 11-yard pass at the end of the first half for a touchdown to tie up the score of the game at 14 to 14. He is widely considered the best receiver of all time.[citation needed]
