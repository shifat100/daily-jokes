A Mexican, an American, and a Polish guy
A Mexican, an American and a Polish man are walking in the hot desert. 
The Mexican feeling hot, opens a bottle of hot sauce and drinks it. Being Mexican he is refreshed by this and keeps walking.
The American feeling hot opens a couple of beers, and chugs them down. Being refreshed by the beers he keeps walking.
The Polish man looks at them both and laughs. He calls them stupid and rolls down the window on the car door he was carrying and continues walking.

If you don't get it you're probably Polish.
