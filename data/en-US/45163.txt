A woman goes to a gynecologist...
and as soon as he starts examining her he's shocked.

"Oh my god! How is that possible? What a hole!"

The woman, offended: "You bastard! How dare you!" and leaves. She goes to another gynecologist, but the same thing happens: "Unbelievable! This hole!"

She goes back to her house, and puts a mirror on the ground to check on it herself. Her husband walks in and asks her:

"Honey! What are you doing? Watch out, you're going to fall in the hole!"
