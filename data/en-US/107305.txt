Does virginity matter?
A young man went to a monk and asked him was it important that the woman he marries is a virgin. 

The Monk asked,"Your house, when you go out do you put lock on it"

The man replied,"yes I would"

The monk,"for protection of house would you put lock on it which is new and of which key only with you or put old lock whose  keys already with someone else or many others"

The man,"Ofcourse I would put a lock which is brand new and keys only with me and not with someone else!"

The Monk,"There you have your answer"
