A gay man joins a large LGBT group...
To be able to join, the person must fill out some forms. So the gay man receives his form and sits down next to a cross-dressing man and a cross-dressing woman.

He starts filling the form out. Name. Age. Address. Occupation. Then he sees portion: "Sex: ____".

Unsure of what to write, the man looks over to the cross-dressing woman and sees she's written "M". He then looks over to the cross-dressing man and sees he's written "F".

Now that he's sure, he writes "MWF."
