Sad clowns
At the end of a day at the fair, John makes his way to his car. On the parking lot he sees a crying clown. John asks

"what's the matter Mr. Clown?"

"I hate blowing bubbles!" The clown sobs before running away.

John scratches his head and as he turns around to move on, he bumps into another crying clown.

"what's the matter Mr. Clown?" John asks.

"I hate blowing bubbles!" the clown cries as he runs away.

Slightly confused, John proceeds to walk to his car. When he finally arrives at his car, he sees another clown, lying on his back with a huge smile on his face. John can't help himself and asks

"You're the third clown I see on this parking lot, but you're the only one smiling. What gives?"

The clown looks at John and says

"Hi, I'm Bubbles the clown!"
