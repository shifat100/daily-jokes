A boy is taking a taxi by himself to get home...
And so this little boy is annoying, which irritates the taxi-driver. He is teasing him and asking him stupid questions like: "what do you get by crossing a cat and an elephant.. or what do you get by crossing a giraffe with a dog" etc. The taxi-driver wants him to be quiet and is thinking: "this kid won't finish with his teasing". The driver gets so mad that he asks the boy: "ey.. if your father is gay and your mother is a whore, what do you get by crossing them?" The boy cleverly responds: "a taxi-driver".
