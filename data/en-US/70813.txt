Funniest Pinoy Pick Up Lines
Whatever type of hugot line they may be, it is undeniable that each and every one of us have this certain guilty pleasure in saying them and indulging ourselves in this kind of banter once in a while. The fact is, some of us enjoy them so much that they have infiltrated internet memes last year. So, to properly commemorate this local commodity of pinoy entertainment, [this article has collated 35 hugot lines since its inception](http://musicrepublicph.tumblr.com/post/136865361185/funniest-pinoy-pick-up-lines).
