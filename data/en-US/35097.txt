A man goes into a pharmacy
A man goes into a pharmacy and buys a pack of condoms. As soon as he pays, he immediately starts laughing like a maniac and runs out of the pharmacy. The next day, the same man comes in, buys condoms, and runs out laughing. On the third day, when the man did it again, the pharmacist, a curious type by nature has his assistant follow the man. When the assistant comes back some time later, the pharmacists asks, "so did you follow him?"

"Yes" says the assistant

"And where did he go?" 

"Home. Yours"
