Josh in Europe.
A guy shows his painting of a couple making love to a friend.

A friend than asks:

"What is it called."

Artist replies:

"Josh in Europe."

A friend asks again:

"Who is this woman on the painting."

Artist answers:

"That is Joshes wife."

Friend asks:

"So im guessing the man is Josh."

The artist corrects him:

"That is Tom."

Friend than asks:

"So where is Josh."

"In Europe."

Replies the artist.
