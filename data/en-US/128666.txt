3 explorers are deep in the Amazon when they are captured and bound to stakes by a tribe of headhunters.
The great warrior chief walks up to the first man and asks, "death? or boogalu?"  well, he doesn't want to die so he chooses boogalu and promptly gets raped by 100 natives.  The chief then asks the second prisoner "death? or boogalu?"  Though he can't stand the thought of it he also doesn't want to die so he too chooses boogalu, and 200 natives have their way with him.  Finally the chief goes up to the third man and says "death?  or boogalu?".   Well he thinks about it for a second and says to himself steve chose boogalu and got used by 100 natives, larry chose the same and took 200, there's no telling how many of them will have their way with me, so he defiantly proclaims, "I choose death"...
and the great warrior chief yells,  "DEATH by Boogalu!!!"
