Bill, Bill, and Bill walk into a bar ...
Bill Clinton, Bill Cosby, and Bill Nye walk into a bar.  Since the bar is crowded, Clinton turns on the charm and asks three young, attractive women whether they can join them.  Seeing the three famous personalities, the women agree.
&nbsp;

As the conversation progresses, one of the young women laments the plight of the Syrian refugees.  "I feel your pain ...," says Clinton with a folksy Arkansas drawl.

&nbsp;

While Clinton keeps two of them busy, Cosby slips a drug into their drinks.  The third, shocked, turns to Nye and asks what Clinton and Cosby are doing to her friends.  Nye responds, "You can make a very reasonable argument that climate change is not that indirectly related to date rape ..."
