Father & Son
A father and son are going for a walk. 

The son turns to the father, and asks "did you know cows can give milk?"
The father replies "so can some sheep."

They walk a bit more, and the son asks "did you know trees can grow fruit?"
The father replies "so can some bushes."

They walk a little further down the trail and the son asks "did you know pickles can come from cucumbers?"
The father replies "so can some girls."
