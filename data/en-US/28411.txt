Overtime
One company always made workers do crazy overtime. Every day, employees came to work at 7 a.m. and left at 7 p.m.

One day, the boss notices a worker come in at 9 a.m. and leave at 5 p.m. The second and third day, the same thing repeats.

Finally, the boss is fed up, calls up the employee, and says, "if you want to stay working in this company, you need to put in the same effort as your co-workers."

"But sir", replied the worker, "I'm on vacation!"
