A woman gets dressed up to go out for the evening, hoping to meet a man.
She has been looking forward to this night for a long time and is excited to meet some new people. She is dressed up, confident and looking great. She goes out but all night, no one seems interested in her, no one approaches her and those that she approaches herself quickly leave her company.

Puzzled, she finally gives up and goes home. She continues to be bothered by it as she does some chores before bed to settle her mind. Finally, when taking a load of clothes to the laundry, she sees the problem.

All her clothes had been washed with detergent.
