The Difference between Theory and Reality.

"Hey dad, whats the difference between theory and reality?" to which his father replied;  
"Well, go ask mother if she would bang Tom Cruise for a million dollars"  
Little Jimmy runs off and comes back a short time later  
"Yup, she would"  
"Ok, go ask your sister if she would bang justin bieber for a million dollars"
