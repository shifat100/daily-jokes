A tourist is picked up by a cabbie in New York on a dark
night.  The passenger taps the driver on the shoulder to
ask him something. The driver screams, loses control of
the car, nearly hits a bus, drives up on the sidewalk, and
stops inches from a shop window.

For a second everything went quiet in the cab, then the
driver said, "Look friend, don't EVER do that again. You
scared the daylights out of me!"

The passenger apologizes and says he didn't realize that a
"little tap" could scare him so much.

The driver, after gathering himself together replied,
"Sorry, it's not really your fault. Today is my first day
as a cab driver - I've been driving hearses for the last
25 years!
