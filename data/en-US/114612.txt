Cinderella goes to the ball...
... dances with Prince Charming, runs home at midnight losing one of her glass slippers.  When she gets home she runs to hide in the bathroom, the glass slipper on her other foot nervously tapping against the tile floor.  The Prince, hot on her trail, arrives at her front door and demands of her wicked stepsisters to tell him where the beautiful girl he just met is. The sisters, jealous, play dumb. The Prince pushes his way in and busts through the bathroom door to find a shocked and scared Cinderella. "How did you find me?", she blubbers. The Prince lovingly gazes into her eyes and calmly says: "I heard you tinkling".
