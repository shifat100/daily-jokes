A Chinese man works for the Marine Corps
A Chinese man is hired for a job managing supplies in the Marine Corps. After about two months working on the ship, the man goes missing on the Captain's birthday.

"Go check the supply room." the Captain says to his Marines.
The Captain and his Marines open the supply room, only to find a giant box, sitting in the middle of the floor. With their guns out, they carefully open the box. Suddenly, the Chinese man pops up from out of the box.

"Supplies!!!"
