"don't worry said no arms guy I can try" 
Thy head up to the very top of the church tower to see a bell, the worker says all you have to do is ring this bell twice a day, but with no arms that might be tough. "I'll do it, watch no arms guy says" 
He backs up about 5 feet and sprints towards the bell and smacks it with his head as hard as he can.
