A Jewish man and a Chinese man were conversing. The Jewish man commented 
upon what a wise people the Chinese are.
"Yes," replied the Chinese, "Our culture is over 4,000 years old. But,
 you Jews are a very wise people, too."
The Jewish man replied, "Yes, our culture is over 5,000 years old."
