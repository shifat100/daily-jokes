Carlos was a passionate soccer fan
After the devastating defeat Brazil suffered in the 2014 World Cup, Carlos sat in his room and began screaming:

"NNNOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO"
and continued screaming for many months. 

His neighbors were extremely annoyed because of all the ruckus and were also concerned because they had not met him for an extended period of time.

So one day, Jose, his friend and neighbor, goes up to Carlos's house and knocks on his window.
Carlos, startled, goes to the windows and opens them.
Jose says, to Carlos, 

"Hola Carlos, long time, no Si!"
