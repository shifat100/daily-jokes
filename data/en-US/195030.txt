Three Englishmen stopped at a restaurant for a spot of tea. The
waiter appeared with pad and pencil. "I'll have a glass of weak
tea," ordered the first.

"I'll have tea, too," said the second, "but very strong with two
pieces of lemon."

"Tea for me, too, please," said the third. "but be sure the glass is
absolutely clean."

In a short time the waiter was back with the order. "All right," he
asked, "which one gets the clean glass?"
