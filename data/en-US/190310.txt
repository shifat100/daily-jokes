A man walked to the top of a hill to talk to God.
The man asked, "God, what's a million years to you?" 
 
And God said "A minute." 
 
Then the man asked: 
"Well, what's a million dollars to you?" 
 
And God said: "A penny" 
 
Then the man asked: 
"God.....can I have a penny?" 
 
And God said: 
"Sure.....In a minute."
