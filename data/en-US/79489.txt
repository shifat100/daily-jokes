3 Men are stranded on a deserted island...
...one young, one middle aged, one old. They are all sitting on the beach and see in the distance another island full of beautiful women. 

The youngest exclaims, "Come on guys, we have to go swim over to them right away!"

The middle aged one replies, "Now hold on there son, that's rather a long swim, and I don't think even you would be up to the challenge. Let's take our time, build a boat, and we'll get there."

The old man say, "The view is fine from here."
