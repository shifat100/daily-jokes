Name change
A guy walks into a social security store and says:

"- I want to change my name! It's ugly, everybody mocks it, and nobody takes me seriously..."

A social worker then says:

"- Don't worry sir, we'll take care of that right now! So what's your name?"

"- John Shit."

"- I'm sorry to hear that sir, that's a very ugly name... So what do you want to change it to?"

"- Jack Shit, seems more fitting."
