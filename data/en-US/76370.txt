Knock knock English joke
Series of knock-knock jokes between Guy and me:

Me:  Knock knock
Guy: Who's there?
Me: To
Guy: To who?
Me:  It's 'to whom' you dingus, let's try it again. Knock knock

Guy: Who's there?
Me: From
Guy: From...whom?
Me: Ok. Congratulations on basic grammar. One more time. Knock knock

Guy: Who's there?
Me: At
Guy: At whom
Me: Gesundheit
