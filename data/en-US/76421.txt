Chip Kelly had gone to a fortune teller
Unsure about the way things were going, he asked if he was getting fired.  When she said yes, he begged her to tell him when.  “Philadelphia has a lot of street parties - it will be a big holiday.”  "Well which one?” he demanded, thinking Mummers and fireworks.  “Don’t worry Chip.  ANY day you get fired will be a big holiday."
