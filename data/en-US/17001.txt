The most annoying people in school were the debate club called "The Reasons"
They felt they knew the answers to everything.

"Those guys suck." One student said. "I hate them so much!" Said another. "Someone should really take them down a peg."

Taking this to heart, the quarterback of the football team got in his truck and drove around waiting for The Reasons to get out of class. When he noticed them, he stopped for literally no Reason.
