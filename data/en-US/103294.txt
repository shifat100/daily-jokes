A Jets fan, a Bears fan, and a Jaguars fan walk into a sports bar...
They decide to play a drinking game...

Jets fan: Whenever Geno Smith throws an interception, I'll take a shot
Bears fan: Whenever Cutler gets sacked or throws a pick, take a shot
Jags fan: Whenever the Jaguars get a touchdown, I'll take a shot

3 hours later, The Bears and Jets fans are hammered while the Jags fan drives them both home.
