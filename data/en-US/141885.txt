A group of friends are always telling the same jokes...
They got so used to it that they numbered them, and instead of telling the joke they would just say "1", or "13", and everyone would laugh. 

One day their all gathered around, telling their favorite jokes ("22", "5", etc). One guys says "42" and the group chuckles, except for one guy who starts laughing uproariously. A few minutes later he is still bursting into fits of giggles, so one of the friends asks, "Damn man, whats with you?"

"Nothing, I just hadn't heard that one before!"
