2 Ants were swimming in a lake...
When an elephant comes and jumps into the lake,  making a big splash. 

Both the ants get thrown away on the waves. 

One of the ants lands on the banks.  And he starts looking out for his friend. 
He finds that his friend has landed on top of the elephant's back. 

So he starts shouting excitedly to his friend, " Yeah!  Drown that muthafucka..."
