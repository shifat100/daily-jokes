Business ethics
One day, Nathan Rosenberg’s son, Moishe, came to him and asks:

— Pappa, what is “business ethics”???

— Oh, Moishe, that’s something very serious. How could I explain it to you? Ah, yes. Okay. You see, business ethics comes to play when, say, one day, you are alone in the shop and a client leaves and forgets a $10 bill on the counter. Business ethics means that you are asking yourself whether you should tell your partner or not.
