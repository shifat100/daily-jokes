Bar games.

They came up with a form of beer football, each taking turns chugging a beer for 6 points, burping after for 1 point, and farting for extra 2 points. The first guy chugs his beer, burps but couldn't fart. The second guy chugs his beer, burps and let's a loud fart rip. The third guy chugs his beer, belches the loudest thinking he would get additional points, finally he get up and hunches preparing to let out a massive fart when the second guy pulls down the third guys pants, and sticks his finger up the third guys ass yelling "I blocked the extra point!"
