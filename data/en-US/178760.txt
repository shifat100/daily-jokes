Caucasian fries
So to day I took my grand kids to Popeye's restaurant for lunch. I told my grandson (who is mulatto) to place his order and get what he wants. 
He steps up to the counter and says "I want chicken strips." 
The lady taking his order says. "what do you want to drink?"
"Coke" he says. 
What side do you want? she asks.
"I want Caucasian fries." says my grandson. 
As I start to step forward (mortified) the lady (an African/American) says "Sweetie your going to have to go to McDonald's for those. We don’t have Caucasian fries here."
