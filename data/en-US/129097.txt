A game of Chess
John spots 2 of his blonde girl-firends playing Chess, and asks if he can join them. They replied that they are just beginners and he would easily beat them. Determined to play, John suggests that they can team up against him. Still doubtful about the equality of competition, they agree that John would be playing with left hand.


In the end John wins and walks away, amazed by his skills in chess, both of them wonder what went wrong...
