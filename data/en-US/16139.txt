A man was presented this riddle from his friend...
"Two girls ate dinner together. They both ordered iced tea. One girl drank them very fast and had finished five in the time it took the other to drink just one. The girl who drank one died while the other survived. All of the drinks were poisoned. How did the girl who drank the most survive?"

His friend then gave him 4 options:
A: The girl who survived was immune
B: The poison was in the ice
C: The poison was in only 1 drink
D: The poison could only work when evaporated into vapour

The man then told his friend: "I think it's B. Being immune to the poison didn't make sense and the poison was in all the drinks, so the answer isn't A or C. But I'm not sure whether the poison was in the ice or could only work as vapour, so I'm going to guess it is B since it seems more likely to be correct."

And he was correct.
