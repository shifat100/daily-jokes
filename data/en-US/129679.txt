Joe Dies Hunting
Joe and Dave are hunting when Dave keels over. Frantic, Joe dials 911 on his cell phone and blurts, "My friend just dropped dead! What should I do?" A soothing voice at the other end says, "Don't worry, I can help. First, let's make sure he's really dead." After a brief silence, the operator hears a gun shot. Then Joe comes back to the phone. "Okay," he says nervously to the operator. "What do I do next?"
