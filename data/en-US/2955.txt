Donald Trump goes parachuting...
Donald Trump decides that being president is tiring and decides to take a vacation.

He decides to go parachuting at a resort in Florida. After the pilot straps Donald Trump's parachute onto him, the pilot says "3,2,1... and go!" and Donald Trump jumps of the plane. 

As he enjoys the view below him, he says, "Finally, a joke on /r/jokes where I don't die!"
