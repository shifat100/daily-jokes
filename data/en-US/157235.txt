Jango doesn't have to pay
The bus arrives at the morning bus stop as always. People board the bus, followed by Jango who holds a gun to the bus driver and exclaims: "JANGO NO PAY!"
Not wanted to argue, the bus driver lets him in and drives off. 
The next day Jango is waiting at the morning bus station along with the other bus goers. They board the bus, followed by Jango who looks at the bus driver and as always shouts: "JANGO NO PAY!"
Frustrated but unable to refuse a man with a gun the bus driver lets him in and drives off. 
Another day goes by and as usual the crowd starts to board the bus. Fed up with the free entries he's giving Jango, the bus driver asks: "Why the hell doesn't Jango have to pay?!"
