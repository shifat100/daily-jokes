Obama, his wife and Oprah
Obama, his wife, and Oprah were all flying to DC on a private jet.  Obama goes "you know, I could throw a one-thousand dollar bill out the window and make someone very happy!"  Then his wife said "well speaking of which, I could throw 10 hundred dollar bills out thewindow and make 10 people very happy."  So Oprah goes "well, I could throw 100 10 dollar bills out and make 100 people very happy."  The pilot rolled his eyes and looked at the co-pilot and said "They think they're so smart.  You know I could throw all their asses out and make 52 million people very happy!"
