There was an Information Technologist...
and he stumbled upon a man in the nearby park. The man was arranging people by there jobs and putting them in rooms labeled with a letter. (Doctors go to room A, businessmen go to room B, etc.)

After a few minutes, the Information Technologist came back with a chair and a table.
The man said "Why did you bring a chair and a table?"
