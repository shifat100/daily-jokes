All Flavor Peaches
A guy goes to a farmer's market, and he sees a stand with a sign: "All Flavored Peaches"

He gets curious and walks up to the farmer behind the stand.

Guy: Are these peaches really flavored?

Farmer: Sure are!  Any flavor you want, I got it.

Guy: Alright, how about strawberry?

Farmer: Here you go. [hands him a peach]

Guy: [takes a bite] Holy cow, that's delicious!  Alright, how about peanut butter and jelly?

Farmer: Got it right here. [hands him another]

Guy: [takes a bite] Hmm, I taste the peanut butter, but where's the jelly?

Farmer: Turn it over!

Guy: [bites other side] Wow!  That tastes like grape jelly!  That's incredible!

So the guy gets a little bolder and he asks him,

Guy: Alright, how about pussy?

Farmer: Let's see... Here we go.

Guy: [takes a bite] Ugh, that tastes like shit!

Farmer: Turn it over!
