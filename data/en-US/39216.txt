A journalist is interviewing a gun nut.
Journalist: "Would you support banning guns in light of Islamic terrorist attacks using them?"

Gun nut: "Nope.  Just ban Muslims."

Journalist: "Would you ban guns after mass-shootings of gay people?"

Gun nut: "Nope.  Ban fags."

Journalist: "You'd rather ban groups of *people* than objects?"

Gun nut: "Yup."

Journalist: "Then who would you ban the next time there's a shooting at a gun range?"

Gun nut: "Journalists."
