In an Austrian cemetery one night
I walked through the dark, and beheld a strange light from an open grave. Although I trembled with terror, I walked to the grave and peered down.

I saw a spectral glowing ghost with a powdered white wig. He held a musical score, and in his other hand,  a large eraser.

"Who are you?" I cried out.

"Beethoven!" said the apparition.

"What are you doing?"

He paused, and erased some more notes from the music. 

In a loud voice the ghost said: "DECOMPOSING!"
