A guy goes to a Halloween party...
And everyone is dressed up in the usual Halloween getups - some are scary, some are funny, some of the ladies are in sexy costumes, etc.  But this guy is dressed as Santa Claus.

Everyone is impressed with his outside-the-box costume decision, since they've never seen anyone dress up as Santa Claus on Halloween.  "Hey, that's very original - dressing up as Santa Claus on Halloween."

But the man gets more and more irritated as he gets these compliments, and finally he snaps - "You people don't get it at all.  I'm dressed as an *Alzheimer patient*!"
