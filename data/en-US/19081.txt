A farmer has university students at his farm.
Students from Howard University were sent on a field trip, and it was the farmer's unlucky day. The farmer is racist and hates just about everything black, and he openly expresses it. As he walks by his blackberries, he announces "These African American are ready to be picked. Anyone want to harvest?" None of the students respond well to the farmer's racism. One student steps forward to correct the farmer. "Excuse me, sir, but these are actually blackberries. You've got it wrong." The farmer corrects his original judgment and the student's and insists they are actually slave berries. "Their taste is not limited to their color." The same student steps forward again. "Want to know what is limited to YOUR color? Your stupidity. Because only white people can possibly be this stupid."
