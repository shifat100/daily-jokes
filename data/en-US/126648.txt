Mrs Sally's Kindergarten class.
Mrs Sally's kindergarten class was coming in from recess. As the children walked in Mrs Sally asked them what they did during recess. The first child was a little white boy named Jimmy. 

"Now Jimmy, what did you do today during recess?" Said Mrs Sally.

Jimmy said "I played in the sand box with Karli and Gabe!" 

"That's great, Jimmy! If can spell 'sand', I'll give you a cookie." Said Mrs Sally

"S-a-n-d, sand!" Gloriously belted Jimmy. 

"That's right, Jimmy!" Said Mrs Sally, giving Jimmy a cookie. 

Next in line was Karli, a little white girl. 

"And what did you do today during recess?" 

"I played in the sand box with Jimmy and Gabe!" Karli said proudly. 

"That's great, Karli! If you can spell 'box', I'll give you a cookie!"

"B-o-x, box!" 

"That's great!" Said Mrs Sally, giving Karli a cookie. 

Next in line was Gabe, a little black boy. 

"And what did you do for recess today Gabe?" Asked Mrs Sally. 

"Well, I tried to play with with Jimmy and Karli, but they wouldn't let me because. I'm black." Said Gabe defeated. 

"Jimmy!" Said Mrs Sally, "that's blatant racial discrimination! If you can spell 'blatant racial discrimination' I'll give you a cookie!"
