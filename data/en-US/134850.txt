Jesus in Galilee
Jesus was preaching in Galilee & he came upon the woman at the well, who had been caught in the act of adultery.  The Pharisees asked him whether the woman should not be stoned.  Jesus replied,  "Let him among you who is without sin cast the first stone."  A stone came whistling through the crowd and struck Jesus in the head.  He looked out over the crowd and then He said,  "You know,  Mom,  sometimes you really piss me off!"
