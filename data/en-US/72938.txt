Labels In Society
I really hate labels in society. Every action you do and every interest you have has a "category" and you get lumped together with a group, and it's stupid. If I like video games, I'm a "nerd". If I like Starbucks, I'm "basic". If I sneak into your house at 3 am and watch you sleep even though I'm pretty sure you dropped multiple hints, I'm labeled a "creep". It's ridiculous!
