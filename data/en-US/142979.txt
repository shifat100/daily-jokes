Final Exam Failure
Last semester I took Macroeconomics and didn't have a clue what I was doing. There were 80 multiple choice questions. For some reason I decided to play the game of probability and choose the letter "A" for everything. In that game, the only probable was that I failed

The following day, the professor asked to see me in class.

"Is everything okay?"

"Sure," I said. "Why?"

"Well, here's your test," and handed me a piece of paper that was covered in red ink.

"Can you explain why you chose 'A' for everything?"

Knowing that there was nothing I could do at this point, I said,

"Well, I've always wanted to be an 'A' student."
