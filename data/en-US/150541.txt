A Muslim man went to China...
And goes to a nearby Chinese restaurant. He orders his food and, being the faithful Muslim man he is, he wants to make sure that his food isn't pork.

He asks the waiter "Is this pork?" but the waiter doesn't know any English.

So instead he points at his food and says "Oink?"

The waiter quickly shakes his head and says "No, no, meow"
