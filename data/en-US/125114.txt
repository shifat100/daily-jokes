Inspector Morse
Back before the days of the Internet, there was a show called Inspector Morse that was big in the UK. As a fan of the show, I decided to set up a competition at a local school where the pupil had to draw Morse and list the actor who played him. The child also had to put his name and age on the drawing as well, so I would know who to award my prize to.

Hundreds and hundreds of drawins came on but none could correctly name the actor who played him, then finally I found one person, the only person in the whole school who drew Morse and labelled correctly who played him. 

His name was John, 4.
