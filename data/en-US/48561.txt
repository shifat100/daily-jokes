John walks into a bar...
...where he meets his old friend. They both take a seat, order a beer and talk about different stuff. Suddenly, one of the guests shouts: "21!". The whole bar laughs moderately. Couple of minutes later someone else shouts: "34!". Everyone starts laughing even harder than before. John, surprised by what's happening, asks his buddy:  
-Why is everyone laughing, when someone shouts a number?  
The friend answers:  
-You see, we've been telling the same jokes for the past couple of years, so we just decided to associate every joke with a different number, and now when someone wants to tell a joke, he just shouts a number.  
John nodded and asked:  
-Can I try?  
His friend nodded as well, so John shouted: "1416!". Everyone in the bar laughed for the next 15 minutes after that.  
-Why did everyone laugh so hard at this one? - asked John.  
And so his friend responds while giggling:  
-Well, we didn't hear that one before.
