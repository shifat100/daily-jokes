Prisoners Telling Jokes
Three men, A, B and C have all been serving their long sentences together. 

To pass their time they often tell each other jokes across the cell halls. After so many years they told every joke they knew and rather than keep repeating the whole joke when retelling one they assinged each joke a number and just said the number when it was appropriate.

Guy A yelled 'across hey remember how Linda was totally "17"?' ; they all laughed.

Guy C said "yeah and Todd was a bit '39' if you ask me"; laughter ensued.

Then Guy B said "dont forget they were both '23' if you ask me" 

A and C stood silent and then both sighed out...

 "That guy has never been able to tell a joke"
