Three Generals and an Admiral...
Three generals and an admiral die and go to heaven.  Bickering the entire way to the pearly gates as to which branch is best.  Finally,  after hear all this commotion, asks what the deal is.  They inform him of their quarrel asking for a solution. Saint Peter responds, "Send a memo to God asking him, he will give you an answer."  So they do and send it up.  A few minutes later a response comes back.

I can not be the one to solve this for you.  Each branch has its own strengths and weaknesses that the others fill the gaps.  Senergy has worked for all of the branches over the years and will continue to do so in the future even though the four of you are no longer with your respective branches.  Let the subject rest.  

Signed,
GOD.  
US ARMY Retired.
