A zebra visits a farm
A zebra is visiting the US and enters a farm. He sees a chicken and asks: “what do you do?” The chicken answers “I lay eggs”. The zebra moves along and encounters a cow. He asks the cow: “what do you do here?” “I make milk” responds the cow. The zebra continues his visit when he comes across a black stallion and asks, “and you, what do you do here?” The stallion looks at the Zebra and says “take off your pajamas and I will show you…”
