A convent of Nuns has a unique tradition...
Every fall, instead of gathering up the leaves, they lay them out into patterns.  They do this as a meditative thing and it's also a fun, creative outlet in an otherwise dull, monotonous life.

One summer, a new Nun arrives by the name of Sister Elia.  By the time september rolls around, nobody had told her about the tradition they have with the leaf patterns.  When she went outside, she assumed the leaf patterns were just kids fooling around, so she decided on cleaning it up that evening.

For weeks, she would walk over to the old shed, stepping on many plants on the way, and grab a rake to get rid of the leaves.  Eventually, the head mother sees her doing this and sits her down to scold her.  

   "Sis Elia, you're raking my art.  You're shaking my convent's plants daily!"
