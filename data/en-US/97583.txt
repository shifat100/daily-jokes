A man goes jogging down the street...
...every morning and passes the same house each day. Each time he passes, he hears chanting from beyond the tall fence of the home. Eventually, he decides to investigate. He again makes his way to the house and hears people on the other side of the fence chanting "10, 10, 10, 10..." After a bit of searching, he finds a hole in the fence and decides to take a look inside. As soon as he puts his eye up to the hole, a stick jabs him right in the eye. The man jumps back in pain and can again hear the chanting beyond the fence. "11, 11, 11, 11..."
