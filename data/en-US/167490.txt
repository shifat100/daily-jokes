A man walks into a fancy restaurant.
The maitre d' informs the man of the strict dress code and will not allow him in until he has a tie on. 
The man returns to his car frantically looking for anything he can use in place of a tie but only found a set of jumper cables.
 The man returns and asks the maitre d' if he can come inside now.
 The maitre d' says, "yes. But don't start anything!"
