Prom date
Billy and Sarah decide to go to the prom together. The theme that year was "Cirque de Soleil". Many girls took the opportunity to show off skin tight outfits. There were the few that decided to go a different direction. While Billy and Sarah were chatting with a group at their table, some random person dressed a clown kept hitting on Sarah. This irritated Billy since Sarah was obviously his date. Deciding to keep his cool, he tells Sarah that he'll be right back with some drinks. As soon as he gets out of his seat, the clown starts following him. Billy tries to avoid him by using the bathroom first, but the clown follows him and takes the stall next to him. Billy is getting seriously bothered at this point, but says nothing. He eventually makes his way to the refreshment table, but there are quite a few ahead of him. Sure enough, the clown immediately appears behind him in line. Unable to contain his anger, Billy turns around and shouts, "What the hell do you want?!" The clown looks sincerely surprised. "What? I'm just here for the punch line."
