Shaunaka said, "O son of Suta, I desire to know the reason why the illustrious *Rishi* whom you have named Jaratkaru came to be so called on Earth."  You must tell us the etymology of the name *Jaratkaru*."  
Sauti said, "*Jara* is said to mean *waste*, and *Karu* implies *huge*."  
-From the *Astika Parva* in the *Mahabharata*
