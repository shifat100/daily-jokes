Just another horse joke
A donkey walks up to a horse exclaiming "good news, the doctor called and it’s going to be a boy!"  The horse mumbles under its breath “I really wish you hadn’t made an ass of yourself.”  The donkey retorts by stating “well.. due to speciation it’ll be a mule and, as such, it will be infertile, dying childless and alone.  You knew this going in, Martha!"
