A Family of Polar Bears
A family of polar bears is hanging out on a piece of ice. Baby polar bear goes: "hey mommy, are you a polar bear?"

"Yes I am, sweetheart".

Then he turns to his dad: "hey dad are you a polar bear?" 

"You better believe it boy. I am a damn fine big polar bear".

He waits another second and goes "dad, mom, am I a polar bear too?"

"Of course you are son" they both go.
