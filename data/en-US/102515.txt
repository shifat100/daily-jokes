Why women result in problems

So, we know that women require time and money. So we can multiply these two: time x money = women

We also know that time is money, so: money x money = money² = women

However, money is at the root of all problems, so we have to replace "money" by "problems": problems² = women

Finally, we simplify this little equation. Since money is *at the root* of all problems, we take the square root of "problems²". Which gives is us our final equation: WOMEN = PROBLEMS
