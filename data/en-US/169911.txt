Pirate captian and his clothes

Captian replies "bring me my red shirt". The battle lasts for a good 30 minutes to an hour. After the battle, the pirates stand tall. First mate walks up to the captian and asks "cap'in, why did you want your red shirt" he says "for if i were hurt, my men would not notice me bleeding, and fight on to the end" 1st mate says "that is very clever sir".
