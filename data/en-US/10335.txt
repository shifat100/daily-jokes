From the set of Pulp Fiction
On the set of Pulp Fiction, Quentin Tarantino had custom clothes made for every character. When John Travolta went into wardrobe to grab some footwear, the woman in charge stopped him. 

"What, I can't wear these?" he asked her, a little taken aback. 

"I'm sorry, Mr. Travolta, but you can't. These boots are made for Walken."
