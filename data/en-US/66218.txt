A Swede and a Finn walk into a bakery
The Finn immediately steals three pastries and puts them in his pocket.
He says to the Swede, "See how good I am? The owner didn't see a thing." The Swede says to the Finn, "That's typical of you Finns. I am going to show you an honest way to get the same result."
He goes to the owner of the bakery and says, "Give me a pastry and I will show you a magic trick." Intrigued, the owner accepts and gives him a pastry. The Swede swallows it and asks for another one. The owner gives him another one. Then the Swede swallows that one and asks for a third pastry and eats that, too.
The owner is starting to wonder where the magic trick is and asks, "So what did you do with the pastries?"
The Swede replies, "Look in the Finn's back pocket....."
