A Furniture Salesman
A furniture salesman is surprised to see a man named Boris who speaks little English in his shop.  

"I come from Poland to America.  Here is 20 bucks, give me sweetheart." Boris says.

"Wait a second", says the furniture salesman, "Let me see that flier you brought with you".

Sweetheart Deal
20 Dollar Discount on Home Furnishings
1134 New York Street, NY, NY

"I see the problem here", says the furniture salesman.
