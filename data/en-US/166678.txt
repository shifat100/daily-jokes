Sister Mary Katherine has been promoted in the monastery, and needs to move out of her room ...
... to make room for the new nun, Sister Mary Kate.

Sister Mary Katherine thought she had done a very thorough job of moving her things and sprucing up the room when she showed the space to Sister Mary Kate.

However, as Sister Mary Kate slammed the door behind her as she entered the room, one of Sister Mary Katherine's wimples fell and scared the heck out of her.

Sister Mary Katherine explained in surprise: "Oops!  Sorry, old habit."
