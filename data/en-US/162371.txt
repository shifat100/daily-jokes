What do you call ... with no arms or legs
These are some of my mothers favorite jokes
What do you call two men with no arms or legs hanging in front of a window? Curt an' Rod!

What do you call a man and a woman on a grill with no arms or legs? Frank and Patty!

What do you call a man with no arms or legs being pulled by a boat? Skip!

What do you call a man with no arms or legs in the water? Bob
