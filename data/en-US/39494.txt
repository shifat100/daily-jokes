A little black boy was sitting on a hill...
When God opens up the heavens and sends the little boy a pair of wings.

The boy takes a look at his wings flies around a bit. He then lands, and after a moment he becomes sad. 

God looks down and asks "What is it my child?"

The boy replies with the question, "God, am I an angel?"

God then chuckles and responds, "No little niglet, you are a bat."
