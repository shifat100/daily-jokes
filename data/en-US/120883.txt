Mother adopts Mexican twins
A mother and her husband adopted two twins from Mexico. Their names were Juan and Jamal. They lived a happy good life until the boys grew up to be 18 and decided to move out. After they moved away the husband heard the wife crying and he asked what was wrong. She said "My two babies just left for the first time and one said he would never visit again" the husband is completely shocked so asks what one will visit the wife says "Juan will visit" "oh that's a relief" the husband says "because once you've seen Juan, you've seen Jamal"
