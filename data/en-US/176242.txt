Relaxing
Raju Singh decided to take a trip to the park and lay down in the grass. A man walks up to him and asks "that looks comfortable, are you relaxing?" Raju replies "No Sir, I am Raju Singh". Later, another man comes up to Raju and also asks him "the grass looks great, are you relaxing?" Annoyed, Raju says "No sir… I am Raju Singh". Finally a third man comes up to Raju and asks him the same question. Very annoyed, Raju gets up and walks to the other side of the park where he sees another man laying on the grass. He shouts "ARE YOU RELAXING?" the man replies "why, yes I am" Raju replies "good, they are looking for you on the other side of the park"
