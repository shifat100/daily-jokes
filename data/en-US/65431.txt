An owl, A Native American, and rapper Lil Jon walk into a bar...
The bartender asks the gathered public if they've seen the Minnesota Vikings win the superbowl recently, but he has a combination of a lisp, a thick accent, a stutter, and his voice is rather soft by default, so it is difficult to hear him speak.

The owl goes: "WHO??"

Lil Jon says: "WHAT??"

A Minnesota Vikings fan says: "When?"

An out-of-state foreigner says: "Where?"

The Native American greets everyone with: "How."

Bartender shakes his head and facepalms. "Why?"
