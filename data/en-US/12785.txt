Scented Candles
A man buys a machine that produces scented candles, but no matter what, none of the candles the machine makes seem to smell. 

The man calls the manufacturers to complain and is put through to a customer service representative. However, the representative seems to have a hard time understanding the man's problem.

The representative finally says, "Sir, you keep saying you're having trouble making sense of this, but if you can't be more specific about the problem with the machine, I'm afraid I can't help you."

The man angrily replies, "I just told you what the problem is! It's not making any **scents**!"
