Sticking together
A dying man was unhappy because of his quarreling sons, and he wanted to teach them one final lesson before he dies.

He gathered them by his bedside and gave the elder a stick, and told him to break it, which he did. The old man gave him two sticks and told him to break them, and again, the son did it.

The man grabbed a bundle of sticks and said: "Will you break this bundle of sticks for me, son?". The son paused for a minute, looked at the bundle and tried to break it.........and did it.

His sons said: "What are you going to tell us by this, dad?"

The old man, angry from inside, stopped for a moment and said: "Sons, I shall have no fear for you after my departure, as long as this mule is with you".
