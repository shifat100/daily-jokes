Mom shot at Walmart in Idaho by two year old son
It's all over the news.  It seems the mother had a handgun concealed in her purse, and her two year old son drew the weapon and fired it at the mother.  The mother was black and her son was white.  Witnesses have said that the mother had her hands up in a non-threatening pose and told her son she could not breath.

Non-violent protests are being held outside the Walmart asking for the indictment of the white son that shot his black mother.  Already there has been looting, and protesters are claiming police brutality in cases where people have not been allowed to take big screen televisions from the store without paying for them.
