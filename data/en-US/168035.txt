Gathering evidence
An investigator investigating a case is having trouble linking the suspect to the crime. He decides to take a break and heads to his favorite doughnut shop. The waitress brings out his usual, coffee and a cream filled long john. He tells the waitress just coffee today hun I'm busy with a case. She takes the long john back and brings another out and gives it to the investigator. Custard filled she says handing over the doughnut. The proof is in the pudding.
