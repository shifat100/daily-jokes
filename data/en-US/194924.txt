A man sent his clothing out to the local laundry. When it came back
there were still stains on his underwear. The next week he enclosed a
note saying, "Use more soap on underwear."

This went on for several weeks, with the underwear returning stained,
and the man sending the note, "Use more soap on underwear."

Finally the laundry came back with a note from the laundry man:

"Use more paper on rear."
