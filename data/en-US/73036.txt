With national party nominations coming
And the amount of political posts I see on Facebook, I just wanted to add my two cents. 

It seems a lot of people favor the "anti-establishment" lately. Whether it be Bernie Sanders, an independent running on the Democratic ticket, or Donald Trump, a converted liberal running on the Republican ticket, nobody wants to support the either party's ideal running candidate. It seems as though people are sick of both Democrats and Republicans, and thus it weighs heavily on me to say this.

I firmly support the two party system. I personally have been supporting this my entire life. It is the only way to allow enough time for everyone to enjoy. A party on both Friday and Saturday is truly the solution this country needs.
