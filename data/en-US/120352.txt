A Coded Message
One day, a French spy received a coded message from an American spy, claiming it came directly from President Bush. It read: S370HSSV-0773H. The spy was stumped, so he sent it to his boss at the agency. His boss was stumped, too, so he sent it to the Russians for decoding.
The Russians couldn't solve it either, so they asked the Germans.
The Germans having received this same message during WWII from the Americans, suggested turning it upside down.
