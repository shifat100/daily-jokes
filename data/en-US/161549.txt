So I'm walking down the street when I see this guy with a cow...
As I'm watching, he takes the cow's crap, and flings it against the wall. Then he takes one of those long paint rollers, and starts smearing the stuff all over the place. I walk up to him stunned out of my mind.

"*What the hell are you doing?*"

He looks at me and goes, "What? I'm a bullshit artist."
