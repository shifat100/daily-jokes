Two friends join the army. (translated)
So Mujo and Haso joined the army and they lived in separate floors in the barracks, now, every night the older soldiers came and beat up Mujo who was in the first floor, this continued for a while until Mujo got a clever idea.
He went to Haso and asked him if he wanted to switch floors, Haso agreed and Mujo felt relieved since he was now in the second floor, come next evening the soldiers gathered infront of the barracks waiting to go and beat Mujo up yet again until one of them suggests that they should beat the recruit in the second floor since the first one probably had enough.
