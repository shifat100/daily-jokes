An American, a Brit and a Mexican are on a plane
The Brit throws out a bag of tea, explaining to the confused others: "We have so much tea in England we can just throw them out!". The Mexican proceeds by throwing a bag of peppers out, explaining "We have so many peppers in Mexico, we can just throw them out!". The American proceeds to throw the Mexican out of the plane.  
"Why did you do that?!" exclaimed the Brit.   
The American turned around. "He killed my wife."
