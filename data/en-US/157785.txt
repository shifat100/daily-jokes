The anti-drug campaign
Two men join a local anti-drug campaign. They walk up to the person running the campaign. 

The man looks towards them and says,
"Ah, I'm guessing you two are here for the campaign."

The two men agree and the manager of the campaign said, 

"Alright guys, we were just running a little contest, here's the deal. You have to take [this](http://i.imgur.com/0eKMWGO.jpg) picture and try to persuade people to sign the petition with it. Whoever gets the most signatures, wins a hundred bucks!"

Motivated, the men go outside to start persuading people to sign the petition.

The next day, the two men come back to the manager of the campaign. The first man very proudly says, "Sir, I have successfully got 69 people to sign the petition! (pun intended) 

The manager replies, "Bravo kid, I think you have a great shot at winning the cash! How did you do it?" The man shows him [this](http://i.imgur.com/M5tvTCE.jpg) picture, to which manager replies, "Nice thinking!" 

Then the manager turns to the second man and says, "So, how many petitions did you get son?" 

The second man calmly replies, "567 people."

Astounded, the manager asks for clarification, to which the man replied, "567 people, sir."

The manager asks how the man did this. So the man replied, "I first told them if they used drugs, they would get arrested, then i showed them [this](http://i.imgur.com/VbQs2Xc.jpg) picture."
