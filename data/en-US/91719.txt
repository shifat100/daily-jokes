A man walks into a bar and orders a shot of tequila
Then he looks into his shirt pocket and orders another shot of tequila. After he finishes, he looks into his shirt pocket again and orders another shot of tequila. 

The bartender is curious and askes him: "Every time you order a shot, you look in your shirt pocket. Why?" 

The man replies: "I have a picture of my wife in my pocket and when she starts to look good, I go home."
