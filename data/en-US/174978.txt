The USS Enterprise

" Please make way, over " he said and waited for an answer.
" No, you need to make way, over " the other side of the radio said.
" Can't you hear what I'm saying, you have to make way, over " the captain answered, irritable.
" We can't, you have to make way, over " 
" I am the captain of the USS Enterprise, one of the biggest ships in the world. You HAVE TO make way, over "
