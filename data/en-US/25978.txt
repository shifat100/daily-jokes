The Native American And The Business Woman
A young attractive business-woman walks three blocks from her parking space to her work building. One day, she sees a native american in the alleyway next to her building. He looks solemn, decked out with a headdress. He looks at her and says, "When". The woman is confused, but continues on to work. The next day, she sees the native american again, and he looks at her and says, "When". She's bewildered, but continues on to work. The very next day before work, she sees the native american, he looks at and says, "When". The woman can no longer handle her confusion, and confronts the native american. "Excuse me, but, aren't you people supposed to say 'How'?". The native looks at her and says, "Me know 'how', me wanna know 'when'?"
