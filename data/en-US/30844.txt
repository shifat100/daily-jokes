A student has a long and stressful summer
When he gets back to school, his teachers first assignment is for the pupils to write about their summers. The student, not having had a good summer, asked the teacher if he could write about another topic. The Teacher refused and told him if he did not write about his summer he would recieve a 0. 

When the day to turn in the assignment comes, the student does not turn anything in. When the Teacher gives him a 0, the he decides to go to the principle to explain the situation.

The next day in class the teacher is called to the office and a substitute replaces them. The student happy that justice is being done lets out a sigh of relief. The substitute asks him what that was for and he says "It's a long story I would rather not talk about".
