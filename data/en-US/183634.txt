My German professor today in class.
So today in my Total War class our professor, a blond blue eyed German man started discussing the Holocaust and opened the class up to discussion. After a while one of the students said something similar to what another group was talking about and so the professor looked at him and said, "Well I think I'd put you in the same camp as them." The look of shock on his face and the classes was priceless. It was a good day in class!
