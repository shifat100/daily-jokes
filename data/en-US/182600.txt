A hilarious Polish joke my grandmother told me.
My grandmother told me this joke many times. It actually happened to her. Enjoy!

In the mid-1970's, my grandma loved to travel. She would go all over the world. She landed in China after a long flight, and exited the plane to go claim her baggage. As she was minding her business waiting in line, all of a sudden she gets knocked over by someone. My grandma fell to the floor immediately. After she fell, she looked up at the guy responsible for pushing her over and angrily said "Excuse me?! Where did you come from?!". The man looks at her and says "Poland".
