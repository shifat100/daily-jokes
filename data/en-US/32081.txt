A young teenage boy realizes that he has an extra testicle...
After learning at school that having 3 testicles is actually abnormal he grows very self-conscious about it and wonders how he will tell his father. He finally musters the courage to bring it up and says, "Dad, there's something I have to tell you: between you and me we have 5 testicles." The dad responds, "what? you only have one!?"
