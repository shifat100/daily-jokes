There is a wide fast flowing river with a boy and a girl on two different sides
The girl needs to cross the river to get to the hospital on the other side so she can visit her mom.

The boy needs to cross the river to get the prison on the other side so he can visit his dad.

They both speak of their dilemma to each other while trying to come up with a plan so they can both cross the river.

After a little thinking, the boy decides to pick up a rock and throw it at the girls head.
