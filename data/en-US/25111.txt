Once in an African Village
Once, in an African village, a native man walked up to a farmer with a look of fury on his face. "My wife gave birth today," the native growled, "and the baby is white! And you're the only white person within 100 miles of here! "
The farmer glanced around guiltily for a moment but quickly regained his composure. "Look at those goats over there," the missionary said, pointing at the village's livestock which were horses. "All of them are white, except for that black one over there. Sometimes nature works in mysterious ways. "
The native's eyes widened, and he nodded at the missionary. "I understand, sir. I'll stop talking about the white baby..." and here his voice dropped to a whisper. "... And you stop talking about the black horse. "
