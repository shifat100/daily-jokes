The gendarme couldn't tell the difference
In his local bistro, the handsome young Frenchman had no trouble at all picking-up the young fresh-faced girl tourist.

He took her to a nearby public park, and thinking that the setting was sufficiently secluded, he proceeded to do what comes naturally at such times.

Whereupon a gendarme runs up from behind some trees and says "Necrophilia?  How disgusting!  How could you make love to a dead woman like that?"

Whereupon the Frenchman keeps up his stroke and waves the gendarme off, explaining "She's not dead, officer - she's American"
