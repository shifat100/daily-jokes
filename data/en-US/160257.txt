The field where I grow my fucks
Some poetry for you fine folks
(Please read in an epic Shakespeare tone)

Behold! This is the field where I grow my fucks

Lay thine eyes upon it and see that it is barren

But, wait! Is that a fuck I see,
Growing upon my barren land?

Quick! I must extinguish this fuck that hath been planted in my field

Who dare seek to plant fucks in my field?

He who plants these fucks in my field is surely mistaken

For I give no fucks, no fucks at all
