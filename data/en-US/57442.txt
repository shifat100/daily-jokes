A woman goes to a psychologist
And tells him that her husband just walks into the room and gets angry and starts raging. 

The psychologist thinks about this and then figures out a solution.

"When you see your husband start to get angry, just take a cup of water and start slowly drinking it", he said.

The woman didn't see how this would help, but she was out of ideas and decided to try it out.

When she went home and her husband started to get angry, she quickly grabbed a cup of water and slowly drank it.

It worked! He immediately stopped being angry and just walked away.

So for the next few weeks, whenever he began getting mad, she just did that.

She went back to the psychologist to thank him and to ask him how the water made her husband calm down.

The psychologist smiled and said, "It had nothing to do with the water. It was all because you stopped talking."
