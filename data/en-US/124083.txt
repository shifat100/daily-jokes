The Greatest French Hockey Player
A hockey-loving guy from Ontario moves to Quebec. He turns on the radio to listen to the Toronto Maple Leafs vs Montreal Canadiens hockey game. He realizes that the game is being broadcast in French, but he decides to listen anyways. Maybe He'll be able to make out what's happening and at least get the score. He's listening for the score and keeps hearing the name "La Rondelle". "Man, this La Rondelle guy is everywhere!" The Man thinks to himself. The next day He's talking to the guys at work and mentions "Hey have you guys heard of La Rondelle? Is he like a star around here?" "Oui, He is Le Hockey Puck"
