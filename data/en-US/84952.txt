A Metric System Pun
"Hey," says Bob to Tim. "I have a riddle for you."

"Oh?" asks Tim. "Well shoot."

"Okay," Bob replies. "What do you get when you have ten dams in one place?"

"Ten dams?" asks Tim.

"Ten dams," confirms Bob. "Like a beaver dam and such."

"Oh this is a hard one," says Tim. "Ten dams, huh? Ten dams... Hm..."
