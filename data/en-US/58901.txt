A man walks into a bar
He goes over to the counter and is about to ask the bartender for a drink when he freezes. There, before him, was the bartender having sex with a monkey.

"What are you doing?" the man asks, repulsed.

"I'm having sex with this monkey." the bartender replies.

"That. That is absolutely disgusting. I'm sorry, I can't drink in this bar." and he leaves.

He goes to the bar over the road and sits down. He has a drink and says to the bartender, "You won't believe what I've just seen."
