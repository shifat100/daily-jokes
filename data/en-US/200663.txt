The Cat In The Hat On Aging
I cannot see,

I cannot pee;

I cannot chew,

I cannot screw;

Oh, my God, what can I do?

My memory shrinks,

My hearing stinks,

No sense of smell -

I look like hell

My mood is bad - can you tell?

My body's drooping,

Have trouble pooping;

The Golden Years have come at last -

The Golden years can kiss my ass
