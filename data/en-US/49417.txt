Balkan race
A Serb, a Croat, a Bosniak, a Slovene, a Montenegrin and an Albanian decided to hold a race to see whose country is the best Balkan country. It was a complete failure: the Montenegrin fell asleep, the Bosniak got lost, the Croat got in a fight with the audience, the Serb got into a fight with the Albanians and the Slovene ran away!
