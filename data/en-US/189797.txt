A couple of old guys were golfing
when one mentioned he was going to go  to Dr.John R. Smith for a new set of dentures in the morning. His  elderly buddy remarked that he, too, had gone to the very same dentist  two years before."Is that so?" asked the first old guy. "Did he do a  good job?"

The second oldster replied, "Well, I was on the golf  course yesterday when a
guy on the next fairway hooked a shot. The  ball must have been  going at least 200 mph when it smacked me right in the  testicles."

The first old guy was confused and asked, "What the  hell does that have to do with your dentures?"

"It was the  first time in two years my teeth didn't hurt....."
