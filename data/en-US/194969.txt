The two finalists were a Yale graduate and a redneck. The final contest was for them to make a poem in 2 minutes containing a word that would be given to them by the judges. The word was "TIMBUKTU".  The Yale graduate was the first to give his poem:  Slowly across the desert sand, Trekked a lonely caravan. Men on camels two by two, Destination Timbuktu.  The audience went wild. They thought the redneck would never stand a chance against him-a YALE graduate.  Nevertheless, the redneck stood up and gave his poem:  Me and Tim a hunting went, Met three whores in a pop-up tent. They were three and we were two, So I bucked one and Timbuktu.  The redneck won hands down.
