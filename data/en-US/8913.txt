A bus is going from Texas to South Carolina at night time.
When daylight comes a fellow passenger asks the driver: 'hey, that was a smooth ride last night except for that bit when it got all bumpy. What was all that about'? The driver says: 'well, we ran over some blacks'. The passenger says: 'seriously? But that lasted over half an hour'. The driver replied: 'yeah, that's cause we had to drive into the fields to get them'.
