Dumb Blonde Joke
A man wanted to prove that blondes were not dumb, so he went to a mall. He asked one blonde to come up on stage. "What's 2+2" he asked. She answered with 7. "Incorrect." he said. "Give her another chance!" the other blondes chanted. "Alright, what's 2+2?" She answered with 22 this time. "Again, incorrect!" the man said. "Give her another chance!" the blondes demanded. He asked her the same question. "…4?" she answered. "Give her another chance!" yelled the blondes.
