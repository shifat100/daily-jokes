My Mishap of a Joke
I gave my fashionable gay friend a straitjacket. 

He couldn't pull it off



**Note** I do think this is somewhat funny, but I also think I've buggered up and basically rammed 2 jokes that would've also been good together.

so - I gave my fashionable friend a straitjacket; they couldn't pull it off

and - I gave my gay friend a straitjacket; they couldn't pull it off. 

the wordplay on strait & gay doesn't really add anything when the joke is actually wordplay on fashion.

So I like it.... but at the same time feel I've possibly fucked it up.
