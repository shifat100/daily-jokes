the 'winning' blonde
A blonde is strolling down the street and spots a soda drink dispensing vending machine... She puts in $1, pushes the button and a soda can pops out.

Impressed by this she puts in another $1, pushes the button and another soda can pops out.

20min later a man walks past and spots the blonde at the soda drink dispensing vending machine and sees around 100 soda cans over flowing the floor. 

"what are you doing, don't you think you have an excessive amount of soda cans?" Asks the man to which the blonde replies...


"I know, I know but I've never been on such a winning streak before - this is so awesome!"
