Two great trees were swaying in a forest.
One of the trees looks down and sees a sapling growing between them. The tree says to the other, "is that a son of a beech or a son of a birch"?  Unsure of the answer, the other tree beckons a woodpecker to investigate.  The bird flies down to the sapling, takes a little taste and returns to the two great trees.  "Gentleman", he said, "that is neither a son of a beech nor a son of a birch. That, my friends, is the finest piece of ash I've ever had my pecker in"!
