An old man is sitting on his porch
An old man is sitting on his porch when he sees a group of teenaged boys walking down the street. As the boys get closer he notices that have something rolled up under their arms. "Hey, what you boys got there?" "Don't worry about it old man," say the boys. "What is that under your arms!?" he asks again. "Chicken wire" one of the boys replies. "Well what that hell are you doing with chicken wire? he asks. "We're going to catch some chickens." "Boys, you can't catch chickens with chicken wire!" "Whatever old man." The boys continue to walk down the street and the old man continues sitting on his porch. 

Later that after noon the old man sees the boys walking back the opposite way. Each boy has a chicken under his arm. 
"Where did you boys get those chickens?" he asks. 
"Don't worry about it old man." 
"Now you tell me where you got those chickens!" he yells. 
"We caught them with the chicken wire." they reply. 
"You can't catch chickens with chicken wire. That isn't what it is for!" The boys walk away

The next morning the man is again on his porch when he sees the boys approach again. This time the boys each have a large bag full of something as they walk by. 
"Hey boys, what you got in them bags?" 
"Duck tape." the boys say.
 "What the hell are you boys doing with all that duct tape?" 
"We are going to catch ducks." 
"You can't catch ducks with duct tape! That isn't what it is for!" he exclaims
"Whatever old man." they say as they walk off.
Low and behold, later that day, the boys walk by with a duck under each arm.
"How did you catch those ducks?" he asks.
"Don't worry about it old man." the boys say.
"Now boys, you tell me right now how you caught those ducks!"
"With duck tape!" they yell as they keep walking.
"You can't catch ducks with duct tape!" the old man yells after them.

The next morning the man sees the boys walking down the street only this time each one has a few sticks under their arm.
"What have you boys got now?" he asks.
"Don't worry about it old man!"
"Now you boys tell me what you got there!" he yells. 
"Pussy willows." one of them says.
"Hang on, let me get my coat." the old man replies.
