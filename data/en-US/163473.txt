Stop me if you've heard this one before...
So Jesus is walking along Space Beach with a child, and the child looks back at the sand and says, "Jesus, why is there only one set of footsteps sometimes?"

Jesus replies, "You think that's scary? I'm the one that's gotta walk out of here alone!"

The child pauses, but then gets the joke, and he dons a smirk. "*Nailed it.*"

"Ugh. Jesus jokes never get old, seriously. How old are you, kid?"

The child frowns. "35, why?"

The robed, bearded man walking alone with him grins. "Aren't you a little old to still believe in Jesus?"

The oddly old kid begins running, but it's too late: in Space Beach, no one can hear you scream.

Also, there's no air, so he's been dead the whole time.
