I used to attend Spanish classes in a yellow building.
But one day i forgot to bring my pen.

And i had to figure out , how to ask someone for a pen in Spanish
I quickly gave it a thought
Tener - To have , pluma - pen
Then that must mean ¿Tiene Pluma? = Do you have a pen?
But for some reason people acted weird when i asked them...
And then my teacher informed me that
¿Tiene Pluma? Was actually slang.
Slang for "are you gay?"
