So there are 3 drunk guys at a party...
...located on the rooftop of a 10-story building, and the first guy says to the second:  

"There is a magic forcefield around this building, and if you jump out the window the forcefield will bounce you right back. Watch this!"

The third guy then goes up to the first and says:

"You can be a real asshole when you're drunk, Superman."
