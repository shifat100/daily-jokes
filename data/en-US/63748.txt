The streets were filled with darkness, the roadside lamps were dim and the night was silent. The full moon shined but it was not strong enough to reach the dark alleyways. It was midnight and I was walking alone on the empty footpath. There was no one else around; no people, no cars, no animals. Just me and the darkness. That’s when I heard a sound. I turned around. Nothing.
