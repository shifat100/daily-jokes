An elderly man is stooped over under a streetlight...
Seeing the old man is obviously looking for something, a passing young man asks what he is looking for.  
  
"Oh, I've dropped my keys and can't find them!"  
  
"Where did you drop them?" the young man inquires.  
  
"Over on the other side of the street," the old man replies.  
  
"Then why are you looking for them over here?"  
  
"Well, I can see much better under the light!"
