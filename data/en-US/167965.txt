-What the f***, man! This is my brand new ride, I ain't even got no insurance on this b****, 'cause I bought it with all my "revenue"! You... you are ohing to literally pay for this! Right here, right now! I don't care how, just get busy or me and my boys are going to stop being nice!

-I am terribly sorry sir, I can't pay you back right this moment, but my son might be able to help. He's a dolfin trainer. Do you mind if I call him over?
