At the Club on the Weekend
So I went to the club this past weekend, and, jerk that I am, I went up to this one girl and sneered, "So, you think you can dance, eh?*"  
And then I assembled an all-star panel of celebrity judges, and determined by a score of 3-2, that, yes, she could dance.  

\* So you think you can dance, eh? is the new Canadian version of the popular American show.  (Only on CBC - home of Hockey Night in Canada.**)  

** For about the next two and a half weeks, at least.
