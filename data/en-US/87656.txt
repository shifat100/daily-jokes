Pig at the Movies
A man in a movie theater notices what looks like a pig sitting next to him.

 "Are you a pig?" asked the man, surprised.

 "Yes." replies the Pig.

 "What are you doing at the movies?" asks the man.

and the pig replied, "Well, I liked the book."
