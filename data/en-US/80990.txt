Today in history: Dec. 7th
Ever hear about that Ozzy Osbourne show that almost was? Just a quick story from television history.  It was a time of dull reality shows, and this was set to be a spin-off of the Osbournes, aimed at being a Seinfeld-style show. Each episode was to start off with Osbourne doing stand-up, then the episode would be based around the material. It was well-written and funny, yet it never made it past the first episode which aired on Dec. 7, 2006 despite its good reviews, shocking many.  The material was nothing more crass than his previous show, and it ran for years. 

Honestly nobody really knows the real reason behind the Comic Ozzy Pilot bombing, but it caught everyone off guard.
