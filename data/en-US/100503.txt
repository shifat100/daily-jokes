In 1992, Severus Snape was interviewed by The Daily Prophet
Reporter: "So, Severus, last question here. What is your favorite musician?"

Snape: "Well, like many of you, I love The Weird Sisters. But I have to admit that I happen to love one muggle rock band"

Reporter: "Really? What is the name?"

Snape: "It's Bon Jovi. I've been their fan for quite a long time. I believe since I listened to 'Runaway' for the first time."

Reporter: "Wow, that's interesting. Maybe I should start listening to muggle music too. And by the way, what is your favorite song, after all this time?"

Snape: "Always"
