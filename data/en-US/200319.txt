Look at the Check
A guy eats at a restaurant.

At the end of the meal, the guy looks at the check:

Salads . . . . . . $3

Steak . . . . . . $10

Works . . . . . . .$5

Cola . . . . . . . $2

-----------------------

Total             $20

The customer asked the waiter "What's 'works'?"

"This time it didn't work" said the waiter and crossed out that row.
