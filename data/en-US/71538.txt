The profiteer and philosopher
A woman walks into Walmart and confronts the manager training new employees.

Woman: You must be a profiteer.

Manager: I guess but how did you know?

Woman: Well, you learned about the immense problem of unemployment and apparently thought wow, a cheap source of labor I can use to cut costs!

Manager: Fair enough. You must be a philosopher then.

Woman: Yes! How did you know?

Manager: Well because unemployment has been around for ages and appears intractable and you've been unable to make any real change and yet when I offer an alternative to their current state, and some take it, its somehow my fault!
