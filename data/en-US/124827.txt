A man finds an old childhood friend...
So a man runs into an old buddy from his childhood. 

Man: "Hey! How have you been?" 
Friend: "Hey man! I've been great!" 
Man: "So how are your siblings? I remember there were six of you." 
Friend: "Not anymore there are 16 of us now." 
Man: "WOW! with that many kids you ought to put your mother on a pedestal!" 
Friend: "oh hell yeah. We put her on a huge one." 
Man: "Really? Why, to do her justice?" 
Friend: "No not justice, It's so my old man won't get to her!" 


This joke is original in Spanish so... sorry if it isn't that funny.
