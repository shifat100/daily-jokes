​Women empowerment
Two men are traveling in a bus.

The older gentleman, being curious, asks the young guy, "What do you do for a living?"

The young man replied, "I help women in boosting their self esteem."

The older guy is now impressed and asks, "That's great! Do you work in a nonprofit for women's empowerment?"

The young man smiles and says, "No, Sir! I **like** the selfies posted by girls on Facebook."
