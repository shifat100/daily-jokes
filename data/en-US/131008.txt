A man comes back to the hospital to find out the results of his check-up.
The man walks into his doctors office and takes a seat. 

The doctor says "Alright, Jim. I'm afraid I've got bad news, and worse news.."

Jim replies nervously "A-alright, well tell me the bad news first."

Doctor: "You only have 24 hours to live."

Jim: "So, then what could be the worse news?" 

Doctor: "I forgot to call you yesterday."
