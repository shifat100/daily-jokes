There was a man who got a job as a...
...hotel receptionist and his boss was giving him some tips the day before his very first shift.

One of the tips was: "When showing the guests to their rooms, always be polite and say their names which is usually situated on the label on their suitcases."

After hearing this, the receptionist started his job the next day and led his first guests to their room. Remembering what his boss said, he looked at the label on their suitcase and said: "Welcome to our hotel, we hope you enjoy your stay Mr and Mrs real leather!"
