Story from a college where speaking in English is mandatory
Our college had the rule that all students should speak in English, all the time, in the campus. We all had different mother tongues, and were more happy to converse in our MTs. So we used to speak to friends in MTs and only when we have to converse with some of the staff and principal, we used English. Our English wasn't very good. So one day, a national holiday was declared due to the death of a political leader. Our college had a habit of conducting lectures even on holidays. So a few of us decided to go and ask the Principal whether we have a holiday for the college the next day. The biggest problem we faced was, no one had any clue how to ask this in English. Finally, a brave friend of mine took up the challenge, he went to the principal's office and asked... 

"Sir, tomorrow is a holiday; True or False"....
