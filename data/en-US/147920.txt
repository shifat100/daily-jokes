My roommate's accidental joke
My roommate is Indian, so every week his mother would bring him some Indian food. This time we chowed down on chicken curry, but left a good amount of [naan](http://www.culinaryadventuresinthekitchen.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/12/naan4.jpg) in the fridge. My roommate then said "I will tell my mother to bring more curry because we've got naan (none)." After I reminded him of this "witty" joke he has made, we all facepalmed.
