Never enough power
Walked into the CFO's office while he was in a meeting with all the Directors around a table because I got a service call about a power issue.   I check under the desk and pop my head up to say "well there's not enough power here".    The CFO laughs and says "Yeah there is a whole lot of power here".   I laughed and said,  "well now I see why you called me,  you wanted even more power at your table" as I plugged in their disconnected power strip.

You had to have been there,  a perfectly timed joke on my part if I must say so myself.   The life of an IT Guy.
