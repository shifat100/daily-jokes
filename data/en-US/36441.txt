A man joined a joke club.
There he listened to people say things such as 506 or 328 and everybody would laugh. He asked what the deal was. They told him: listen, we all know the jokes by heart and assign them numbers. Instead of retelling them we just say the number, remember the joke and laugh together.
He thought 'fuck, I'll get an easy laugh here' so he shouted: 758
Nobody laughed. Silence. So he asks 'is there no joke numbered 758? To which they answered : yes there is, but it is the way that you tell it.
