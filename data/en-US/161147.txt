So this kid comes home from school crying...
...and his mom asks him what's wrong. He sniffles and says, "I lost a dollar in the playground today!" 

His mom smiles sympathetically and says, "Well, that's okay." She hands him a loonie ($1 bill for Americans).

To her surprise and dismay, the kid starts to wail even louder after this. 

"What's wrong?" she asks, concerned.

"I should've said I lost two dollars!"
