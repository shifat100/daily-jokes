So three guys are captured by a South American tribe...
Three guys, Ben, Joel, and Josh are walking through the South American jungle one day when they are captured by an indigenous tribe and are told they will be allowed to go if they complete a trial. After some discussion they all agree and they ask what the trial involves, they are told they each need to go off and find ten of any type of fruit they can find.

Josh searches hard and finally finds some small red berries, when he comes back,  he is told to insert them into his anus, he thinks it is odd, but for his life it's okay. He has no trouble fitting the berries in there and he completes the trial successfully.

The second guy, Joel, comes back with 10 peaches, but they really weren't easy to find, again he is asked to insert them into his anus and he manages to get 9 of them up there with some difficulty and a bit of stretching, but then he starts laughing and fails the task.
"Why did you start laughing?" Josh said.

And then Joel replied, still in fits of laughter.

"Because I just saw Ben coming down the path with 10 pineapples."
