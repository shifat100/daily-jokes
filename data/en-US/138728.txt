Two Native Americans run into a town
They go to the pharmacy and ask for help. They don't speak much English though and say: "big chief, no shit. big chief, no shit." The pharmacist gives them some medicin and tells them to return if it doesn't work. The next day they return. "big chief, no shit, big chief no shit!" Alright, the pharmacist says, and he gives them another medicin. Return if it doesn't work! They next day they return again, crying. What happened? asks the pharmacist. Did it work? The Natives reply: "big shit, no chief, big shit no chief!"
