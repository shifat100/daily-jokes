An American, a French, and a Mexican are on a plane
The American places his hand outside of the plane and exclaims,"We are in America!". The others, confused, ask him how he knows this. "I felt the Statue of Liberty.", he replies. Later, the French yells,"
We are in France!". The American and the Mexican, perplexed, ask him how he knows. "I touched the Eiffel Tower." A while later, the Mexican says,"We are in Mexico", a little annoyed. The others ask him what he felt. "Nothing, but they stole my watch."
