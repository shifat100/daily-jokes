Not a joke- but a pretty good idea for a practical joke
A little backstory:  I live on a pretty rural road.  Once or twice a month on the weekend we get a huge train of bicyclists that come through on the road.  These are the die hard cyclists in the painted on clothes and whatnot.  The other day, I finally realized how the entire train of cyclists knew where to turn and whatnot.  Three bikers came through the neighborhood and stopped at every intersection and used a can of spray paint to paint a symbol on the road surface to direct bikers in their travel on the weekend.  Lots of symbols that are a circle with a line through one side (think of a power button symbol on your computer) and basic arrows at intersections.  

The thought then struck me-- It would be pretty damn funny to go back down the route with my own can of spraypaint and redirect the bikers through the ghetto and around in a big circle going through the best drug dealing neighborhoods in town.  But that idea just don't seem good enough.  I would love some ideas on how to pull a fast one without anyone getting hurt.  Just some simple fun!
