A Giraffe walks into a bar.
He sits down at the bar and gets the Bartenders attention.

Bartender says: "What will you have."

Giraffe says: "Vodka Martini, stirred."

Bartender makes the martini, brings it the giraffe and says, "That will be $20."

Giraffe pays and sips his drink.

A few minutes later, the bartender returns and says to the giraffe, "you know, we don't get a lot of giraffes in here."

To which the giraffe responds, "well for a $20 martini, you sure as hell won't.
