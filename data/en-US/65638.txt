A woman holding a baby gets into a bus....
and the bus driver exclaims: "Oh my, what an ugly baby!" The woman, shaken by the insult, sits down next to an older gentleman. He notices her distress and asks: "Why do you look so distraught?" The woman answers: "The bus driver insulted and made fun of my little darling, I feel very disrespected." The old man replies: "You shouldn't let yourself be treated like this, do something about it. Go up to him and tell him off while I'll keep an eye on your monkey."
