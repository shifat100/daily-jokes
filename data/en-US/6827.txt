A 3 legged dog walks into an Old West Saloon...
As he enters a hush falls over the establishment. The piano stops playing, poker games halt as all eyes are on this dog as he walks up to the bar. The 3 legged dog slowly takes out his gun and rests it on the bar top. The barkeep says "What can I do for ya" to which the 3 legged dog sternly replies "I'm looking for the man who shot my Paw."
