Lame Irish joke
A company was celebrating it's 99th year in buisness and invite graphic designers to submit a concept they would use for promoting this milestone. Paddy submitted a artwork of a field with three trees, each tree being pissed on by a young boy. "Dirty tree an dirty tree and dirty tree - 99 you see?" He explained. The execs were amused but rejected the concept. The next year for the big One Hundredth year celebration Paddy submitted the exact same concept except each tree also had a dog taking a crap on it. "Dirty tree and a turd, dirty tree and a turd and dirty tree and a turd" he beamed :)
