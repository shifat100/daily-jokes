Super... Smash... Brothers!
To celebrate the return of the celebrated fighters Mewtwo, Lucas, and Roy, Peach decided to throw a large, extravagant party at her castle, but she requested everyone bring something for the party.

First, Mario and Luigi pull up in their Mario Karts, pulling out their boxes of mushroom-covered pizza.

Then, Link and Toon Link arrive, pulling into the train station and dragging a significant supply of fireworks.

A duo of Arwings swoop down, and Fox and Falco jump out of them, parachuting down - and each carrying a box of reflector-shines, noticing the fireworks.

Then, a glint is visible in the sky as a large ship sails through - N's Plasma Frigate, as Pikachu, Lucario, Greninja, and Jigglypuff jump out. "You can come out, it's fine," Charizard roared, as Mewtwo embarrassedly hopped on board. Each of the Pokemon had flutes with significant decoration - except for Mewtwo's.

Mewtwo has no customs.

5 minutes later, the Blue Falcon pulls up into the driveway. Captain Falcon is standing outside, wheeling in 8 kegs.

"Hey, look, I brought the FALCON PUNCH!"
