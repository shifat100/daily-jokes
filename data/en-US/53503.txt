Problem with fire ants?
Step 1: Buy a can of black spray paint

Step 2: Stir up each ant mound with a stick. The ants will emerge by the hundreds to defend their mound. 

Step 3: Spray each mound and the surrounding area, make sure you get plenty of paint on the ants as well. 

Once the ants realize they live in a black neighborhood, they 
quit working and start killing each other. 

Problem solved.
