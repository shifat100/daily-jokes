The secret to long life
On the day of a man's 110th birthday, the local newspapers decide to pay the man a visit.

"Wow, 110! What's your secret?" They asked.

"Well," he replies "I always make sure to eat right. I get plenty of exercise each day, I hardly drink, and I've never smoked in my life."

Suddenly there is a loud crash heard in the next room. "What was that!?" The media asked.

"Oh that's my grandpa. He always gets rowdy when he's been drinking."
