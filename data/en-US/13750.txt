How do you start a rave in Ethiopia?
Well first you need to build up your playlist, a lot of popular / rave music is important to get the crowd going!


Then, you need to find a venue, stadiums can be quite good etc.


You ideally need a DJ, or even your own iPad and a couple speakers if it's a smaller setup


Then, of course, to get the crowd going on the big night, you need to staple food to the ceiling.
