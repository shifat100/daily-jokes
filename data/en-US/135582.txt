While I was fishing...
A warden came up to me without me hearing him. He caught me with a big bucket full of water and quite a few fishes.

He asked me if I had a permit for all those fishes. I replied...

"No, because these are tamed. The already belong to me !"

The warden said : "What the fuck are you talking about ?"

Me : "Everyday I bring them here to the lake. Drop them in the water and let them swim for awhile. And when it's time to go home, I whistle and they all come back and we go home..."

Warden : "Yea right !"

Me : "Here, I'll show you !" 

So I empty my bucket in the lake and I wait !

After a little while...

Warden : "So ?"

Me : So what ?"

Warden : "Are you gonna call them back ?"

Me : "Call back who ?"

Warden :"Well the fishes obviously !!!"

Me : "What fishes ???" and I just walked away.
