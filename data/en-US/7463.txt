A Steel Mill Needed More Workers...
My teacher was telling our class how in the Ohio area, the locals, being accustomed to the cold, didn't want to work in a hot steel mill.  The owners of the mill went down to Latin America and offered the Latinos relatively high wages to come work in Ohio.  Since they were already used to the heat and way underpaid, the Mexicans readily agreed.  

I thought they might have wanted the job for another reason.  I explained to the class, "Yeah, they heard the word 'steel' and we're all in."
