A bovine love story
Honeyball the bull was in love with the new cow, but the farmer decided to keep the cow inside an enclosure, fenced with long pointed spiked. Honeyball tried in vain to get close to her. After a week Honeyball was desperate, so he decided to give it a final try. 

He paced up and took a strong galloping jump over the long spiked fence. He succeeded. The cow was astonished and said, wow Honeyball you are so strong! The bull walked up to her and said coyly, darling, from now on you can call me honey .... my balls are up there on the spikes.
