3 Guys Each Get 3 Wishes From A Magic Genie
3 guys, Steven, Earl, and David are exploring an ancient temple. When they get to the middle of the temple, a magical Genie appears.
"Hello Adventurers! As a reward for finding me, I will grant you each 3 wishes. What are your first wishes?"
Steven, the proud one, asks for a trillion dollars.
"It is done," the Genie says.
Earl, the thoughtful one, asks for world peace
"it is done"
David, the funny one, asks for his left arm to spin clockwise
"it is done" and his arm immediately spinning clockwise very fast.

"Ok, time for your second wishes."
Steven asks for a glorious mansion.
Earl asks for world hunger to end.
David asks for his *right* arm to spin *counter*clockwise
These wishes are all granted immediately.

"Alright" the Genie says, "Time for your last wishs so chose wisely!", eyeing David and his rapidly spinning arms.
Steven asks for a beautiful wife who will pleasure him however he wants.
Earl asks to solve climate change.
David asks for his head to rock backwards and forwards
"Ok... it is done" and the Genie disappears forever.

After the leaving the magic temple, the 3 men go their seperate ways. However, after 10 years, they meet up to discuss their lives and reflect on what they had done. Steven starts the conversation. "Ahhh, my life is amazing, I have everything I ever wanted and everything I didn't realise I did want!"
Earl says "Ahhh, my life is amazing, I have solved so many of the world's problems, everyone loves me!"
David says, with his still spinning arms and rocking head, "Guys, I think I fucked up"
