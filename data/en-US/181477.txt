Two Chickens walk into a library...
...and walk up to the desk, where they say "Buk Buk Buk!" So the Librarian gives them three books.

A while later the Chickens come back, looking slightly frustrated. They go to the desk and say "Buk Buk Buk Buk Buk!" The Librarian gives them five books.

Again, the Chickens come back and ask for seven books. The Librarian, now quite confused, follows the Chickens as they leave with their books. They arrive at a park, where she sees the Chickens throwing the books at a frog, who says "Reddit, Reddit, Reddit."
