Money for Dry Cleaning Vomit
An old drunk is swaying on his barstool.  After slamming yet another shot of whiskey, he vomits all over the front of his shirt. 

"Ahhhh shite!  I'm ruined. She's gonna give it to me now!" He grumbled.

"What's that now?" Inquired the bartender.

"My wife...she said if I got drunk ONE more time, she was gonna throw me out.  And now I'm all covered in my own God damned puke.  I'm done for."

The bartender tries to calm the man down.  "I've got a trick for you.  Take a $10 bill and stick it in your front pocket.  When your wife asks what happened, tell her some OTHER asshole got drunk and puked all over you.  Then he gave you that $10 for dry cleaning."

"THAT'S BLOODY GENIUS! I'ma try that one."


After a few more rounds, the drunk stumbles home.  He puts on his best sober impression and waltzes into his living room.  His wife is waiting up for him.  
Upon seeing his vomit-stained shirt, she explodes.  "YOU DONE IT AGAIN, NOW HAVEN'T YOU!" 
 
"No, no, no.  Some other guy...he got drunk...and HE threw up all over ME.  So, he gave me this here $10 to get it cleaned," he said as he waived the bill in pride.

"Well, that's not a $10 bill, that's a $20 bill," said his wife.

"Ohhhhh, ya.  I forgot to mention, he also SHIT in my pants!"
