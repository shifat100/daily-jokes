Having a baby face can be rough...
I turned 34 this past week but still look really young. I even got carded for cigarettes this past weekend. It really is hard to explain how much disrespect you have to endure when you look like a teenager. Even from family.

For instance, I was sitting at the diner table at my mom's house when my aunt walked in. She lives out of town and we don't see each other much. Though I didn't expect her to say, "How old are you now? Twelve?" I lost it. I got so mad that I stopped what I was doing, rolled up my Transformers coloring book and came close to stomping up the stairs to my room.
