My Favorite Joke
I have no idea who originally wrote it and since I am rarely on this sub I have no idea if it has already been posted.

Guy walks into a bar, sets a big bag down on the floor and orders a drink.  No one else is there so the bartender asks the guy what's in the bag.  The guy shrugs, reaches into the bag and sets a tiny grand piano down on the bar.  He reaches in again and pulls out this little guy, no taller than a foot, dressed in a full tux who proceeds to sit at the piano and play beautiful classical music.

Bartender:  That's incredible!  Is it animatronic?  Did you make it?  You could make a fortune, it looks so real!  Where did you get it?

Guy:  You wouldn't believe me if I told you.

Bartender:  Oh, come on.   Where did you get it?

The guy reaches into the bag again and sets a dirty old metal lamp on the bar.  Guy, "I was walking down the beach about a half hour ago and I saw this half buried in the sand.  I thought about those stories about genies in lamps so I picked it up and rubbed it and sure enough a genie appeared."

Bartender:  Yeah, right.  You're right, I don't believe you.

Guy:  Try it yourself.

So the bartender picks up the lamp and rubs it and just like the guy said there is a flash of light, a cloud of smoke and a genie appears saying, "I will grant you one wish."

Bartender:  I want a million bucks!

The genie claps his hands, says, "Your wish is granted" and disappears.  The bar is suddenly filled with ducks.  There are ducks everywhere.  The bartender turns to the guy and screams, "I SAID A MILLION BUCKS!  NOT A MILLION DUCKS!"

Guy:  Do you really think I wished for a twelve inch pianist?
