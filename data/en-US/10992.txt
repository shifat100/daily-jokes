A bear walking along the pond sees the fish eyeing the fly and thinks. If that fly drops 6 inches the fish will go for the fly and I can grab the fish. So the bear crouches behind a bush and waits.

A little while later a hunter comes by the pond and sees the situation and thinks to himself if that fly were to drop just 6 inches then the fish would get the fly, the bear will go for the fish and I can get a clear shot on the bear. So the hunter sits down and starts to enjoy his lunch.

And we would all be very happy.

A small time goes by and:
