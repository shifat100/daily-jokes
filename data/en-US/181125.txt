An American, a Chinese Man and a Mexican are standing on a cliff
and throwing objects off the side. The Chinese man holds up a bag of rice and says "We have too much of this in my country!" And throws the rice over.

The Mexican approaches the ledge and throws off a stack of taco shells repeating "We have too much of this in my country!"

Finally, the American approaches the ledge, grabs the Mexican and yells "We have too much of this in my country!" and throws him off.
