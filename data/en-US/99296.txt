A girl publishes an ad on social networks
She promises 10,000$ to anyone who can fuck her 16 times in a row.

First comes the Englishman. He manages two, three times, but during the fourth one, he gives up.

Next comes the Frenchman. He gets up to seven, then during the eighth one, he gives up.

Finally comes the Hungarian. The girl is drawing little lines on a paper to help her count. It goes up to eight and the Hungarian still doesn't show signs of tiredeness. So she decides to only count every third one from now on.

But still, even when she's only counting every third one, the Hungarian manages eleven, twelve. She gets scared now, so she decides to only count every fifth one from now on.

But even when she's only counting every fifth one, the Hungarian manages fourteen, fifteen. The girl is really alarmed at this point. Suddenly, the Hungarian looks at the paper and exclaims:

"Hey, you're cheating! We're starting over!"
