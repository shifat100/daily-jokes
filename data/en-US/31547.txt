Hip shapes - such as the dude-decahedron and octagona-stay-up-al-night - were threatening to kill other shapes unless they joined them in becoming cool.  


Parallelogram was on his way back from chess club when he was surrounded by the gang. They pointed their guns at him.


'What do you want from me?' moaned parallelogram

'I don't understand' whined parallelogram 


Octagona-stay-up-al-night looked at parallelogram over his shades.
