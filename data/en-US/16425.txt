A former Cincinnati zoo employee is at a job interview...
The interviewer is looking over the applicant's resume and notices his former role at the zoo.

"It says here that you worked in the Great Ape exhibit up until the summer of 2016," the interviewer says as he stares intensely at the applicant.

The applicant pauses but maintains a straight face, "yes, that is correct." 

The interviewer continues to eyeball the applicant and hesitantly asks, "so what made you leave your position there? Are you seeking better pay or was the work environment stressful or...?" 

The applicant lets out an exacerbated sigh...

"OK, fine! Let's talk about the 800 pound gorilla in the room."
