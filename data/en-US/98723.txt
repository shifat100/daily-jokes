A black man, a Mexican and a white man...
They find a genie which agrees to grant them each one wish.
The Mexican asks for all of his people to be happy and in Mexico. 
POOF! The wish is granted.
The black guy asks for all of his people to be happy and back in Africa.
POOF! The wish is granted.
The genie finally goes to the white guy and says, "What will your wish be?"
The white guy says: "You mean to tell me that all of the blacks and Mexicans are out of the country?"
The genie says,"Yes, of course! That is what they wished!"
The white guy says, "Then i guess I'll just have a coke."

(From the movie Boondock Saints - 1999)
