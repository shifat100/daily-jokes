School Subjects Come To Life
One day all of the school subjects came to life and announced themselves to the world.

The first subject was Math, who came out with a cheerful attitude and full of curiosity.

The second subject was English, who came out all proper, correcting everyone's grammar.

Third came Philosophy, who came out with wonder of the world around him, and questioning his very own existence.

And after a while, lastly came Middle Eastern Studies, who came out sobbing out of control.

Everyone asked the other courses why it was so sad.

To that they replied, "Well, it is a sensitive subject."
