Bob's Burgers
Two men go to China on vacation, and they find out about a food eating competition.  They decide to register for it online, but find out in a reply message that the contest has been challenged due to lack of entrants.  They go to a nearby restaurant to eat instead.  

"Damn", one of the men says, "Now I will never impress anyone here with how much I can eat".  

"Yes you will", the other man says, "right after you finish the three burgers you just ordered for yourself.".
