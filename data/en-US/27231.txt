So an American family goes on vacation to Paris, France
The family finds just about all the French people in Paris to very, very rude, except for the bellhop in their hotel, who is extraordinarily sweet and friendly, always all smiles, warm and welcoming.

Well, anyway, they get back home to America and decide to develop their pictures of their trip to Paris.

They get their photos back and find pictures of the bellhop, smiling, with all their toothbrushes stuck up his ass.

Moral of the story: next time you're in Paris and a Parisian is being rude to you, just be grateful he isn't being nice to you.
