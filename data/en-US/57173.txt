A Guy with a newspaper
A Guy with a newspaper was going around the streets selling newspapers, he kept saying "31 People fell for it"
People were looking at him, curious for who fell for it & what did they fall for?
One guy went to the newspaper guy & asked him who fell for it & what they fell for, he refused to answer them - told them they had to buy the newspaper to find out.
he kept moving & they followed him begging him for the answer, as they didn't want to buy the newspaper for 1 article
he told them pay the $10 & you can find out
about 5 minutes later, they finally agreed to pay...
They bought the Newspaper & he continued moving on the street saying "32 People fell for it"
