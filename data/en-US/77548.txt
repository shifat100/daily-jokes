Two people are building a computer
Credit has to go to my tech sazzy grandfather for this one.

Tow people are building a computer for a project. After bickering endlessly about how much storage to have, what motherboard to get, what cpu they need, and what sort of graphics they need to run, they buy all of their parts and are ready to build it. One of them suddenly realized that they forgot the memory saying, "this is ridiculous! We have a perfect computer with no memory. What were you thinking?"

The other turns to him and says, "don't worry. God will provide the RAM."
