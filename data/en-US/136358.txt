"Oh, particular nasty weather," says the smartass. The woman shrugs him off and the drunk laughs, knowing what his friend is doing.

This woman is shocked and offended and turns toward him, shouting "What did you say?" and lifts her hand, ready to slap him. 

"Particular nasty weather!" says the smartass, ducking away from her.
