A woman gets stung by a bee on the golf course.
She rides back up to the pro shop to try to get something to relieve her pain.

**Golfer:**  "Excuse me sir, do you have any hydrocortisone cream?"

 

**Pro shop worker:**  "Yes mam, what's wrong?"

 

**Golfer:**  "I got stung by a bee."

 

**Pro shop worker:**  "Oh no! Where at?"

 
**Golfer:**  "Between the first and second hole."

 

**Pro shop worker:**  "Your stance is too wide."
