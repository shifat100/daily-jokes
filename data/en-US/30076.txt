Two Rabbi's are on a walk
Two rabbi's are on a walk when they come across a church with a sign out front, "We will give you $100 if you come in and convert!"

One rabbi says to the other "I'm going to see what this is all about".

Ten minutes later the rabbi walks out of the church with a smile on his face.

"So, are they really giving out $100" says the one rabbi.

"What is it with you people and money!" says the other.
