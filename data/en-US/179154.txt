You really oughtta diet...

He gets to the spa and starts out on the treadmill, jogs around the gym a couple times, swims a couple laps in the pool, sweats for a half hour or so in the sauna, and finishes off with a shower.

One of the guys says, "Holy shit, dude! You's about a fat son of bitch, ain't ya? I'll bet it's been a while since you seen your dick, ain't it?"

To which the obese man says, "Yeah, it's been a while."

And the other guy says, "Man, you really oughtta diet!"
