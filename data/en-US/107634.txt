A blonde, a brunette and a redhead die and go to the purgatory.
They see God who says that they will have to climb a 100 floors towers where at each floor they will hear a joke.
If they laugh, they will be send to hell and if they go to the top without having laughed once, they'll go to heaven.
The brunette starts climbing but at the 10th floor, she starts laughing.
God then says "You didn't achieve the goal so you wille be send to hell."
Then the redhead starts climbing but laughs at the 5th floor so God send her to hell too.
Finally it is the blonde's turn. She starts climbing and it seems that she will never laugh but when she is on the 99th floor she burst out laughing. God approaches her and asks "Why did you laugh, you were so close to heaven ?"
Then the blonde says "I just understood the first joke."
