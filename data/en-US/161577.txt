Dr. Michael Christopher Anders
The esteemed Brain Doctor, Michael Christopher Anders and his two assistants Lynzee and Brooke are testing a patient to see how his brain reacts to stress while sleeping.

Now obviously this test must take place at night so the two girls take turns watching over the man, with Brooke taking the first shift and Dr. Anders checking in periodically

Late into the night, Lynzee busts into Dr. Anders room and tells him urgently "Dr. Anders, I just stopped by to pick up my coat and the man hasn't been able to sleep all night!" Dr. Anders leaps from his seat and responds "No sleep? Tell Brooke, Lyn!"
