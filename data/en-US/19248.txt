Two little boys meet each other on the beach.
A: "Look at my scoop! My mom just bought it at the toy store!"

B: "That's cool, I have one too, but I have something you don't."

A: "Look at the rake then, bet you don't have one!"

B: "Yes I do, and I still have something you don't have."

A: "And what would that be?"

B: "Cancer."
