A girl goes on holidays in Jamaica
She met a nice local and they start dating.   
After a couple of days she tells him:   
- I am sorry but you never told me your name.  
- I can't, you'll find it ridiculous, I don't want you to laugh at me...  
- Oh please, please, I promise. I won't laugh at you.  
- Ok, my name is Snow.  
  
The girl looks at him with a poker face but then start to laugh, and laugh.  
- You promised you won't find it ridiculous, he says.  
- I don't. It's just, I just imagined the face of my friends in Canada when I will explain I got 20 centimetres (8 inches?) of Snow in Jamaica...  
  
**Edit:** Changed the name to just *Snow*.
