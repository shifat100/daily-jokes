Shower pom
A little girl was showering with her mom when she noticed her mom has hair between her legs and asks her what that is. Her mom tells her it's a special shower pom that she can use to clean herself with.

Few days later the little girl runs excitedly to her mom and says "mommy I've figured out another use for your special shower pom!" The mother is confused and asks what does she mean, the little girl then replied "I saw daddy clean his teeth with the babysitter's pom the other day!"
