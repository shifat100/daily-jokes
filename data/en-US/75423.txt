A Competition
There was an international competition held every year for the person with the hardest head.

Competitor A said "I can crush 10 bricks stacked together with my head", and so he did and everyone clapped.
Competitor B said "I can crush 50 bricks stacked together with my head", and so he did and everyone clapped.
Competitor C said "I can crush 100 bricks stacked together with my head", but he refuses to do so. Everyone was telling him to do it now but he still refuses. And so the judges said, "Competitor C won because of his hard-headedness".
