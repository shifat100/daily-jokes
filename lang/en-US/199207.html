English (US) Joke

T.G.I.F A Blonde is getting on an elevator and meets a gentleman on board. "T.G.I.F." she says. "S.H.I.T" was his reply. Puzzled she replied "T.G.I.F" The gentleman was getting a little disturbed the the remark, so he says again. "S.H.I.T.". The Blonde leans over and whispers "THANK GOD IT'S FRIDAY". The gentleman responses with "SORRY, HONEY IT'S THURSDAY".
