He thinks nothing of it and starts banging her harder. She then starts screaming 'machigatta ana, machigatta ana'. He thinks to himself 'boy, she must really like it'. After his meetings the following day he is invited to play golf with a Japanese group. He hits an amazing shot and punches the air screaming 'machigatta ana'. One of the Japanese looks at him and says: what do you mean by 'wrong hole'?
