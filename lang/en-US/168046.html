Since is translate your national jokes day here it goes: a romanian one. Bula is at school, the teacher enters the class and starts calling pupils to see who is missing: "Andrei?" "Here." "Anda?" "Here." "Bula?" "Here." Behind the class George starts laughing. "What's so funny George?" "Miss! If you change the first letter from Bula's name you get Pula (Penis). Hahaha" Bula frowns.
