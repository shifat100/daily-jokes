English (US) Joke

A couple walked home on Christmas Eve in Soviet Russia when the weather turned bad The wife said, "it's snowing!" "No dear, it's raining," replied the husband. They argued this as they walked until they came across a guard. After learning his name they asked, "Comrade Rudolph, is this snow or rain?" "It's rain," he replied. "See," said the husband, "Rudolph the Red knows rain, dear!"
