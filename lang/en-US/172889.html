English (US) Joke

A little boy and a little girl were walking home from school... The little boy says to the little girl, "This weekend, my dad's gonna take me to ride a horse!" The little girl says, "Oh, I have my own pony." They walk a little further. The little boy again pipes up, "Next weekend, my dad's taking me to a baseball game!" The little girl replies, "My uncle plays major league baseball." The boy scowls as they continue to walking. Finally in frustration, the little boy pulls down his pants and yells "Well, I've got one of these and you don't!" The little girl calmly lifts her dress and replies "I have one of these, and with one of these, I can get all of those I want."
