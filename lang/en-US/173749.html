English (US) Joke

My grandfather told me this one. A university professor was taking a trip to the holy land. He had a break in his seminar schedule and was visiting the sea of Galilee. He saw a sign advertising boat tours, and thought it would be interesting, so he asked the guy with the boats "How much?" "Six hundred dollars!" Said the man proudly. "All day, up to three passenger and gear!" "But, um, there's just me, and all I want is a quick row around and a couple of selfies!" "Still six hundred dollars!" The man shook his head. "Minimum price!" "That's outrageous." The man said as he walked away. "No wonder Christ walked."
