English (US) Joke

A poem about rape... He grabbed me by my slender neck, I could not call or scream. He dragged me to his dingey room, where we would not be seen. He pulled away my flimsy wrap, and looked upon my form. I was so cold and wet and scared, while he was dry and warm. He pressed his feverish lips to mine, I could not make him stop. He drained me of my every self, I gave him every drop. He made me what I am today, that's why you see me here. An empty bottle thrown away, that once was full of beer.
