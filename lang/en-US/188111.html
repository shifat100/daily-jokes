English (US) Joke

A joke my art teacher told me This is a long one, and a groaner, but it's worth it. There once was this town that was known for it's flowers. There were three florists who would make the best flower arrangements people had ever seen, and the town received many awards for them. The three florists enjoyed the competition, and didn't resent each other. One day, a friar moved into the town and started his own flower shop. He made cheap bouquets and undercut the other florists. They soon started losing customers and money. The three original florists got together and decided to hire an assassin to kill the friar. They found one name Hugh, and he agreed to kill the friar. Hugh killed him, and the three florists got there customers back, and were happy. Which just goes to show, Only Hugh can prevent Florist Friars.
