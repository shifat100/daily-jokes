English (US) Joke

A wealthy man owns dozens of island vacation resorts. He has made millions in his investments, and marries a world famous supermodel. Several years down the road, he is diagnosed with an incurable disease. As time passes, he grows more and more feeble, wasting away. As he recognizes that his time is near its end, he has his accountant look over his assets, and finds that, with him being bedridden, his wife has liquedated most of his assets, selling off his resorts to the highest bidder, and keeping the proceeds for herself. The single one remaining is the resort in which he currently resides. Furious, the dying man uses his business contacts to hire an assassin, deciding to use what is left of his assets to pay for his wife's murder. The assassin will kill his wife, chop her body into separate parts, and mail her to each of his past business rivals. The assassin arrives, and the man signs over the deed, saying: "Cut my wife into pieces. This is my last resort."
