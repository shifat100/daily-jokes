English (US) Joke

A young boy gets in trouble at school... A young boy known for getting in trouble in school comes home late one day and his father asks him, "Why are you home so late from school?" "Mrs. Daniels made me stay after class because I got in trouble" "What did you do?" "We were going over multiplication and she asked me what 3 x 2 was, I answered 6. Then she asked me what 2 x 3 was" "Thats the same fucking thing!" yelled the father. "I know! That's what I told her!"
