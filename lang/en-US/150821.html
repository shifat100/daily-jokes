He thought it would be a good idea to ditch work and go to the doctor's office to get his knee checked out. He went into the doctor's office and saw the place was fairly busy. When finally got to meet with the doctor, he said: > Guy: DOC! Ya gotta help me! I tripped on the pavement on the way to work and scraped my knee.
