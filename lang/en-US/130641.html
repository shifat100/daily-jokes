English (US) Joke

Lawyer goes to jail to confer with his client. Lawyer says: "Joe, I've got some good news and some bad news." Lawyer: The bad news is that they did a DNA analysis of the blood found at the crime scene and you're going to charged with triple homicide. Prisoner Joe: What's the good news? Lawyer: Your cholesterol is down to 200.
