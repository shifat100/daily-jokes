English (US) Joke

Some guys are talking about pets... They get to talking about how good big dogs are because they can make good guard dogs. One of the guys says "I preferred my old chihuahua pebbles better. And no other dog made me feel safer! He died killing a rabid full grown doberman for me!" The other guys are confused and ask how that was even possible. "The doberman choked to death."
