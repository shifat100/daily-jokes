English (US) Joke

A gambler is held at gunpoint. When the gangster asks him where his money is, the man said: "I bet that you'll miss." The gangster, confused, nonetheless shoots and surprisingly misses. Suddenly, the gangster's boss appears and gives the gambler several wads of money. The gambler smiles and says, "I told you - I bet you'd miss."
