English (US) Joke

Three men go to hell There is a white man, an Asian man, and a black man. The devil proposes that if he grabs each mans penis and it melts they stay in hell forever, if it doesn't then they get to go back to living a normal life. The devil grabs the white mans penis and it melts instantly. After a little bit of searching he then grabs the Asian mans penis and it also melts instantly. The devil, satisfied, then grabs the black mans penis but it doesn't melt. The devil screams "What is this trickery!!!" The black man says "You idiot, chocolate doesn't melt in your hands it melts in your mouth."
