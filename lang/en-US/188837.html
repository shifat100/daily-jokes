English (US) Joke

Three men are walking in a large field. Three men are walking in a large field, when they come across a horrid stench. Upon looking around, they find a small pile of dung. The first man states, "Thats some nasty bird poop." The second man laughs and says, "Nope, thats bunny poop." The third man looks at both men, and says, "Bullcrap."
