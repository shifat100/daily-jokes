English (US) Joke

Customer Support A foreign man applied for a job as an outsourced customer support representative. At the end of his interview, the company hiring him was very impressed. "Well Mujibar, we really think you'd make a great employee, we just have one more test for you to pass the interview. We need you to use the words 'green,' 'pink,' and 'yellow' all in the same sentence. He thought for a moment, and in a thick foreign accent he said "The phone goes 'green, green,' and I pink it up and say 'Yellow, this is Mujibar, how may I help you today?'"
