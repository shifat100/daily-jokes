English (US) Joke

Two old women were driving down the freeway... at 10 miles per hour, eventually the police came and pulled them over. Once the officer got up to their window he asked "Miss are you aware that you are traveling at 10 mph on the freeway?" "Why yes officer that is the speed limit after all." The officer was taken back from her statement and asked, "Where does it say the speed limit is 10 mph?" The old woman pointed up to a blue sign on the overpass with the number 10. The officer laughed inside and said "Mam that is the sign for the 10 freeway." After hearing this the second old woman looked at the driver and said "I told you we were going to fast on the 215."
