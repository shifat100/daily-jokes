English (US) Joke

my favorite joke paraphrased from an early 2000's Readers Digest: A man sits in a bar sipping his scotch after a long day at work. A beautiful brunette approaches him. "My name's Carmen" she says. "Is that a family name?" the man replies. "No, just a combination of my two favorite things, cars and men." she says with a smile,"What's yours?" A grin spreads across the man's face as he replies, "Golf tits"
