English (US) Joke

Two men get into a car wreck... Both men are all right and meet to exchange information. The older man offers the younger man a drink to calm his nerves while they wait for the insurance investigator to arrive. "Thanks for that drink, sir," the younger man says, emptying the little bit left in the bottle. "That was pretty scary." "Don't thank me," the older man replies, casually tossing the empty bottle into the woods. "I'm a lawyer."
