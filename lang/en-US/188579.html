A joke best told with an accent I've found that this joke is best told with a Cockney or Australian accent. So Jim an' Marty are sitting in Marty's livin' room, when Marty exclaims, "Oy, it's feckin' cold round ere. Jim, could you be pal, run upstairs, and fetch me slippers". Jim's says, "yeh, ol' right." and walks upstairs where he sees Marty's two sisters. Jim gets en ideer in 'is 'ead when 'e see em. He says, "Oy, Marty jes sent me up 'ere ta shag ya both". One of em is all for this, but the other is suspicious. "Ow do we know you're not lying?" she asks. Jim shrugs 'is shoulders an yells down th' stairs "OY MARTY! Wot about one of em?" Marty scream's back at th' top o his lungs "THERE AIN'T NO SENSE IN FECKIN' ONE OF THEM NOW IS THERE?!?!?"
