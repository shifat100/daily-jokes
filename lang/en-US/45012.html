English (US) Joke

A teacher asks her 2nd grade class... "Who's a Trump fan?" Not wanting to look stupid for not knowing what that meant, they all raised their hands except for Johnny. "And why aren't you a Trump fan?" she asked, used to Johnny always trying to be different. "Because I'm a Sanders fan" he replied. "And why are you a Sanders fan?" "Because mommy and daddy are" "And if mommy and daddy were idiots, what would that make you?" she asked "A Trump fan"
