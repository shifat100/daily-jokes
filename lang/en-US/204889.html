English (US) Joke

Steve Wright IV When I fly, I go Air Bizarre. It's a good airline. You buy a one-way round trip ticket. You leave any Monday and they bring you back the previous Friday... That way you still have the weekend. If you saw a heat wave, would you wave back? I broke a mirror and got seven years bad luck, but my lawyer thinks he can get me five. If the pen is mightier than the sword, in a duel I'll let you have the pen! I was cleaning out my closet and I found a swim suit that I had made out of sponges. I remember one time when I wore it. When I got out of the swimming pool nobody could go swimming until I came back. The other day, when I was walking through the woods, I saw a rabbit standing in front of a candle making shadows of people on a tree. It doesn't matter what temperature the room is; it's always room temperature. I have a very rare photograph. It shows Houdini locking his keys in his car.
