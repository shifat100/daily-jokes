The 10 minutes joke One morning, a girl was taking a shower with her mom. She asked, "Mommy, Mommy, what are those?" The Mom says "Those are titties, dear." "When will I get those?" the girl asks. "When you're older." the Mom says. The next morning, the girl is taking a shower with her dad. "Daddy, Daddy, what's that?" she asks "That's a penis, dear." he replies "When will I get that?" she asks "In ten minutes when your mom goes to work."
