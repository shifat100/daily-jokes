Three nuns were talking about their chores... Three nuns were talking about their chores. The first nun says: "I was putting away father's laundry, when i found some pornographic magazines in his drawer!" "So what did you do?" The second nun asks. "I threw them away." The first nun replies. The second nun scoffs and says she can top that. "When I was making father's bed, I found some condoms underneath his pillow!" "So what did you do?" The first nun asks. "I poked holes in all of them!" The second nun exclaims. The third nun fainted.
