Ugly Baby I was soooo ugly when I was born that my mum got morning sickness after I was born. The doctor came in to the waiting room after I was born and told my dad, "We did everything we could, but he pulled through." I've never been able to understand why my bath toys were a radio and a toaster. And when I play in the sandpit, the cat always covers me up.
