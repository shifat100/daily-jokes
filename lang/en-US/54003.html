English (US) Joke

A crowd of tourists is walking through an art gallery... The guide stops in front of a painting of three black men sitting naked on a bench. It's a normal painting in every respect, except the man on the right side has a "Caucasian" penis hanging down between his legs. The tour guide explains to the crowd how many eminent critics over the years have tried to explain the symbolism of the white penis in the painting. Some suggested it represents the "purity" of love, while others discussed how even in their manhoods, black men are considered to be oppressed compared to whites. The woman goes on discussing the merits and counterpoints of these theories while the crowd looks on fascinated. In the end she says, "But like all art, the ultimate interpretation is up to the viewer. What do you folks think the white penis symbolizes?" A little old lady at the back of the group says, "Well, when I looked at it, I just thought they were all coal miners, dirty from head to toe, but the fellow on the left when home for a nooner with his wife."
