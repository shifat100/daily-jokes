English (US) Joke

Three blind elephants examine a human being Three blind elephants came upon a human being for the first time. They gathered close and felt the strange creature with their snouts. The first blind elephant said, "A human being is thin and stands on its hind legs." The second said, "A human being is flat and mushy." The third said, "Yeah, my bad."
