English (US) Joke

Father takes his son to the pharmacy... They are walking down an aisle and the son see's a 3 pack of condoms and asks "Father what are these?" The father replies, "Well son those are for high school boys, one for Friday one for Saturday and one for Sunday." They keep on walking and the son see's a six pack and asks, "Dad this pack has six?!" The dad replies, "Yes son, those are for college boys two for Friday, two for Saturday and 2 for Sunday." They continue walking and the son see's a twelve pack. This time in shock the son says, "Dad wow these have 12 why?!" The father replies, "Well son those are for married men, 1 for January, 1 for February.....
