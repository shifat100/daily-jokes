Religious smoking joke A Franciscan and a Jesuit were friends. They were both smokers who found it difficult to pray for a long period of time without having a cigarette. They decided to go to their superiors and ask for permission to smoke. &nbsp; When they met again, the Franciscan was downcast. “I asked my superior if I could smoke while I pray and he said ‘no,’” he said. &nbsp; The Jesuit smiled. “I asked if I could pray while I smoke.
