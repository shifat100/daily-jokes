English (US) Joke

Daniel decides to open a camping supply business... A student named Daniel has just graduated college with a degree in business. His dream is to combine his love of the outdoors with his new knowledge and he decides to open a camping supply store. He decides that in order to stand out from the competition, he needs to do some things differently. He decides that at his store customers will be allowed to design their own tents and customize their camping gear. He even decides on a catchy name, "You Tent It!", a name that he feels captures his new business perfectly. He lines up investors and finds a great location right by a national park. Everything is going along great right up until the day before the grand opening. He receives an order of tent pegs that are defective. He knows that he can't sell the defective tent pegs, but he also knows that he can't delay the grand opening without angering his investors. Left with nowhere else to turn, he calls one of his old business college professors. Luckily, the professor knows someone in the tent peg industry and the professor offers to put him in touch with them. Feeling relieved, he opens the store as planned. The professor, naturally curious to see how things turned out, stops by the store on opening day. As soon as he walks in the door he sees that his contact in the tent peg industry was able to come through. The professor rushes up to Daniel and exclaims "You Tent It! Dan, you got new pegs! New pegs!"
