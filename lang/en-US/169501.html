English (US) Joke

An old lady at the clinic. Doctor: I'm afraid to tell you that you have Alzheimer's disease. Old lady: What? No way! I played poker, did math problems, and everything to prevent this... Doctor: It's OK ma'am. Just keep doing what you're doing to prevent your condition from worsening. Old lady: Sure. Doctor: I will also encourage you to write down things that may be memory intensive, and keep a daily journal. Old lady: I will. Doctor: I will see you in three week's time. Old lady: Thank you! I guess i should accept my condition. At least it's not as bad as having Alzheimer's.
