English (US) Joke

Three Army Lieutenants have to cross a river for infantry training. The first one prays to God and says "Lord give me the strength to cross this river." There is a flash of light and he is granted stronger arms to swim. The second one says "Lord give me the endurance to cross this river." Another flash of light and he is granted strong lungs to help him swim. The third one says "Lord, remove this obstacle from my path." There is a flash of light and the young lieutenant turns into a woman. Crossing a Water Obstacle is no longer part of her test and she is applauded by the media for being so strong and independent. She has a loyal group of male followers who dote on her every day and she lives happily ever after.
