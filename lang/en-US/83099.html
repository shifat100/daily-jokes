English (US) Joke

English man, Irishman and a Scotsman rob a bank. Being chased by police, they run into a factory and hide in large sacks, the police man enters the factory, sees the sacks and kicks the English man's sack, "Meow" says the English man, the cop kick the Scotsman sack, "Woof" he shouts, the police man then kicks the Iris man's sack and hears "Potato".
