English (US) Joke

A black man and a white man are arguing about whether God is black or white They decided to climb to the top of the tallest mountain and call out to God with the hopes of getting a response. "God! Are you white or black?" "I am who I am!" comes a booming response. "See," says the white guy. "He is white!" "Why would you assume that?!" asks the black guy. "He could just as easily be black!" "If he was black, he would say, 'I is who I is." Edit: Forgot a word
