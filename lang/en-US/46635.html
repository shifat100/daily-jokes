The preacher and the SJW in the town square A preacher is standing on a soapbox preaching in the middle of the town square, "I don't believe that women should have any rights, and that gays should be hanged". A young SJW in the crowd has finally had enough and walks up to him and exclaims, "I can't believe what a primitive asshole you are, you must be a Republican!". He pauses for a moment and responds, "Nope, these are my religious beliefs, I'm a muslim from Saudi Arabia". She quickly replies: "Oh I'm so sorry, I hope you don't think I'm Islamophobic"
