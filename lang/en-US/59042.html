English (US) Joke

A stranded guy in a deserted island Alone in the whole island with only a goat as company. After a few days the guy gets horny and try to bang the goat but fails to catch it every time. For several months keeps trying but never catch it. One day He finds a beautiful girl from a shipwreck drawning in the water and saves her. The girl verythankful says to Him:thanks for saving me, I will do anything you want. He says smiling: will you help me catch the goat?
