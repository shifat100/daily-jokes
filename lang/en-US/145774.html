How to play "Future You" FUTURE YOU How to play "Future You" (You'll have to be over 25 to play this game) Go to a play park, or a fair ground or a school or anywhere you might find children. Find a child who looks enough like you. Go up to that child when they're alone and say "I am you from the future, those people are not your parents, I'm sorry but your real parents were dead long ago... Now listen carefully, you need to become me. Become the super spy the world needs! You have to get to Mexico, forget about everyone you know, they've all been placed here by the shadow man to stop you by any means. Just get to Mexico, you will meet who you need to meet when you get there, trust me. Don't look back just go, GO NOW!" This game can also be played with more players, where whoever had the kid causing the longest news story, wins...
