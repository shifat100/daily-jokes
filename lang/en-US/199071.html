English (US) Joke

Dear White Fella... Couple of things you oughta know Firstly, when I'm born, I'm black!! When I grow up, I'm black When I get sick, I'm black When I go out in the sun, I'm black When I'm cold, I'm black And when I get scared, Gee, I'm black And When I die, I'm still black But you white fella... When you born, you pink When you grow up, you white When you get sick, you green When you go out in the sun, you red When you get cold, you go blue When you get scared, you yellow And when you die, you purple And YOU got the cheek to call ME coloured??!!
