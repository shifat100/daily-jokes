The Christmas Party A waitress at a restaurant that David worked at had a change of clothes stolen from the break room. To make matters worse, she'd planned on wearing them to the Christmas party. As a brand-new employee, David knew none of this backstory, so you can imagine his surprise when the found a note on the employees' community board saying: "It has been two weeks since the Christmas party, and I still have not found my clothes."
