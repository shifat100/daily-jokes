English (US) Joke

An old lady walks into a market and asks the vendor for some broccoli... the vendor responds: "we don't have any broccoli, but we have some tomatoes" the old lady responds: "yes..yes..I'd like some broccoli please. Thank you!" the vendor responds: "I'm sorry lady, I told you we don't carry broccoli, we have some carrots if you'd like" lady: "Oh thank you..I'd love some broccoli!" the vendor, clearly irritated, responds: "Lady, can you say no, as in nose.." lady:"oh yes, sure..nose..." vendor:"can you say red, as in reddit.." lady:"ok..reddit" vendor:"can you say fuck.. as in broccoli.." lady:"wait..but there's no fuck in broccoli..." vendor:"THERE'S NO FUCKIN BROCCOLI YOU DUMB BITCH!!!" jokes a lot better when you tell it in person :)
