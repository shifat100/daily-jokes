English (US) Joke

[long]A white man says to a colored man... A white man says to a colored man next to him, "Colored people are not allowed here, " The man next to him with a brown colored skin tone turned around and stood up. He then said: "Now listen here...when I was born I was BROWN, when I grew up I was BROWN, when I'm sick I'm BROWN, when I go in the sun I'm BROWN, when I'm cold I'm BROWN, and when I die I'll be BROWN." "But you sir...when you're born you're PINK, when you grow up you're WHITE, when you're sick you're GREEN, when you go in the sun you turn RED, when you're cold you turn BLUE and when you die you turn PURPLE." "And you have the nerve to call me colored?"
