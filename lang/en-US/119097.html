English (US) Joke

A man sees a skunk on his way home He picks it up and takes it home, when he gets home he puts it under the bed. His wife walks in and then she says "what are you doing?" and he says "Putting a skunk under the bed." and she says "Well what about the stink?" and he said if "I could deal with it for 15 years so can the skunk." -My Cousins Grandpa
