English (US) Joke

House of ill repute In the 1920's a man walks into a house of ill repute. He tells the guy inside that he wants the best thing he can get for two dollars. The man tells him to go up stairs and go into the first door on the right, and he'll know what to do. The man goes, and there's a chicken in the room and a large mirror on the wall. So the man screws the chicken. The next day, not feeling satisfied with the experience he goes back and tells the man he wants his money back. The man says he can't do that, but he'll give him something better for free. This time go into the second door on your right. He walks in and there's a few other men in the room looking through a two way mirror watching a crazy orgy. So the man says, "wow this is incredible." One of the other men in the room says, "you should have been here yesterday, there was a guy fuckin a chicken."
