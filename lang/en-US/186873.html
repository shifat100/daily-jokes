English (US) Joke

Gladiator's Monday A gladiator was having a rough Monday at the arena. His opponent had sliced off both of his arms. Nevertheless, he fought on, kicking and biting as furiously as he could. But when his opponent lopped off both of his feet, the gladiator had no choice but to give up. He was now both unarmed and defeated.
