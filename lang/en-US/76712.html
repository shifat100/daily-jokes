English (US) Joke

A blonde is trying to put together a puzzle She simply cannot figure out how to do it, so she calls her boyfriend. He asks her: "What is the puzzle is supposed to look like when finished?" and she replies, " it's supposed to look like a tiger." He drives to her house, and when he gets there, he begins laughing hysterically. "Why are you laughing?" She asks. "These are Frosted Flakes."
