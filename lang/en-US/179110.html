English (US) Joke

A man is waiting in line and sees another man... He says, "excuse me sir, are you an attorney?" The other man says, "yes. I am." The first man says, "what is your fee?" The attorney says, "$400 per four questions." The man says, "isn't that terribly expensive?" "yes." says the attorney. "what is your fourth question?"
