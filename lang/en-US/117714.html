ONE, TWO, THREE, HUHHH!" The next morning our poor midget heads down to the hotel lobby and spies his friend there. His friend says to him; "So, have any luck last night?" Our poor friend sighs "No, it was awful, I just couldn't get in the mood! I got no sex and paid the hooker anyway. Still it sounded like you had an amazing time!" To which his friend replies; "What do you mean? I couldn't even get on the bed!"
