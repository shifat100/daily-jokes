When you become a dad A proud new dad sits down to have a drink with his father "Well son, now that you've got kids of your own, I think it's time I give you this" "Dad you don't mean..." "Yes son, I do" Dad pulls out a copy of 1001 dad jokes, 5th edition "Dad I'm honoured...", he says, tears sparkling in his eyes. "Hi honoured", replies his father, "I'm dad".
