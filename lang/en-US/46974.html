English (US) Joke

An illiterate Father went camping with his highly educated Son... As they walk through the wildness the son boasts about how being educated makes him appreciate nature, evolution, etc. To which the father just shrugs and continues along. When they reach their camping site, they set-up their tent and fell asleep. Unable to sleep properly, the father wakes up his son after few hours. "Son, what do you see when you look up in the sky?" "I see millions, perhaps billions of stars" "And what does that mean, Son?" "Well - many of them are probably galaxies that can house a million earth like planets. In the grand scheme of things it just makes us in this tiny moat of dust called earth seem insignifi..." The father just then hit the son in his head with a pan "Idiot! it means someone stole out tent!!"
