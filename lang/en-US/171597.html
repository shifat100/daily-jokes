English (US) Joke

A man sits at a bar... ...After the third beer he decides to go home. He tries to walk out, but as soon as he is off his chair he falls on the ground. He figures he's probably not drunk enough. He orders another one, tries to go, but again falls on the ground. After two more beers and tries he decides just to crawl home. The next morning his wife says to him in the bed. "You were in the bar last night." "How did you know?" "The bartender called. You forgot your wheelchair."
