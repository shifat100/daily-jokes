English (US) Joke

Three pregnant women are knitting sweaters for their babies at the OB/GYN waiting room. Three pregnant women are knitting sweaters for their babies at the OB/GYN waiting room. The first one takes a pill out of her purse and says, "I want my baby to have a strong nervous system, so I'm taking a folate pill." The second one takes a pill out of her purse and says, "I want my baby to have healthy blood, so I'm taking an iron pill." The third one takes a pill out of her purse and says, "This is thalidomide." The other two women look in horror. "WHY?!" The third one calmly replies, "I can't knit sleeves for shit."
