English (US) Joke

A cow and a jackass (donkey) are eating from a trough A cow and a jackass (donkey) are eating from a trough on a ranch in Mexico. Jackass: Cow, I overheard you were going to be killed tomorrow! Cow: Yes, I've heard. Jackass: What do you plan do about it? Cow: I'm going to escape to India! They worship cows in India! Jackass: Well, that's a great idea! Cow: You'll be killed, too! What do you plan to do about it? Jackass: Well, I plan on going to America! Cow: America? Why? Jackass: Well, I heard through the grapevine they'll let a jackass be president!
