English (US) Joke

Two girls move into a dorm, one from Georgia and the other from Connecticut... The girl from GA arrives to the dorm room and finds the CT girl with her parents hanging drapes. The GA girl says "Hi, y'all! Where y'all from?" The CT girl, in a snobbish northeast attitude replies, "We're from a place where we don't end sentences in prepositions." The GA smiles politely and responds, "Beg my pardon. Where y'all from...cunt?" Props to House of Cards for this joke, although I'm sure it's been done before.
