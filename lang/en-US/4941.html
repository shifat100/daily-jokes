English (US) Joke

So a man is on a cruise... That's sponsored by the Democratic party for a fundraiser. He's walking around enjoying his time when he comes across a group of people watching a game of limbo. He sees that the crowd is really enjoying watching the game, so he hops in. When it's his turn to go under, he ducks waaayy under the bar as a show off to the crowd. As he expected, the crowd loves it and starts cheering and shouting. On his next turn, the man ducks even further under the bar than he did before, and the crowd goes wild! The man, excited by the crowd, shouts "Oh yeah? How about this?!" And grabs the bar and puts it on the very bottom peg and slides under with ease. To his surprise however, everyone in the crowd becomes enraged and starts throwing their drinks and appetizers at the man. The man, soaked from head to toe goes to the DJ and asks "what the hell was that?!" The DJ turns to the man and says, "well you lowered the bar sooo much that they thought you were Donald Trump"
