English (US) Joke

A woman goes to her doctor and as he is examining her, she asked him if her vagina was bigger than normal. The doctor said that it was but he could give her an exercise to tighten it up. He told her how to do the exercise and told her that she should squat naked on a mirror so she can see what she is doing and to make sure she is doing it right. Once she got home she wanted to start the exercises as soon as possible. She got a large mirror and put it on the floor and after removing all her clothes she got on it to start the exercises. In the middle of her efforts her husband came into the room and asked her what she was doing. She told him she was doing some special exercises. The husband said "That great dear but be sure not to fall into that big hole."
