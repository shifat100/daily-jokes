English (US) Joke

A black guy walks into a convenience store... ...and he buys a box of paper towels. He goes to cash it out and it comes up to $2.99. He sees the nametag on the cashier's shirt says "Arnold." "Hey Arnold, sorry, I've only got a $20 dollar bill for this, haha." "No problem" Arnold replies as he rings him up. Arnold hands him a $5 dollar bill as change. "I think the change should be $17.01, no?" He asks. "No, you're mistaken," Arnold replies. "I'm Arnold Schwarzenegger".
