English (US) Joke

You want to know what's ironic? That the CEO of a company does no work for 10 years and everybody hates them, but the moment they go on 'Undercover Boss' and give away a small sum of their yearly earnings to save face, the same people who hated them with a passion now love them so much. Either those employees are suck-up brown-noses or money really changed their minds that much!
