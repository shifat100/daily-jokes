English (US) Joke

A guy is flying to Hawaii... He's unsure of the pronunciation, not sure if its "Hawaii" or "Havaii", so when he gets off of the plane, he looks for a local. He finds a guy that appears to be a local and asks him, "is it pronounced 'Hawaii' or 'Havaii'?" The local responds "Havaii!" The traveler then says thank you, that he was really confused and the local really cleared things up and the local replies "you're velcome!"
