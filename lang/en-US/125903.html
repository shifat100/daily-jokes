English (US) Joke

One time I took a blonde girl to the movies... We bought our tickets and waited in line for snacks. I got popcorn; she got M&M's. We got a drink to split. We sat down during the previews. I started eating my popcorn and she opened her M&M's and dumped them all out in her lap. She carefully separated them all by color, took all the brown ones, and threw them in the trash. "What was that about?" I asked as she returned to her seat. She smiled and replied "Oh, I'm allergic to chocolate so I always throw the chocolate flavored ones away."
