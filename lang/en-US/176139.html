English (US) Joke

Too Tense A patient walks into his therapist's practice. Instinctively, the therapist asks, "How are you feeling? What is the problem you'd like me to address?" The patient composes himself before saying, "Sometimes I dream I'm a wigwam and other times I'm a teepee. Sometimes wigwam, sometimes teepee. Teepee, wigwam, teepee, wigwam, teepee, wigwam. I don't know what to do!" The therapist brushes this off and says, I know what's wrong! You're *two tents*!"
