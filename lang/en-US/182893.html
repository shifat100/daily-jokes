English (US) Joke

A mexican guy, a black guy, and a white guy are all walking down a beach when they find a magic lamp... So they rubbed it and a genie pops out, tells them they get 1 wish each. The mexican guy goes 1st and says: "I wish that my homeland is rid of all hardships so all my people in the USA can move peacefully back to Mexico." and poof! The wish is granted. The black guy goes next and says: "I wish that my homeland is rid of all hardships so all my people in the USA can move peacefully back to Africa." and poof! The wish is granted. Last up is the white guy, he asks the genie: "with their wishes, all the Mexicans and blacks are back to their home lands?" The genie replies: "yes, they all are." The white guy then says: "I'll have a coke please."
