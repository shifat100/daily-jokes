English (US) Joke

A Scotsman is tending his flock of sheep... (long) when he decides to take a nap under a nearby tree. After he falls asleep, a young woman walking on a nearby road decides to play a joke on him. She lifts up his kilt, takes a ribbon from her hair, ties it around his manhood, and leaves with a giggle. After awhile the Scotsman wakes up and walks over by the bushes to take a wee. He lifts up his kilt and is amazed to see a bright blue ribbon tied around his manhood. without skipping a beat he says "well I don't know where you've been laddie, but I can see you won first prize!"
