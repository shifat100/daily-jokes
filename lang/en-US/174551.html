English (US) Joke

Fluctuations.. So a Japanese woman goes to a bank to exchange some yen for US dollars. She hands over 1000 yen and the bank teller returns $10. The next week, she does the same, only this time the teller returns $9.50. The woman, puzzled, says, "but last week, you gave me $10 for 1000 yen..?" The teller shrugs his shoulders and says, "Fluctuations." The woman says, "Well fuck you white people too!"
