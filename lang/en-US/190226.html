English (US) Joke

Gong clock A guy and his friend stumble into his apartment late at night. The friend wonders what time it is, but when he looks over at the night-table where a clock would be, he sees a large gong. "Dude, why do you have a gong instead of a clock?" The guy says "Oh, here", and takes a mallet and whacks the gong really hard. "DON'T YOU FUCKING IDIOTS KNOW ITS 3 AM?!"
