English (US) Joke

Two priests are putting a sign... ... on which says "The end is near! Turn around until it's too late!". The next moment a car storms right pass them and a man inside a car yells: "Leave people alone, you fucking religion fanatics!". In the next moment you can hear screeching of wheels and then a strong bang. One priest turns around to the other and says: "Don't you think, maybe we should just shorten it down and write "Bridge is down!"."
