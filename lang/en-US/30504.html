English (US) Joke

A white man tells a black man Why do people call you color man ?? To what the black man answers " I don't know When I was born; I was black. When I started to grow, I was black. When I go to the beach I'm black. When I have a cold I'm still black. When I have panic I'm black. When I'm sick I'm black. even when I die I continued to be black. Instead you my friend When you're born you're pink. When you start to grow you are white. When you go to the beach you look red. When you're cold you look blue. When you have panic you look yellow.
