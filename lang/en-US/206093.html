Here's a Clue A couple who lived together were talking one day, trying to figure out how to entertain themselves on a rainy day. "Let's play Hide and Seek," said the woman. "I'll hide and if you find me we'll have sex!" "But what if I can't find you?" asked her boyfriend. "I'll be behind the piano," she said.
