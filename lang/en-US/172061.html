English (US) Joke

A man in overalls walks into the emergency room... A man in overalls walks into the emergency room with the tips of his fingers missing. "What happened to you?" Asked the Doctor as he began stitching him up. "Well," the man replied, "I had to trim my bushes today, and I thought of a way to speed it up. Instead of using clippers, I held my lawn mower above the bushes. But then the blade lopped off the tips of my fingers underneath." The doctor shook his head, finished the job, and sent the man home. Ten minutes later another man walks into the emergency room, also missing all of his fingertips. "What happened to you?" Asked the doctor. "I was driving down the road, and I saw somebody with to best idea of how to trim their bushes."
