English (US) Joke

Vladislav There was once a man named Vladislav. He worked an average job, received minimum wage, and was frustrated with his life. One day he received a call from his wife, who told him out of guilt that she had been cheating on him for several months now, and wanted to stop by the house to collect her belongings before she left him. Upon reaching home, the wife saw Vlad sitting on a couch, staring at the wall. "Vladislav..." He looks at her and says with an unmoving expression: "Baby, don't hurt me."
