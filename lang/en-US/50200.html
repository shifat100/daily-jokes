English (US) Joke

4 hikers are walking through the woods... When suddenly the one in front stops. "Oh god, I think that's a pile of bear shit." He says, pointing towards the steaming pile of crap lying in the middle of the path. "Mean, it *looks* like bear shit..." He says Then the second hiker steps up and leans over the pile of crap to inhale some of the fumes. "Yeah, it *smells* like bear shit." Then the third hiker steps up and picks up the pile of crap. He molds around in his bare hands for a while as the other stare at him. Finally, he puts it back down and says "It certainly *feels* like bear shit." Then the fourth hiker steps up and grabs a fistful of it and puts it in his mouth. The others watch as he chews it for a while, thinking about the taste. Finally he swallows and says "I guess it *tastes* like bear shit." Then the first hiker piped up again "Alright, it's *propbably* beat shit." He says, looking down at what's left of the pile. "So make sure not to step in it!"
