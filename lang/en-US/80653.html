English (US) Joke

A man was driving his car in a remote village and looses control of his vehicle and crashes, a villager with a horse approaches him for help. Villager yells to his horse "yo Billy pull". The horse doesn't move at all. Villager yells again "Go Bob Go". Still no action from the horse. then finally he says "Yo Misty you better start pulling" and the horse starts pulling the car. The man thanks the villager but is curious to know how this villager does not know the name of his horse. The villager: "Oh yeah of course I do, but the thing is that he is blind and if he knew he was dragging all by himself he wouldn't bother"
