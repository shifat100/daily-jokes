So I was teaching my grandpa how to browse Reddit... So I was teaching my grandpa how to browse Reddit on his own so he could kill time by having fun. I told him about /history and /news because I thought he might like it. I also made him his own account named PLS_DONT_PM_ME_BOOBS for obvious reasons. He seemed happy so I went to my own room.
