I Hate My Life I hate my life. I'm 22 and work at a fast food restaurant where my coworker hates me with a burning passion. I'm extremely underpaid and if I ask for a raise my boss will kill me. I hate my town and the people in it. But I can't leave because I don't have a license. And do you want to hear the worst part? I live in a pineapple under the sea.
