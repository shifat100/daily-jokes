English (US) Joke

A man has lost his wife... ...in a shopping mall. He looks all over for her, but she's nowhere to be found. He asks a guard for some help. "What does she look like?" The guard asks. "Well she's short, about 5'2". She has short, brown hair. She's wearing jeans and a striped top. Last I saw she had a Yankee Candle bag. Please, I'm fearing the worst here." "We'll do whatever we can to find her, sir." Suddenly, a tall blonde woman walks out of a store. She's wearing a polka dot dress, and holding a Bath and Body Works bag. Guy says "There you are!"
