English (US) Joke

Guess your Blood Type (OC) A son one day is curious about his blood type. He asks his dad, "Dad, what's my blood type?" The Dad says, "Son, please guess" The son says, "Am I type AB?" The Dad replies, "Well son, close, your Mom is type A, I am Type B, but you are O - dopted!"
