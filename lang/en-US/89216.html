English (US) Joke

Sheikh was talking to his travel agent.... Sheikh: I am about ready for a vacation. Only this year, I am going to do it a little differently.... The last few years, I have been taking your advice on where to go.... Three years ago you said go to Hawaii. I went to Hawaii and spent some days and my wife Razia got pregnant..... Then two years ago, you told me to enjoy Bahamas, and Razia got pregnant again.... Last year you suggested Tahiti and Razia once again got pregnant..... Travel agent: So, what are you going to do this year that is different?.... Sheikh replied: This year I'm taking Razia with me :-)
