English (US) Joke

Sir, taste my soup please. A man walks into a five star restaurant with high hopes. He orders a $20 soup meal and waits patiently. After about five minutes, the waiter bring his soup out, lays it on the table and walks away without a word. The man is infuriated, and stand to his feet and tells the waiter to return to his table for a moment. "Sir, taste my soup please." He says. "I cannot do that sir, you are our guest and it is against company policy to taste a customers food." The prestigious waiter says. "Im telling you right now to taste it." "Sir I am again sorry but I cannot ta-" "Taste the soup or I will call up every person I know to write the worst review on this restaurant and get you fired." He interrupts, sternly. "Alright I see. I will taste the soup.... Wait a minute sir, I can't taste the soup if you don't have a spoon." "No shit."
