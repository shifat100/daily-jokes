English (US) Joke

So I had a friend with a stutter. She told me that she has trouble dating. Curious, I asked her why. She said that she keeps putting out on the first date. When I asked why she tells me this; "Well whenever things get heated, I try to tell them that I'm not that type of girl. Unfortunately, by the time I'm done telling them that, I am."
