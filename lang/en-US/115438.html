English (US) Joke

Mr. Isaac's Fourth Grade Class Mr. Isaac’s fourth grade class was struggling in Social Studies. During their Civil War unit, he decided to try something to spice up the boring curriculum that was causing the class to fail. He spent all night working on his presentation. He hand sewed a Jefferson Davis costume, and even practiced his accent. The next morning, he started off class as “Mr. Davis, a real-life veteran of the Civil War.” He launched into his presentation flawlessly, and the kids loved it! He told them all of the events of the war and the Presidents of the North and South. The next evening, as he was grading papers, he realized woefully that he actually had not focused clearly enough on Davis’s life, as they had all missed vital quiz questions about him. The class however, did ace all the Lincoln questions. Overall, they passed the quiz and brought their grades up. His presentation was Abe lesson in disguise.
