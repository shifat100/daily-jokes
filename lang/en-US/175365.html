English (US) Joke

A grandfather and grandson go to a shopping mall... All of a sudden the grandson turns around and looks around and realizes he can't find his grandpa. A security guard comes up to this little boy and asks... "Can I help you boy?" The little boy reply's "I cant find my grandpa!" So then the security guard asks "Well, whats his name?" "Um, GRANDPA" So then the guard ask's, "OK, Well, whats he like?" The little boy stands there and thinks for a few seconds and says "Crown Royal Whisky, and ladies with big tits." My Grandma told me this joke a few years ago, it's my favorite!
