English (US) Joke

A married woman goes to the doctor and says: "Doctor, I hear voices inside my head!" Doctor: "what do the voices say?" Woman: "they say 'kill your husband" Doctor: "Ok, that's not good. I'm going to start you immediately on a psychiatric medication. Come back and see me in one week." One week later, the woman goes back to the same doctor and says: "I'm still hearing the same voices." Doctor: "what are they saying now?" Woman: "they're saying 'beat your husband with a club" Doctor: "Hmm, I'm going to increase the dosage of your current medication. Come back and see me in two weeks." Two weeks later, the woman goes back to the doctor and says: "Doctor, now I hear only one voice but it has a slightly different tone" Doctor: "What does the voice say?" Woman: "It says 'why did you ever marry him?" Doctor: "Excellent! you're back to normal. come back and see me in a year."
