English (US) Joke

Life is all about how we see, interpret & accept things A very rich man took his son to a village to show what **poverty** is all about : After the trip, he asked his son about **poverty...** The son replied : We have 1 dog, they had 4... We have a small pool, they have a long river... We've lamps, they've stars... We've small piece of land, they've large fields... We buy food, they grow theirs & eat fresh... They have friends to play, we don't have friends, we have to play with Computers & PS... They have happiness, we have only money... Their fathers have time for their childrens & our father don't have... The boy's dad was speechless... Then boy said ''thanks dad for showing me how poor we are..." **Life is all about how we see, interpret & accept things..!** Awesome...!!
