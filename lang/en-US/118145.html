Are You A Doctor? Are You A Doctor? A Boy Swallows 3 Coin While Playing. Father See This And Starts Beating On Boy’s Back. Boy Vomit 2 Coins But One Was Still Inside. Father Shouts: “Help! Help! Help!” A Woman In A Business Suit Saw It & Comes To Them. She Pulls Down Boy’s Pants, Hold His Testicles, Squeezes & Twist Them Gently Then Tighter. I Am With Bank Loan Recovery Department.“
