I heard reddit likes Native American jokes Two guys are driving their pickup truck down some dusty old range roads when of on the side of the road is some Indian walking along. "let's run him down!" exclaims one of the men so they swerve to the side and knock the indian over, sending him rolling down the steep ditch and out of sight. "Look, another dumb indian!" the guys say as the speed up and hit him head on, sending the indian flying through the windshield. Back at the police station they are being interviewed by the cops and the driver asks "well what are the charges officer?" "Well" says the cop, "the first one was charged with fleeing the scene of a crime and the second one was charged with breaking and entering"
