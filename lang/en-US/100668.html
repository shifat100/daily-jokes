English (US) Joke

A rabbit and a bear are walking down a road when they found a genie's lamp. After rubbing it, a genie popped out and told them that they each get three wishes. "I want every woman in the world," the bear asked first. " I want a helmet," said the rabbit. " Actually, I wish for every attractive female in the universe," the bear said for his second wish. "I also want a motorcycle," asked the rabbit. " No, I want every beautiful women that had ever existed," the bear said for his final wish. The rabbit, already revving up his motorcycle, added: "I wish the bear was gay," and drove off.
