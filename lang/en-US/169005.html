No more lies A father just came back home after buying a robot. This robot slaps people when they are lieing. The father walks up to his son and asks: "Where were you last night?" Son: "I was just playing basketball on the streets" *The robot slaps the son* Son: "Okay okay! I was over at a friends house" Father: "What were you doing over there?" Son: "I was just watching a movie about Spiderman" *The robot slaps the son* Son: "Okay okay! It was a porn!" Father: "Wow son, that's not what I did at your age!" *The robot slaps the father* Mother: "Haha, after all, he is your son" *The robot slaps the mother*
