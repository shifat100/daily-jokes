English (US) Joke

So these two guys are out driving One day these two guys are out driving around in their pick-up truck with a pig in the back seat sticking it's head out of the window. A cop sees the two men and the pig and pulls them over. "You fellas should take that pig to the zoo they don't belong around here as pets" The guys agree that it's a great idea and so the cop leaves and the guys head off with their pig on the way to the zoo. The next day the cop sees the same guys driving around, and they still have that pig in their car. The cop pulls them over again and says "I thought I told you boys to take that pig to the zoo?" The boys respond with "We did! It was great and the pig loved it! Today we're taking him to the movies."
