Motel Deal My wife and I were travelling cross country when we stopped in a tiny town for the night. There was only one motel. I walked into the office and asked the clerk, "How much for one night?" He answered, "Sixty dollars." "Sixty dollars?" I shouted. "Don't you have anything cheaper?" "Sure," he said. "You can get a room for half price if you don't mind making your own bed." I agreed that this was fine. So they put us in a room with a pile of lumber and a hammer and some nails...
