English (US) Joke

An Afghan, upon landing at Islamabad Airport, introduced himself to a Pakistan Immigration Officer, as Ex-Minister of Ports & Shipping of Afghanistan. The surprised Pakistani Officer asked:"But there is no sea in Afghanistan. How can you be the Ex-Minister of Ports & Shipping.?" The Afghan replied: "Wallah ya Habibi, don't you have a Minister for Law & Justice in Pakistan.?"
