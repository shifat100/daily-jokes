English (US) Joke

A man was contemplating suicide on a bridge. A man was contemplating suicide on a bridge when he looked down and saw a little man with no arms dancing. This cheered him up and made him change his mind about suicide. When he got down off the bridge he approached the little man to thank him. "Thank you, I was going to jump off the bridge until I noticed you dancing. If you can find the good in life even with no arms, I can do the same" "Dancing?" Said the little man. "I wasn't dancing, me arse is itchy and I can't scratch it!"
