English (US) Joke

The Biter Bit "So," Jane asked the detective she had hired. "Did you trail my husband?" "Yes, ma'am, I did. I followed him to a bar, to an out-of-the-way restaurant and then to an apartment." A big smile crossed Jane's face. "Aha! I've got him!" she said gloating, "Is there any doubt what he was doing?" "No ma'am," replied the sleuth, "It's pretty clear that he was following you."
