Sound Advice At a local coffee bar, a young woman was expounding on her idea of the perfect mate to some of her friends. "The man I marry must be a shining light amongst company. He must be musical, tell jokes, sing - and stay home at night!" An old granny overheard and spoke up, "Honey, if that's all you want, get a television!"
