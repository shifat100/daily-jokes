English (US) Joke

Insane Asylum was getting full So the head director though of a brilliant plan. He would draw a door on a wall and tell the patients that their free to go once they get pass that door. That afternoon, he drew the wall and told the patients about it, then waited. Every hour or so someone would walk up to the "door" and would try to open it with no luck. The director then notice that this particular patient never once walked up to the door and tried to open it. He then confronted the man and ask why didn't he try to go out? The patient replied, "look at all those idiots trying to open that door, they don't know that I have the key in my pocket".
