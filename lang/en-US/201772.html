English (US) Joke

Shakespeare Many authors are disputing who wrote the Shakespeare plays. They each say they did. In their arguing, they decide to try and prove who wrote it by first showing Shakespeare's work, then theirs. Shakespeare version: TITANIA to BOTTOM Thou art as wise as thou art beautiful. Jack London's version: TITANIA to BOTTOM You're as wise as the snowy owl and as beautiful as the clear night sky with fresh white snow under the Northern lights. Dr. Seuss' version: TITANIA to BOTTOM You're as attractive as green eggs on a plate and the way you sing is fantastically great! Homer's version: TITANIA to BOTTOM Be you as wise as Odysseus and as beautiful as fair Helen. Guess who won.
