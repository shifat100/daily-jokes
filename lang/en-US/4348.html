English (US) Joke

An elderly man is out playing golf with a friend... An elderly man is out playing golf with a friend. During his turn, right before his swing, a funeral procession drives by along the border fence. The elderly man pauses, takes off his hat, and places it on his chest. As the procession drives along, the elderly mans friend stands just flabbergasted. Once the procession passes, the elderly man resumes setting up his swing. "Now hold on a second!" The elderly mans friend shouts, "That was quite a show of respect! I have never seen anything like it!" "Well, I had to do something." "Why's that?" Replied the elderly mans friend. "I was married to her for 40 years!"
