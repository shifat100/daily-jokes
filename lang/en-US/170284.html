A man walks into a bar and orders a triple scotch A man walks into a bar and orders a triple scotch. The bartender pours him his drink and asks, "What happened?" "I came home early from work today...and found my wife having sex with my best friend" The bartender pours him a second drink - "That's awful, what did you say to your wife?" "I told her we were through. 'Pack your bags, and leave!'" "What about your friend?" "I waved my finger at him, looked him straight in the eye, and told him...bad dog."
