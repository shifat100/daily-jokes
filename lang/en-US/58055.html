English (US) Joke

A little boy and an old man are sitting on a park bench... ...The little boy unwraps a candy bar and eats it. Then he eats a second one. And a third, fourth and fifth. The old man, watching this, says "You shouldn't be eating so many candy bars. You will ruin your teeth and get fat." The little boy responded, "My Grandfather lived to be 102 years old." The old man replied "Did your Grandfather eat 5 candy bars every day?" The little boy said, "No, he minded his own fucking business."
