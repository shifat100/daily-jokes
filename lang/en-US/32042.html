Black Sheep Joke *I DID NOT MAKE THIS JOKE UP. I HEARD IT FROM A FRIEND.* A missionary is sent to Africa to teach an African tribe about the Christian ways of life. He teaches them about Jesus, the Bible, and sin. He especially teaches them about the evils of sexually sin. "Thou shall not commit adultery or fornicate before marriage.", he tells them. Later on, the wife of the tribe's chief gives birth to a white baby. The tribe threatened to kill the man for this. "No, no" the man says, "This is just a natural occurrence. The baby was born Albino. You see that herd of sheep, they are all white, but yet, there is a black sheep among them." The chief leans over to the missionary and says, "I won't tell about the white baby if you don't tell about the black sheep."
