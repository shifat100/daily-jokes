English (US) Joke

Einstein, Newton and Pascal are playing hide and seek. It's Einstein's turn to seek, so he closes his eyes and counts starts counting to ten. "1, 2, 3..." Pascal runs away to hide. Newton makes a metre by metre square on the ground with a stick and stands on it. Einstein finishes counting, opens his eyes and sees Newton standing in front of him. "You're it Newton. I found you!!" Einstein said. To which Newton replies "No you didn't. You found Pascal".
