When the guests arrives you'll welcome them and I'll go to the kitchen and drop one utensil and then you'll say "what happen" . Then I'll say "oh no!! I dropped the chicken " . Then again drop another utensil and say "I dropped the spaghetti. Now we only left with green beans." *Guest arrives* Wife: Welcome. Please make yourself comfortable. * loud sound comes from the kitchen * Wife: Everything alright, honey? Husband: Sh**t. I dropped the beans.
