English (US) Joke

Epilepsy epidemic [OC] A recent epidemic of epilepsy is spreading throughout the US. Obama decides to hire a known epilepsy expert sight unseen to head up a federal effort. Convening a panel the next week, a woman enters the room and sits down. Obama leans over to his aide sitting beside him "Who is this person? I haven't seen her before." The aide replies "She's your seizure czar."
