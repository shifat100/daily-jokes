English (US) Joke

So me and my paedophile friends have a weekly gathering... Every week one of us brings a talent down the pub to show the others - this time it was my turn. I brought along my guitar and after some Dutch courage I began to play. Within a few seconds of starting the guys started cheering me on, one of them was even weeping, saying how amazing the song was. I had no idea what the big deal was, I was just fingering A minor.
