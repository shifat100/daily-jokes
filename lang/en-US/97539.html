English (US) Joke

A kid goes to a party... A kid goes to party… When he arrives, he is immediately stopped at the front door by a large group of people who, like him, are all trying to get inside. He can hear the music so he waits, thinking the party just started. Well, ten minutes go by and nothing really changes. Everyone seems to be standing in the same place. Now frustrated, he leaves and attempts to sneak in through the backdoor, but yet again… there is another damn crowd of kids blocking the doorway. Another ten minutes go by… followed by another… he tries to squeeze his way through but no one will budge, not even an inch. After waiting over forty-five minutes he finally has enough. He can’t take it anymore. He screams at the top of his lungs: “WHAT THE HELL IS EVERYONE WAITING FOR?!?!” The kid in front of him turns and says: “This is the punch line, dude.”
