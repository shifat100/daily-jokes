English (US) Joke

Taxidermy A man walks into a bar down in Alabama and orders a beer. The whole bar is looking at the man curiously as he sits down. The bartender looks at his nice suit and tie and asks him if he's from around here. The man replies, "No sir, I'm from Pennslyvania." The bartender asks the man what he does for a living up in Pennyslyvania. The man replies, "I am a Taxidermist." The bartender slowly asks, "What is a Taxidermist?" The man answers back, "Oh, I just mount animals." The bartender grins wide and yells to the whole bar, "It's OK Boys!! He's one of us!"
