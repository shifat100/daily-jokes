English (US) Joke

Two hunters are out hunting Suddenly, they see a bear racing towards them. They start running, but all of a sudden one of them stops and starts to take off his hunting boots and put on running shoes. The other hunters says to him: "What are you doing? Those will not make you run faster than the bear." The first hunter replies calmly: "No, but they will make run faster than you."
