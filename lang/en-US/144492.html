English (US) Joke

A missionary goes to Africa... A missionary goes to Africa and was living with a tribe. While he was there, a white baby was born. The tribe's chief goes to the missionary and asks, "how do you explain this white baby? You're the only white man here, this doesn't seem right...". The missionary looks around and tries to come up with an excuse. "Uhh well, you see... Uhm...see the sheep there? All of them have white wool, except for that one that's black... Its just nature." The chief looks at him then says " oh I see how it is... If I don't tell you won't tell..."
