English (US) Joke

Its the Christians vs Muslims football game... and the Christians score a goal. From his seat in heaven, surrounded by angels, God cheers. after a while the Muslims score a goal. Again God cheers. The angels are now confused... "Whose side are you on, Lord?", they ask. "Niether", replies God, "I am just enjoying the game." (modified from Catholics vs Protestants)
