English (US) Joke

An 80 year old lady gets married for the 4th time. This time to a funeral director. The local paper does a story on her and they ask her about her previous marriages. She tells them that at 20 she married a bank manager, at 40 a ringmaster, at 60 a pastor and at 80 a funeral director. The reporter asks her why? 1 for the money, 2 for the show, 3 to get ready and 4 to go.
