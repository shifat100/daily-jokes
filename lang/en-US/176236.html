English (US) Joke

Buckwheat and Sally were fooling around in class... ...when the teacher rapped her wooden pointer down on the desk. "Alright class, who can spell the word 'dictate?'" "I can! I can!" Buckwheat said, waving his arm up in the air. "Don't be silly Buckwheat. You don't even know how to spell." "I do. I do, teacher. I know how to 'pell." "Alright then. How do you spell dictate?" "Um...d...i...c...t..a...t...e. Dictate." "That's really good, Buckwheat. I'm really proud of you. And can you use it in a sentence?" "Yed I can. Yed I can, teacher. Hey Tally, how my dictate la't night?"
