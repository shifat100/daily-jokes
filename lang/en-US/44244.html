English (US) Joke

2 blondes A blonde was driving home when she was pulled over by a female cop, also blonde. "Can I see your ID?" says the cop. The blonde starts rummaging through her purse and is getting flustered. "What's it look like again?" "It's small, rectangular and looks just like you" The woman hands the cop a small mirror. "Like this?" "Oh," said the cop. "I'm sorry, I didn't realize you were a cop too."
