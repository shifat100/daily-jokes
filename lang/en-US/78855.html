An Indian lady visited a bar for the first time An Indian lady visited a bar for the first time, She sat on the table in front of the bartender.. A guy at her left side ordered: "Jack Daniels, Single" A guy at her right side ordered: "Johnny Walker, Single" The bartender looked at the lady & said: And you..?? She replied: "Savitri Deshpande, Married."
