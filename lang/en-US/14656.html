English (US) Joke

Two cowboys are at a saloon in the Old West. After a long day of shootin, tootin, and high-falootin, they've settled down for a conversation over the finest beer in the frontier. &nbsp; One says to the other, "You ever ate a banana?" The second cowboy replies, "No, I hain't. What's a banana?" "Well, it's this new, shiny, bright yella fruit, that a bunch of ex-plor-ators found in the South America. Darn tasty, too. I'll get one to ye." &nbsp; Months pass. The two cowboys meet again at the same saloon. &nbsp; "So, did ye get the banana I sent ye?" "I sure as hell did." "What'd ye think?" "It was... &nbsp; "...it was the worst thing I ever ate! I mean, the cob was so soft!"
