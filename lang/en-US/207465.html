English (US) Joke

Faith is Always Right The teacher said to the cooking class. "Tell me: what is the most important thing to put in a chocolate cake?" Faith quickly replied, "Your teeth!" The teacher said, "No, the ingredients." After correcting Faith, the cooking teacher said, "Who can tell me the best way to keep yogurt from spoiling?" Faith answered, "By eating it!."
