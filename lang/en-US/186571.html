English (US) Joke

Four men are watching a juggler... An Englishman, a Frenchman, a Spaniard, and a German are watching a man do some juggling on the street. The juggler notices that the men can't see very well, and stands on some boxes. "Can you all see me now?" He calls "Yes." "Oui." "Sí." "Ja."
