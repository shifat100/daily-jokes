English (US) Joke

A lifeguard blows his whistle at a little boy and asks him to come over... He says to the boy, "Hey, you're not allowed to pee in the pool." "That's not fair!" says the boy, "There must be dozens of people peeing in the pool every day! Why do you gotta pick on me?" The lifeguard says, "Well most people don't do it off the diving board."
