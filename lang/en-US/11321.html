English (US) Joke

A white man, a black man, and a Chinese woman walk into a museum. The white man says "Wow, I can't believe my ancestors BUILT this country To which the black man responds: "Actually, it was MY ancestors that did that". The white man then says: "Oh, uh. My ancestors built the railroad"!! The Chinese woman responds: "It was MY ancestors that did that". The white man than says: "My ancestors!!!... Were terrible people".
