English (US) Joke

Hear Me, Lord! Bubba listened to the preacher at the revival and when the preacher asked those with needs to come forward for prayer, Bubba got in line. When it was his turn, the preacher asked, "What do you want me to pray about?" Bubba said, "Pray for my hearing, preacher." The preacher put one hand over Bubba's ear and his other hand on top of Bubba's head and prayed a while. Then he removed his hands and asked, "Bubba, now how is your hearing?" Bubba answered, "I don't know, preacher. It's next Wednesday at the Baton Rouge courthouse!"
