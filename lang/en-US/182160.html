English (US) Joke

prom night a high school boy asked a girl to prom and she says yes. when he goes to get fitted for a tux there is a crazy long line, hes never seen a line so long. so he waits in line forever. its finally his turn and he looks good in his tux. then he has to get a limo and there is and even longer line there, this line seems to take a lifetime. after hes done waiting and waiting hes at the front of the line and he gets the nicest limo. hes at the prom dancing and his date get thirsty and when he get to the concession stand there is no punch line.
