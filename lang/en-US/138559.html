English (US) Joke

Jesus is on the cross... ...and, looking down at Mary and the disciples gathered round him, he says "You can stop this right now, you know". To which the disciples reply, "How, master? Tell us how we can stop this!". Jesus tells them, "I'm sure there is a carpenter somewhere in the crowd of onlookers, and I'm sure he will be willing to let you guys borrow a ladders and a pair of pliers". The disciples, suddenly full of enthusiasm at the prospect of saving their master and Son of God, rush out to the crowd and soon return with the items Jesus suggested they borrow, a ladder and some pliers. "How do we do this, Lord?", they ask. "Place the ladder against the beam of the cross, climb upon it and use the pliers to remove the nails in my hands and feet", Jesus instructs them. "Simple enough", one of the disciples proceeds to do as the Lord instructed. "FEET FIRST, YOU IDIOT!!", screamed Jesus, tipping forward, heading face first towards the ground.
