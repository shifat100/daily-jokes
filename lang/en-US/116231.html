English (US) Joke

Med school exam A young student is struggling during his histology exam and the professor wants to give him one last chance: "You've clearly studied the subject, but you need to be more confident. Answer me this last question and you're good to go. Is the vaginal epithelium ciliated?". The student is tired, wants leave quickly so, without thinking, abruptly replies: "Uhm...well...yes...Yes! It is ciliated". The professor then says: "Young man, remember my words: the grass doesn't grow where the train passes through..."
