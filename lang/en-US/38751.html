What is the price of this TV? Once a villager went to an Electronic shop to buy a TV, He asked the Shopkeeper What is the price of that TV? The Shopkeeper replied "Its not for sale" The villager got disgraced and went back to home, On second day he got suited booted to pretend like a rich person, Again he asked the shopkeeper Whats the price of this TV? The Shopkeeper replied the same, Its not for sale, in short the villager visited the shop almost 10 times and listening the same reply from the shopkeeper. At last the shopkeeper disclosed the secret and told him "Its not a TV its a Microwave" hehehe
