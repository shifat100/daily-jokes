English (US) Joke

Two hunters go out hunting in a forest They shoot a deer and begin dragging him back. However, they were dragging it by the tail and the antlers kept getting caught in the bushes and trees. Eventually, they pass by another hunter who advises them that they should hold the deer by the antlers so it doesn't get caught. They decide to heed his advice and carry the deer by its antlers. Soon after a while, one hunter says to the other: "You know, that guy was right. This is a lot easier." The other hunter says "Yeah but I think we've been walking in the wrong direction"
