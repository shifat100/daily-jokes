English (US) Joke

LPT: How to pick up girls Try this: 1. Acquire several dozen limes. 1. Go up to them and then drop all the limes. 1. Start picking them up, but keep dropping them. The clumsier you look the better. 1. Keep doing this until you have their attention (this could take up to thirty minutes). 1. Finally gather up the limes. Try looking a bit sheepish. 1. Look them deeply in the eyes and say, "Sorry, I'm bad at Pickup Limes."
