A Cow Rolls Again How did the cow eat grass? Without knowing where it was going. Why did the cow moo? She was on a roof. How did the cow eat grass? Her chewing is slow. Why did the cow moo? She ate her tooth. Why is there grass on the roof? A sloped ridge hides the building. What makes three loud thumping sounds? A goose, the roof and a tooth.
