English (US) Joke

psychiatrist story This guy goes to see a psychiatrist. “Doc,” he says, “I’ve got this terrible problem. Every time I get into bed, I think there’s somebody under it. So, I get under the bed, but then I think there’s somebody on top of it. Top, under, top, under. You’ve got to help me. I’m going crazy!” So, the psychiatrist says, “Come to me three times a week, and I’ll cure your fears.” “How much do you charge?” “A hundred dollars a visit.” “OK. I’ll think about it,” the man says. Six months later, the doctor meets the man in the street. “Hey, why didn’t you come to see me again?” he asks. “For a hundred dollars a visit? You must be joking. A bartender cured me for ten dollars.” “Oh, really? How did he do that?” “He told me to cut the legs off the fucking bed.”
