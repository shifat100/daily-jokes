English (US) Joke

Last night I was hanging out at the OddFellows lodge... ....and a friend had brought a bottle of sippin' bourbon to share while we played cards. There was quite a lot left when the night ended. Now it's a given that you don't leave booze at the lodge, or it will simply evaporate. Afterwards he gave me a ride home. For various complicated reasons involving a house-guest he didn't want to take the booze home himself, so he asked if I’d take it with me. So yeah, I took a Bulleit for him. True story.
