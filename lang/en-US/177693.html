English (US) Joke

Two old ladies are outside smoking a cigarette It starts raining and without hesitating one of the ladies pulls a condom out of her purse and covers the cig to keep it from getting wet. The other lady thinks this is genius and walks to the nearest pharmacy. She grabs a pack of extra large condoms and proceeds to check out. The cashier says," ma'am, are you sure you need these in extra large?" The old lady replies, "well I'm not sure, do you think they'll fit a Camel?"
