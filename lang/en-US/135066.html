Britain is the person who has past differences with people, might've been a few wars here and there but is now just indifferent to everyone and sits there drinking tea. When America walks down the hallway, and talks the the smaller countries, "s'up Lichtenstein, s'up Pakistan" and Pakistan's just like "oh my god he knows my NAME!!"
