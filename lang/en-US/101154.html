English (US) Joke

Two explorers are captured by a native tribe They are brought before the tribe's Chief who proclaims, "You have a choice. Death! Or Bunga Bunga!" Explorer 1 says, "Well, I have a wife and kids. I'll choose Bunga Bunga." The Chief cries out, "Bunga Bunga!" And the whole tribe immediately gang rapes explorer 1 mercilessly. Afterwards, and in shock, Explorer 2 says, "Fuck that! I have a family too, but I'd rather die than go back to them after that. I choose death." The Chief laughs, then cries out, "Death!........by Bunga Bunga!"
