English (US) Joke

2 Poles are watching a Football game... There is an attack by one team and the first Polish dude says: -I bet you 20 bucks he will not score -You are on - Replies the second one. The attack goes through and the person scores. So the the first pole reaches for his wallet, but the second one stops him saying: -I cheated a little, this is a rerun I knew he'd score, keep your money. -I also watched the game before, though I am still surprised the goalie let the same goal happen twice.
