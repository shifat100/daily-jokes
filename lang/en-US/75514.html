English (US) Joke

Gipsy kid becomes a skinhead A gipsy kid gets home from school, his head is shaved His mother asks him what happened, he says: "Mom, i'm a skinhead now!" His mother slaps him and sends him to his room He meets his father on the way, his face is red his father asks him: "What happened son?" He replies: "I'm a skinhead now, dad." His dad slaps him and sends him to his room He goes to his room and thinks: "I'm only a skinhead for 12 hours and i already hate these gipsies."
