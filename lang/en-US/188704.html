English (US) Joke

A gorgeous student walks into her professor's office... She says "Professor I'm really struggling in your class." He say's "Ok it's good you're here then, what can I help you with?" "Well I don't quite understand the material but honestly, I would do anything to pass." "Anything?" He asks. She leans in close and softly says "Anything." He leans in closer, almost whispering as he asks: "Would you, study?"
