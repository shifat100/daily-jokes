English (US) Joke

Paddy is a poor Irish farmer... He spends all day from dawn till dusk working his little farm to provide for his sick wife and 12 daughters. Every night he kneels by the side of his bed and prays. 'Dear Lord, I'm a good catholic just trying to do right, please oh Lord could I win the lottery?' For years and years Paddy struggles on, ekeing a meagre existence from his tiny farm, attending church every sunday, nursing his sick wife and teaching his 12 daughters how to live moral lives. And every night still, he kneels by the side of his bed and begs the lord to please let him win the lottery. One night, he is kneeling and praying like usual, when suddenly the sky bursts into golden light, a glorious chorus of angels sing and winged cherubs strum harps as the lord himself appears to Paddy. 'PADDY,' Gods deep voice booms. With tears streaming down his face and his arms stretched towards the sky, Paddy shouts 'YES MY LORD!' 'BUY A TICKET.'
