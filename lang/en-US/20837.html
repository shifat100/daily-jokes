English (US) Joke

Renting out the basement This requires two ethnicities, call them E1 and E2. It's also an old joke. Joe the homeowner was short of cash, and need to find a way to raise his income. So he decided he would get a renter. But he didn't want anyone in the house, so he figured to rent the old outhouse behind the place - nobody used it any more. So he put a sign out, 'ROOM FOR RENT', and pretty soon an E1 guy came by. He needed a new place to live real quick so he signed on the dotted line. His name was Dave. Next day Joe was happy to see a TV antenna sprouting up on the roof of the outhouse. Then, a few days later, he noticed a second TV antenna on the outhouse. He knocked on the outhouse door and Dave came out. Joe asked him, "Why do you have two TV antennas?" Dave replied, "I sublet the basement to an E2."
