English (US) Joke

(NSFW) A mother and her son are walking through the park.... And along the way they see two dogs humping. The child asks his mom "what are those two dogs doing?" His mom isn't sure how to answer and says they're making sandwiches. A short while later they see two cats humping. The child once again asks what they're doing. "They're making sandwiches too!" His mother replies, deciding it's time to go home. Later that night the little boy wakes up from a nightmare and starts crying, and his mom comes in. The little boy sees her face and asks "were you making sandwiches?" Confused the mom replies "why do you think that?" And her son tells her "you have mayonnaise on your face."
