English (US) Joke

A boy asks his dad for a drink of water... After being put to bed a boy calls to his father from his room, "Dad, will you bring me a glass of water?" The father was already in bed himself so he answered that the boy would be fine and he should go back to bed. There was about a 5 minute pause and then the boy called out again asking for water. The father replied in the same way. After another 5 min the boy called out again with the same request. The father, getting annoyed, replied back, " No, you're fine. Go to sleep and if you ask again I'm going to come in there and spank you." There was about a 10 minute pause this time before the boy called out again, "Dad, when you come in here to spank me will you bring me a glass of water?"
