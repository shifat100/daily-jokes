A musician encountered a pianist late one night... It was pitch black so he never met him, but he heard this beautiful melody in the dark... Dumbfounded the musician demanded explanation; he said, "Impossible. How can you even hit the right keys in the dark like this?" "Hahaha," the pianist laughed. "It's simple, I *C#*!"
