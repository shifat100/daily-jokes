English (US) Joke

An American, a Japanese and a Bosnian architect are looking at a mountain. American: If we Americans started drilling on the one end of the mountain and on the other end of the mountain, we would build a tunnel in about two months. Japanese: That's nothing, if we Japanese started drilling on the one end of the mountain and on the other end of the mountain, we would build a far better tunnel in only one month. Bosnian: If we Bosnians started drilling on the one end of the mountain and on the other and of the mountain, in one month our employer would get two tunnels for the price of one.
