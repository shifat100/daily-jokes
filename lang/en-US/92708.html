English (US) Joke

Inspector Jacques Clouseau was flying to Bangkok for the first time and was very excited... ...so as soon as it was announced that the plane was about to land in Bangkok, unable to contain his excitement, he started shouting,"Bangkok, Bangkok, Bangkok"... Air Hostess: Sir, be silent. Clouseau: Okay...Angkok, Angkok, Angkok!!
