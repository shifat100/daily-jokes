English (US) Joke

So a blonde was trying to sell her car.. Unfortunately, her car had over 200,000 miles on it and she wasn't able to get very good deals for it. After mentioning her issue to one of her co-workers, he says that he can reset the mileage so she can get a really good deal for selling it. She agrees to and he does his business, gives it back to her, and they go back to their daily lives. After a month or so, the co-worker sees the blonde still riding in the same car and asks her, "what happened? Were you not able to sell the car?" And the blonde replied "Why would I want to sell this car? It has no mileage on it!"
