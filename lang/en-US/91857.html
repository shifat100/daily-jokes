English (US) Joke

A couple is driving along when all of a sudden! The wife yells "Look out!" Husband immediately notices a skunk on the road and unfortunately hit the poor skunk with the car. Wife demands that he stop the car and go see what happened to the poor skunk. Husband comes back with the skunk and states "The skunk is alive but he appears to be injured". Wife replied "Lets rush to take him to the vet now!" Husband hand her the skunk and begin to drive towards the vets office. Soon after the skunk begins to shiver. Wife states "aww poor baby its shivering." Husband replied "Well put him between your legs to keep him warm". Wife gives him a weird look and states "Wait what about the smell!?!?" Husband says "Oh. You're right just pinch his nose."
