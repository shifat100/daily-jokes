The captain and the prostitute A captain of the army goes to a prostitute and asks her : "Say, madam, for a hundred dollars, would you accept my company ?" And the prostitute answers : "Of course, a handsome military like you" The captains thanks her, turns around and shout : "Company, FORWARD !" (I hope the joke translates well)
