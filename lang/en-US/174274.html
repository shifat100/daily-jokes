English (US) Joke

"I can't see the president!" The president is holding a speech. A lady is listening in the crowd, when suddenly someone taps her on the shoulder and tells her: "Pardon me, miss! Could you take off your hat please? I can't see the president!" The lady does so. A few seconds pass, when the same man taps her on the shoulder again: "Sorry, but would you be so kind to move your hair to your other side? I can't see the president!" The lady moves her hair. But again, the man asks her: "Excuse me, but wouldn't it be much of a hassle if you stepped aside for a bit? I can't see the president!" The lady gets frustrated, turns around and tells him: "What else do you need? Should I give you a telescope as well?" "No thank you, I already have one on my rifle."
