Guy looks at the menu again and says: alright, l'll have a large bowl of pasta and some broccoli. Waitress says...I'm sorry, but we're out of broccoli! The guy thinks for a minute and says...there's no fuck in broccoli... Waitress says RIGHT...THERE'S NO FUCKIN BROCCOLI!
