You Know You're A Redneck If... You hear crack is illegal and you pull up your pants. Someone yells "Hoe Down" at a dance and your wife falls to the floor. You use newspapers for more than 3 uses in your home. Your family tree is a wreath. If your home is mobile but the 4 cars in your yard are not. Your father gave you this advice, "If you can't keep it in your pants, at least keep it in the family." The last thing relatives say before they die is, "Hey Ya'll! Watch this!"
