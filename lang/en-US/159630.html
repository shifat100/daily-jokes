English (US) Joke

Why BUTTERCUPS Why not PUSSYWILLOW :( A man is participating in a golf tournament. He was left to golf with just his caddy. On his tee-off, the golfer's ball lands in a patch of buttercups. The caddy tells the golfer he can take the ball onto the course, and he won't take a one stroke penalty. However, the golfer refuses and takes the ball out of the buttercups and takes the stroke penalty. Suddenly, Mother Nature appears. **"What you just did was amazing. I am so proud that you enjoy nature and all of its beauty. For your reward, I will give you a lifetime supply of butter."** **"Thanks,"** says the man. **"But where were you last week when my ball landed in the pussywillows?"** [Source](http://smile.xonaki.com/Joke/EN?categoryCode=EN&jokeId=122)
