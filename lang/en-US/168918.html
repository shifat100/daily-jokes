English (US) Joke

God decides to take a vacation... So he goes to his travel agent to get some recommendations. God asks the agent where he should go and the agent says, "How about the Moon? It's supposed to be all the rage right now." God thinks about it and says, "No... I'd like to go somewhere with a little more atmosphere." So the agents says, "Okay, well how about Mars? It's really nice this time of year." God considers it for a second and then says, "No... I'd really like to go somewhere with water." The agent goes, "Oh well I've got the perfect place, how about Earth? It's got beautiful water and lots of atmosphere!" God thinks about it again before saying, "No... I went there a couple thousand years ago and knocked up some Jewish girl and they've been talking about it ever since."
