English (US) Joke

The German tax evader in Switzerland A German wants to bring his untaxed savings to a number account in Switzerland. He sneaks into the bank, looks around and whispers to the bank assistant: "Psst! I've got 2 million euros in my suitcase!" The bank assistant replies in a normal voice: "Why do you try to be so unsuspicious? Poverty is not a shame in Switzerland."
