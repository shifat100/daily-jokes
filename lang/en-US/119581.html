English (US) Joke

3 little girls walk up to their father The first little girl asks, "Daddy, why is my name Rose?" and the dad says, "because when you were born, a rose petal fell on your head, so we decided to call you rose." The second little girl asks, "Daddy, why is my name Lily?" and the dad says, "because when you were born, a lily petal fell on your head, so we decided to call you Lily." The third little girl says, "hurdddurflbbbbb dur." and the dad says, "shut up Cinderblock."
