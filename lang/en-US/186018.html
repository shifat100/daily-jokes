English (US) Joke

A joke for pirate day. A pirate ship's first mate comes up to the deck to find the ship's (steering) wheel missing. The first mate rushes to the captain of the ship, only to find the wheel in his pants. The first mate asks, "Captain, did you know the ship's wheel is in your pants?" "Aaarrrh," the pirate captain replied. "I know, it's driving me nuts!"
