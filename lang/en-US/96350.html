English (US) Joke

Bugs the bunny was goofing in front of the convenience store when Leo the lion pulls up in a porsche He parks and walks in to do the groceries leaving the key in contact. Bugs not resisting jumps in, takes it out for a quick spin and brings it back scratched and dented. When Leo came back and saw the damage he goes in a furious rage. He grabes the security guard, ask who did it and leaves in a hurry. Bugs, who was hiding, walks up to the security guard and asks what he told Leo. "All I said was that i saw someone with long ears driving" The rabbit laughs, spits in his hands and pulls his ears back over: "he he, the donkey's fucked"
