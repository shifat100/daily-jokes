English (US) Joke

Martha, Lily and Maggie were having tea and the conversation went to comparing their own son's achievements..... Martha : Well, my son is currently the top lawyer in the UK that was knighted by the Queen herself and whenever anyone sees him will say "Oh there goes Sir Stewart!" Lily : Not bad, but my son has been made the Baron of Yorkiebar and anyone who sees him will say "Oh there goes Lord Allen!" Maggie : Very impressive ladies but my son is 10 metres tall and 20 metres wide and anyone who sees him will say "Oh..MY GOD"
