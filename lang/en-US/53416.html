English (US) Joke

I was in Asda earlier... this thick bitch was on the check-out, face like a slapped arse and all the charisma of a half eaten waffle. I came to pay, I had only bought milk and bread but had no change. "£1.03 please" "Sorry this is all I've got," as I handed her a £20 note. Haven't you got anything smaller, 'cos it will take all my change, and I don't wanna count out £18.97?" she replied and pulled a stroppy face. "I've got nothing, if that helps," I replied... she didn't get it so I thought fuck it, I'll pay by card. "Shall I pay by card?" I asked. "Don't do me no favours," she snapped. I kept my cool and just put my PIN in. "Cash back?" she asked sarcastically. I couldn't resist it... "Oh yes, £18.97 please."
