A Jewish man visits Germany shortly after WWII Forgive me English second language... Jewish man come to germany and visits the camps It is a very sad sight. He looks around and sees all the gas chambers. His family had owned the land where the camp was and the power plant that once employed so many hard working people. The Jewish man started to weep. He thought about how huge the gas bill must be, and fell to his knees kicking some stupid old dead people bones out the way as he wondered who he could sue to pay the god-damn bill.
