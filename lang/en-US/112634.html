English (US) Joke

A woman is at a supermarket... ...about to check out her food items for the upcoming week. She passes the cashier her things: a carton of milk, a package of Oreos, a few TV dinners, and other assorted goods. The cashier pipes up suddenly with a knowing grin. "You're single?" She looks at him, confused, before laughing and saying, "Oh, because of what I'm buying?" And he says, "No, you're ugly."
