English (US) Joke

Little boy asked his father 'Daddy, what does a vagina look like?' The father thinks for a moment, then responds, "well son, that depends. before sex, or after sex?" The little boy has no idea, so confusedly says, "umm.. both i guess." "Well son, before sex, the vagina is like a fragrant flower. Soft, gentle and full of beauty." "Ok daddy.. what about after sex?" "well son, have you ever seen a bulldog eating mayonnaise?"
