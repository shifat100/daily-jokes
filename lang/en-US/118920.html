English (US) Joke

The carpenter came home one day... A carpenter came home one day only to discover his wife in bed with another man. In a total rage, he dragged the naked man down the stairs to the garage and put his penis in a vice. He then secured it tightly and removed the handle. Next he picked up a hacksaw. The terrified man, screamed, "Stop! Stop! You're not going to cut it off are you?" The man, with a gleam of revenge in his eye, said, "Nope. You are. I'm going to set the garage on fire."
