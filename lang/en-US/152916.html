English (US) Joke

My dads from Northern Ireland and there they see things slightly differently. Just the other day I walked down to get breakfast and he was reading the paper. "I can't believe how the Brits talk about their own soldiers" he said pointing at his paper. I looked at the front cover and it was the Sun. The paper that makes Mein Kampf look like it was written by a bleeding heart liberal. I thought this can't be right so I went over and looked at the headline. Which was. The IRA are murdering bastards
