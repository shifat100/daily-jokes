English (US) Joke

Man has sex with a woman in Africa... As he was doing it she kept screaming "Iko ouka waka waka". He didn't know what it meant but continued. The next day he is playing golf with an african buddy. When he putts, the african screams: "Iko ouka waka waka". The man asks him what does it mean. The african answers :"You missed the hole".
