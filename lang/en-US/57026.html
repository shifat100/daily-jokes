[NSFW] Two hobos are walking down the tracks... we'll call them Jim and Steve, walking down the train tracks one night when Jim exclaims "I am the luckiest son of a bitch alive". Steve asks "Why is that, Jim?" Jim responds with "Well, 2 weeks ago, I was walking down the train tracks, same stretch right here, and I found a $50 note, went down to the bottle shop, got myself a big bottle of the good shit, I was rotten drunk for the next 3 days." Steve chuckles "Wow, that's pretty good, but I can beat that" "Oh fuck off, how can you possibly beat that?" demands Jim. Steve tells his story "Well, last week, walking down this same stretch of tracks right here, I found a woman, tied to the tracks. So I untied her, took her over into that little tunnel over there and got my reward out of her for the next 2 days" Jim is astounded "Holy shit, that's amazing! Did you get a blowjob?" Steve looks sad and says "Nah, I never found her head"
