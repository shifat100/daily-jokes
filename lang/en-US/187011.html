English (US) Joke

God calls Adam and Eve and tells them he has two gifts... "One is a penis, so you can piss standing up" says God. Immediately Adam shouts: "Me! Me! I want a penis! I always wanted to piss while standing!" So God gives Adam a penis. Overjoyed, Adam starts running around pissing on everything he can see. He pisses on the flowers and trees and animals. Eve and God are watching him. Then Eve asks: "What is the second gift?" "A brain." After a moment's thought Eve says: "Yeah, you better give that to Adam as well, otherwise he'll piss over everything in here."
