English (US) Joke

A little black kid is helping his mum cook and he puts flour on his face and says "look ma, I'm a white man" She slaps him and tells him to go say that to his grandma. He goes to his grandma and says "look, I'm a white man". She slaps him too and tells him to go tell his father. He goes to his father and says "look dad, I'm a white man" He slaps him too and asks "what have you learned?" The boy says, "I've only been white two minutes and I already hate you black bastards."
