English (US) Joke

A man comes into jail the first time in life... On the first day in the shower a huge hairy guy full of muscles comes out of the fog right to him :"You're my new wife now. Let's get it on." The man looks all around but theres nobody who'll help him and he surrenders in fear of what might come. "With or without spit?" asks the big hairy man. The first-timer answered "When I have to: with spit" The big man turns around and yells into the showers: "Yo Spit, he says you can come too."
