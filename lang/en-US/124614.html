English (US) Joke

The police asked me some questions today Police: Where do you live? Me: With my parents. Police: Where do your parents live? Me: With me. Police: Where do you all live? Me: Together. Police: Where is your house? Me: Next to my neighbor's house. Police: Where is your neighbor's house? Me: If I tell you, you won't believe me. Police: Tell me. Me: Next to my house.
