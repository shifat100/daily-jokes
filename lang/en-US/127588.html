English (US) Joke

[NSW] Bugs Bunny helps Daffy Duck understand the Theory of Relativity. Bugs and Daffy are chilling in their dorm at the Looney Tunes University. Clearly frustrated with his physics homework, Daffy slams his textbook, and tells Bugs "This Theory of Relativity crap is confusing the fuck out of me any chance you can help me understand this shit?" Bugs calms his friend down, and assures Daffy, that he will help him understand the concept behind said theory. Bugs says "Ok Daffy, I want you to get up and bend over." Daffy does as Bugs says. Bugs comes up behind Daffy, calmly spreads the feathers apart and sticks his dick in Daffy's asshole. Shocked and confused Daffy asks Bugs "How is this supposed to help him understand anything?". Bugs replies "It is simple, you have a dick in the ass, I have a dick in the ass, but it is all relative".
