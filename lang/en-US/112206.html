Smart Boy

In a Store a man asked for 1/2 packet of butter. The salesperson, a young boy, said that only full packs were available in the Store, but the man insisted on buying only 1/2. So the boy went inside to the manager’s room and said “An idiot outside wants to buy only 1/2 pack of butter”. To his surprise, the customer was standing right behind him..! So the boy added immediately, “And this gentleman wants to buy the other half!!!!!!”. After the customer left, the manager said “You have saved your position by being clever enough at the right time. Where do you come from?” To this the boy said, “I come from Brazil. The place consists of only prostitutes and football players!!!!!” The manager replied coldly, “My wife is also from Brazil “. To this the boy asked excitedly, “Oh yeah? Which team does she play for?”
