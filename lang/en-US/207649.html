English (US) Joke

The Final Wish One day, Farrah Fawcett died and went to heaven. At the gates, St.Peter greeted her. "Hello, and welcome. You have lived a life full of prosperity and love. You have given millions around the world entertainment with your acting. Now I will provide you with one wish for the Earth before you enter Heaven. What is it?" Farrah thought for a minute, but answered, "I would like all the children in the world to be safe." The next day, Michael Jackson died.
