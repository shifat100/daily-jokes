English (US) Joke

When a girl is told ugly If a guy tells you that you are ugly, he is just being mean. If a girl tells you that you are ugly, she is just jealous. If a child tells you that you are ugly, you are plain ugly. --- I thought it was funny although I don't agree. Even the guys & girls can tell an ugly girl that she is ugly. That's not a logical necessity but a fallacy called "affirming the consequent".
