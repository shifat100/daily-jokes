English (US) Joke

So four men go to hell... The demon who is responsible for their punishments has a list in front of him and asks them about their sins. He says to the first one: "I see here you have committed the sin of pride" "Yes, and I'm very proud of it!" he responds. Then he goes on to the second one: "I see here you have committed the sin of greed" "Yes, but I wanted more!" he responds. He proceeds to the third guy: "I see here you have committed the sin of wrath" "Yes, but don't mention it because I'm getting upset" he responds. Then he goes to the last guy and says: "I see here you have committed the sin of lust" And he responds: "GO FUCK YOURSELF ALREADY" disclaimer: the joke works much better in greek, but I tried. :D
