English (US) Joke

A Psychiatrist is sitting in his office... When his secretary comes in and says "Sir, there's a man here to see you who thinks he's a flock of crows. If you ask me we should just send him to the loony bin and be done with it." And the psychiatrist replied "Doris! Are you asking me to commit a murder!?"
