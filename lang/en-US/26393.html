English (US) Joke

A boy and his uncle are fishing at a lake The boy them notices something, and shouts "Did you see that?!". The Uncle says no. "A big Bald Eagle just flew right over us!". A while later, he spots something again. "Did you see that?!". His Uncle says no once more. "Are you blind? a huge bear just walked over that hill!". A few hours later, he spots something else. "Did you see that?!". Now the Uncle's getting pretty annoyed, so he shouts "Yes!". So the boy replies, "Then why did you just step in it?"
