She starts to cry again. The man again asks her why she is crying. The woman states that she has never been kissed. They guy grants her wish again and kisses the woman. More time passes, the woman notices that the bottle is empty and the guy is shitfaced. Again she starts crying. The guy looks over at the woman and asks "What the hell is wrong with you now?" The woman says "Well, I've never been fucked." The guy ponders on it for a minute and says "Okay I can do that too." The guy stands up, and walks over to the woman.
