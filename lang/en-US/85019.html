English (US) Joke

Watch out for Uncle Frank A teacher tells Johnny’s class to ask their parents to tell them a story with a moral at the end... The next day the teacher asks who wants to go first. Johnny raises his hand and says, "My badass Uncle Frank was a pilot in Vietnam. One time he had to bail out over enemy territory with nothing but a flask of whiskey and a knife. He drank the whiskey on his way to the ground and landed in the middle of an enemy camp. He killed them all and walked out of there with a smile on his face." "Johnny, what is the moral of this horrible story?" shouted the mortified teacher. "Stay the fuck away from Uncle Frank when he's drinking."
