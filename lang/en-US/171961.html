English (US) Joke

Two men are in the operating room Two men are in the operating room getting prepared for surgery. One says to the other, "Hey buddy, what are you in for?" The other says, " I'm here to get my tonsils taken out" The first man says "Oh that's not so bad. I had that done when I was younger, and I got to eat ice cream for two days strait." The second man felt at ease with this reassurance. "Thanks for that, doesn't sound so bad. What about you, what are you in for?" The first man says, "I'm here to get circumcised." The second man says" Oh I had that done when I was a baby. I couldn't walk for over a year!"
