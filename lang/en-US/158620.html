English (US) Joke

There was an elderly couple aged 65 years old Who had been married for 45 years. They shared a bed and like most couples had one side each, on her side was a bedside table with make up, hair curlers magazines etc, on his bedside table there was a small wooden box. The husband went away to work one day and his wife was cleaning the house, she got to the bedroom made the bed and started dusting the bedside table. She accidentally bumped the small wooden box and it crashed onto the floor, and out rolled one egg, and $2000 cash. She thought to herself 'what on earth is this' then quickly put the contents back in the box and put it back so her husband would not notice. Later that evening the husband and wife were eating dinner, he noticed that she was just sitting there picking away at her food. He said "what's wrong dear, is everything alright?" Her curiosity got the better of her and she replied "I was cleaning the bedroom today, and that wooden box beside your bed, well it's been there for many years and I've never asked about it, but I accidentally knocked it over on the floor. Why is there an egg in there?" Her husband replied "look I'm really sorry, I was trying to never tell you why but seeing as you have asked, I will tell you. Every time I have been unfaithful to you I put an egg in that box". His wife was shocked at the revelation that he had been unfaithful to her, but also remembered they had been married for 45 years, so being unfaithful once over those years perhaps wasn't so bad. She then asked him "why is there $2000 in the box?". He replied "well, every time I got a dozen eggs, I sold them!"
