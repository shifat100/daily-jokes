English (US) Joke

Russian political joke from time of the Russian Empire A man in the street shouts: "Nicholas is a moron!" (Nicholas is a common Russian given name, it's also the name of two Russian emperors). He is naturally arrested by the police and charged with insulting the emperor. He tells the officer: "I meant another Nicholas". The officer answers: Do no be silly. If you said 'moron', you obviously meant our Emperor!"
