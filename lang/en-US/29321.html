English (US) Joke

God went to a Frenchman He said 'I've got some commandments, do you want some?' 'What are they like?' The Frenchman replied 'Thou shall not commit adultery' Answered God 'I don't think so...' Slurred the Frenchman, so God went to a German and asked if he wanted any. 'What are they like?' The German questioned 'Thou shall not kill' God replied 'Hmmm, perhaps not' The German sighed, so God went to an Italian, offering him some commandments 'What are they like?' The Italian inquired 'Thou shall not steal' Answered God 'Perhaps not' The Italian replied. So God went to a Jew and offered him some commandments 'How much are they?' The Jew asked 'They're free' God answered 'I'll take ten' Said the Jew
