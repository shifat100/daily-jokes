English (US) Joke

Going Through Customs At A US Airport Airport Staff: Sir, do you have anything to declare today? Me: *starts sweating* Uh no... *trips and falls* *hundreds of Kinder Surprise Eggs roll out of my pockets, jacket and briefcase* Airport Staff: **GET ON THE GROUND NOW!** Me: But I am. *armed Guards swarm around me and pin me down* Armed Guards: **WHAT'S IN THE EGGS?** Me: I don't know it's a surprise!
