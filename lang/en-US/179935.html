English (US) Joke

Two guys are arguing over a dispute... The first guy says, "Now that's it. This time you truly went too far!" "Oh shut up! You always say I go to far. I just borrowed a couple hundred of dollars. You have so much more in your bank." Replied the second guy. "Ohh, I'm serious. This is the one time you broke the record and just went too far. You're never coming back this time! "Well do you know who else broke a record and went too far? Asked the second guy "Who?!?!" "Amelia EarHart. And she also never came back...."
