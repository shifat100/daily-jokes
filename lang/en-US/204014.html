Inflatable Doll A guy goes in an adult book store and asks for an inflatable doll. Guy behind the counter says, "Male or female?" Customer says, "Female." Counter guy asks, "Black or white?" Customer says, "White." Counter guy asks, "Radical Christian or Muslim Extremist?" Customer says, "What the hell does religion have to do with it?" Counter guy says, "The Muslim Extremist blows itself up."
