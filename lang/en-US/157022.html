English (US) Joke

A duck walks into a bar... Duck says: Got any grapes Bartender Says: No sorry, this is a bar The next day the duck walks in again Duck says: Got any Grapes Bartender says: No this is a damn bar, if you say that again I'm gonna nail your beak shut! The next day the duck walks into the bar again. Duck says: Got any nails? Bartender says: Nope Duck says: Got any grapes? Bartender says: I might not have any nails, but I sure as fuck have a stapler! The next day, after going through surgery to remove 246 staples from his beak, the duck had lost his desire for grapes.
