English (US) Joke

A factory inspector pays a visit to a ruler factory... and he walks in through the door. As he's walking around, he notices a room with a black man in a thong, and a lot of boxes. He finds this rather odd, but he decides to ignore it. After that, the factory manager comes to greet him. "Good morning, sir", he says. "Please. Follow me." The factory inspector follows the manager to a conveyor belt. "These are our conveyor belt workers. They check the rulers on the conveyor belt for defects", explains the manager. The factory inspector nods. He then follows the manager to an area where workers are loading rulers into boxes. "These are our loading workers. They load the rulers into boxes." Once again, the factory inspector nods. Finally, the manager leads the factory inspector into the strange room he had seen before. "This is Tyrone. He makes sure all our rulers are exactly 12 inches."
