English (US) Joke

I'm sorry A non-English speaker (A) and an English speaker (B) bumped on the street, A: "I'm sorry." B: "I'm sorry too." A: "I'm sorry three." B: "What are you sorry for?" A: "I'm sorry five." B: "Excuse me, are you sick?" A: "I'm sorry seven."
