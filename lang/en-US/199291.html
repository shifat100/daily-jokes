English (US) Joke

Tatamagouche An Ontario coupling were on vacation, driving around the province of Nova Scotia. At noon, they arrived at the town of Tatamagouche. The two began to bicker about the pronunication of the town's name. The husband suggested that they stop for lunch and ask. At the restaurant, they placed their order. The wife asks, "Excuse me, but could you slowly pronounce the name of this place?" The employee looks surprised, but complies. "Bur ... ger ... King" (BTW the town's name is pronounced Tat ah mah goosh)
