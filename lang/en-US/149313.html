What'll thirty buy me? So Betty dashes around to Frank and ask him. Frank says, "Tell him thirty dollars buys a hand job." So Betty goes back tot the guy and says his thirty will buy a hand job, take it or leave it. The guys says, "Okay, sugar, jump in, I'll take the handjob. Is there a quiet alley around here?" Betty has him drive around the corner to Frank's alley, and the guy unbelts his pants to reveal the most gargantuan schlong. "Wait here!" gasps Betty. "I'll be right back" She jumps out of the guys car and knocks on Frank's window. Frank lowers the window, "What now?" Betty says, "Frank, hey, Frank, lend this guy seventy dollars!"
