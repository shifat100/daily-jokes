English (US) Joke

Compliments A man walks into a bar and has a drink. Then he hears a tiny voice say, "You are so handsome." He looks to see who said that but finds no one. So he drinks again. Then he hears a tiny voice say, "You are so smart and kind." He looks around again and doesn't find anyone. He asks the bartender whats going on. The bartender says, "O that, that's the peanuts. They're complimentary."
