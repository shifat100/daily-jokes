English (US) Joke

A stuttering man entered a bar He asked the bartender: "C-Could I h-have a b-beer?" The bartender answered: "S-Sure, in a m-minute" As the man took a seat, another man asked the bartender: "I'll have a beer." The bartender answered: "Sure, in a minute." Irritated, the man went back to the bartender and said: "A-Are you s-screwing w-with me?" The bartender: "N-No, that w-would be the o-other guy I s-screwed with."
