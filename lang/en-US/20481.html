English (US) Joke

Pigs digging So one day, Draymond Green was having a great game. He went home and got a call from the local farm, asking if he could help promote their farm. Draymond was feeling particularly happy that day after going ham on the court so he agreed. Once he arrived on the farm, he was greeted spectacularly by the farmer and given a tour around the farm. When it got to the point of the pigs area, he saw that one of the pigs was digging strangely in the ground. When he got closer to get a better look, he fell face first into the mud. That was when he thought to himself, "Snap! What a pig dig mistake!"
