English (US) Joke

A woman walks into a sex shop looking for a dildo. She asks the clerk "How much for the white one?". He says "That one's $25." She asks "How much for the black one?" He says "That one's $45." She looks around for a bit then asks "Mmmm, how much for the plaid one?" Clerk responds "Ummm, that one's $65." "Great I'll take it." she says. The store owner returns later in the day and asks the clerk "So how'd it go today?" Clerk says "Well, I didn't sell any merchandise but this woman came in and bought my thermos for $65."
