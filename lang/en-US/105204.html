English (US) Joke

An Afghan lands at a Pakistani airport, and walks up to the immigration desk. He introduces himself to a Pakistan Immigration Officer, as Ex-Minister of Ports & Shipping of Afghanistan. The surprised Pakistani Officer asks: "But there is no sea in Afghanistan. How can you be the Ex-Minister of Ports & Shipping.?" The Afghan replies: "Wallah ya Habibi, don't you have a Minister for Law & Justice in Pakistan?"
