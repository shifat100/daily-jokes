English (US) Joke

The president of France, USA and Brazil are in a plane The french president puts his arm out of the window and say, "We are in France!". "How do you know?" the others president asked. "I just touched the Eiffel tower!". &nbsp; So a little time later the president of the United States puts his hand out of the window and say "Well, i can confirm that we are in America!". "How?" the others asked. "I just touched the statue of liberty!". &nbsp; Some hours later the president of Brazil puts his arm out of the window and say. "Yes, we are in Brazil". "You just touched the Christ?" they ask. "No No, somebody took my watch".
