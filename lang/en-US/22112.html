There's a circus entertainment... and the master of ceremony announces: - Meet the world famous acrobats are on the stage! Applause! One guy from the audience yells: - Haha, look at these faggots! They are wearing tight trousers! After the acrobats have done their act the master of ceremony appears on the stage again: - Let me introduce 2 funny clowns! The guy yells again: - They do makeup, they are in wigs and dressed like faggots! Look at them! Disgusting! The act ends and the master announces the next one: - Dear friends! It's an unique act: a deadly jump from under the circus' roof right to the stage without any insurance! The guy: - Hands off me, fags! Where the hell are you dragging me to?!
