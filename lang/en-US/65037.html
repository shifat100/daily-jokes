English (US) Joke

Toast. A married couple is making breakfast. As the husband is buttering the toast, he says, "Have you ever noticed that if you drop a piece of toast, it always lands butter side down?" The wife says, "No. It just seems that way because you remember when it makes a mess. Here, I'll show you - watch this." She then drops a piece of toast and it lands butter side up. She says, "See? I told you so." The husband replies, "Well, obviously you buttered the wrong side..."
