English (US) Joke

John and Jane go to see a marriage counsellor. The counsellor says, "So, you two are married, correct?" "Correct," they reply. "And you are having regular sex?" asks the counsellor. "Absolutely," they reply, "So," laughs the counsellor, "What is the problem?" They say, "Well, our partners don't really approve."
