English (US) Joke

Oh, Jim... Jim goes to the Doctors about a rash. "Doc, I've had this rash on my chin for 2 weeks now. I've tried everything; aloe vera, tea tree oil, vaseline. Nothing works." The Doctor says "Oh I've seen this before" "You have?" "Yes. I had the exact same thing last week. I was going down on my wife once a night for a week and one morning, it was gone. You should try it" "Ok Doc!" So he goes off and comes back 2 days later. "Wow, Doc, You were right. It does work. And I must say, what a beautiful house you have"
