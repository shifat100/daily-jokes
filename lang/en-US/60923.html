English (US) Joke

A man walks into the bar and orders 3 beers at once He explains to the bartender that he just moved to the city and promised his two drinking buddies that whenever he went out to drink, he'd get one for them too. This tradition continues for months until one day, this man walks in and orders two beers. The bartender says "oh no! Did one of your friends die?" The man says "Nah all is well. I just quit drinking"
