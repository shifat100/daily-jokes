A Tiger Wedding A tiger was getting married and all animals attended the wedding. Every animal stood at distance and wished the tiger A cat came and climbed to the stage and danced nicely then extended his hand to wish the tiger The tiger roared in rage and said how dare you come on the stage? Even the panther is maintaining its distance and you climbed the stage. The cat replied and after listening to that the tiger fainted. What would have the cat said ??? Any guess??? The cat said "Oh shut up buddy, even I was a tiger before marriage"
