English (US) Joke

Two men at a bar make a bet. Two men were sitting outside a bar and drinking beer. Suddenly one of them points at the apartment building next to the bar and says „I bet you 50 bucks that every woman in that building is on her period“. The other man thought it was easy money so he takes the bet. So the men walk into the first floor of the apartment building at ring the doorbell. They see on the postbox that a husband and a wife life there together. The husband comes to the door and the men ask really awkward if his wife was on her period and the man replies that she‘s indeed on her period but how did they know? It‘s only a guess said the man who placed the bet. The same thing happened on the 2nd and the 3rd floor every woman was on her period. On the 4th floor and the last one they noticed a man and his teenage daughter live there alone. They ring the doorbell and the father comes to the door. More awkward than before they ask the man with a bit of a hesitation if his daughter was on her period. The father stares at them for a bit and suddenly said „Can you tell?!?!?“ and wiped his mouth.
