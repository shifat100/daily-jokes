English (US) Joke

Two Jews walk by a Christian church. . . There is a sign on the door that says, "convert to Christianity and receive $100". One of them speaks up and says, "I'm going in." His friend says "you're really going to change religions for $100?" "A $100 is a $100, I'm doing it!" And he walks inside. A few minutes later he walks back out and his friend says, "Well? Did you get the money?" He replies, "Oh, that's all you people think about isn't it?"
