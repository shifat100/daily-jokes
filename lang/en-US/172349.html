Theory and Reality A father is asked a question by his son: "What's the difference between theory and reality?" The father doesn't totally know how to explain it, so he goes to the grandmother and asks "For 100,000 dollars, would you sleep with any random man?" She says "The money would be good for the family, but that's against my religion, so no." the father goes to his wife and asks the same question. She tells him "The money would be good for the family, but I'm married to you, so no." Finally, the father asks the daughter the same quesiton and she says "the money would be good for the family, but it's a little weird, so no." The father then goes to the boy and says "In theory, we could have 300,000 dollars, but in reality we have 3 selfish bitches."
