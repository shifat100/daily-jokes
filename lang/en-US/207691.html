An Invitation This 4-year-old kid is sitting on the couch watching T.V. silently, with an angry look on his face. After a while, his mother notices this and asks him, "Why the long face?" "Well, Mommy, I know that you invited everyone to your wedding. You invited grandpa, grandma, my uncle, your friends and all . . ." "So what's the problem, Sweety?" his mother asks. "WELL, WHY DIDN'T YOU INVITE ME?"
