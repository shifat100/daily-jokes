English (US) Joke

A young man is preparing for prom night. First, he needs a prom date. He goes to ask the prettiest girl in school, but he has to stand in line behind a dozen other guys doing the same thing. Remarkably, she says yes to him out of them all. So, the next day he goes to rent a tux, but being prom season, he has to stand in line at the store behind a bunch of other guys. He finally gets his suit and looks fabulous. Next, he goes to the florist to get her a bouquet of flowers, but like before, he has to stand in line. He drives to her house the night of, but traffic is bad, and he has to wait in line at the traffic signal. He finally arrives. Her whole extended family want photos with the beautiful young lady, and he has to stand in line just to get a photo with his date. they arrive at the prom location, and have to wait in line to get in the building. At long last, they begin to dance. She gets thirsty, and asks him to go get drinks. He gets to the concession table and is relieved beyond words that there is no punchline.
