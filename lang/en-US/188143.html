English (US) Joke

My mate Paddy called me last night. He said, "My dog had nine puppies 12 weeks ago and now apparently they are worth £600 each." "Fucking hell, mate." I said, "Drinks are on you then!" He said, "I'm not selling them." "Why not?" I asked. "Well," he said, "If they're worth £600 each now, can you imagine what they'll be worth in 10 years?"
