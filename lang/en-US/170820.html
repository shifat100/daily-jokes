English (US) Joke

A man went to heaven... So a man in the hospital died of a sickness and went up to heaven. Before he could enter he had to go to a sort of office building, he noticed the office walls were covered in clocks. He asked the angel working at the desk, "What are these clocks for?" To which the angel replied, "These clocks move 1 minute for every lie someone tells or told." The man points to a clock, "Whose is this?" The angel tells him, "This is Abraham Lincoln's clock, it has only moved 2 minutes, indicating he only told 2 lies." The man points at another clock. The angel says, "That is Mother Teresa's clock, it hasn't moved at all." The man then asks the angel, "Where is Barack Obama's clock? To which the angel replies, "In Jesus' Office, he's using it as a ceiling fan."
