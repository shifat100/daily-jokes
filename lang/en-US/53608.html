Hillary appeared on the New York radio program 'The Breakfast Club' with hosts DJ Envy, Angela Yee and Charlamagne Tha God on Friday. the trio broadcasts to a nearly entirely black audience from New York City's Harlem neighborhood. 'What's something you always carry with you?' Yee asked.  'Hot sauce,' Clinton shot back without skipping a beat.  Charlamagne pointed out that 'Hot sauce in my bag, swag' is a line in Beyonce's racially charged song 'Formation.' 'Now, I just want you to know people are going to see this and say "Okay, she's pandering to black people,'' he said. 'Okay, is it workin'?' Clinton shot back, half-serious.
