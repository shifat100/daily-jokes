English (US) Joke

A Frenchman and an Italian were seated next to an Cornishman on an overseas flight. After a few drinks, the men began discussing their home lives. "Last night I made love to my wife four times," the Frenchman bragged, "and this morning she made me delicious crepes and she told me how much she adored me." "Ah, last night I made love to my wife six times," the Italian responded, "and this morning she made me a wonderful omelette and told me she could never love another man." When the Cornishman remained silent, the Frenchman smugly asked, "and how many times did you make love to your wife last night?" "Once me ansum," he replied. "Only once?" the Italian arrogantly snorted. "And what did she say to you this morning?" "Don't stop."
