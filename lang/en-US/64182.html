English (US) Joke

There was an Information Technologist... and he stumbled upon a man in the nearby park. The man was arranging people by there jobs and putting them in rooms labeled with a letter. (Doctors go to room A, businessmen go to room B, etc.) The man then saw the Information Technologist and said to him "If you are an I.T., you are E". The Information Technologist suddenly left, leaving the man puzzled. After a few minutes, the Information Technologist came back with a chair and a table. The man said "Why did you bring a chair and a table?" The Information Technologist replied "I thought you said Furniture"
