A English businessman was rushing through an airport when A English businessman was rushing through an airport when all of a sudden, he bumps into a tiny Asian women. She immediately says, "I'm sorry!" In a hurry, the businessman says, "I'm sorry too." She responds with, "I'm sorry three!" Confused, the businessman stops and says, "What are you sorry for?" She yells, "I'm sorry five!!!"
