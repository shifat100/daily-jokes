English (US) Joke

In a medieval kingdom... There's a force of Knights who dominated the land - you could always tell if they were about to attack you, because they would chuck a giant rock with a war symbol on it before they arrived to kill. Hence, they became known as the 'signs'. They had two forces: a main force, but then a company who would travel beside them, and supply them. One morning, a man shows up named 'Juan', and challenges the signs. The signs laughed, and thought they needed only their squires to defeat him. The main force sent their squire, but the company of the signs sent their squire as well. The three men battled their hearts out, but at the end of the fight, they were all worn out, and couldn't lift a finger - they had come to a draw. The next day, the two squires attacked Juan again, same result - the next day, the same. This went on and on, until the leader of the signs declared a truce. It just goes to show that the squire of a sign and the squire of a co-sign are always equal to Juan.
