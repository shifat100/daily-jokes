An english bloke walks into a British pub with an ostrich and a cat An english bloke walks into a British pub alongside an ostrich, and a cat. He walks up to the bar, and says to the barkeep, "Barkeep, I'll have a pint!" The ostrich says, "I'll have a pint!" The cat says, "I'll have a pint, but I'm not paying!" The barkeep then says, "Okay, your total will be six pounds forty" so the bloke reaches into his pocket, pulls out exactly six pounds forty, and gives it to the barkeep. They take their drinks, enjoy them, and leave. The next day, the three of them walk into the pub again, and the same thing happens. The bloke says, "I'll have a pint!" The ostrich says, "I'll have a pint!" The cat says, "I'll have a pint, but I'm not paying!" The barkeep says, "Okay, your total will, once again, come to six pounds forty". The barkeep says, "Sure.
