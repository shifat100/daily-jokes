First day as SOLDIER!

A new soldier was on sentry duty at the main gate. His orders were clear. >**"No car was to enter unless it had a special sticker on the windshield"** A big army car came up with a general seated in the back. The sentry said, "Halt, who goes there?" The chauffeur says, "General William." "I'm sorry, I can't let you through. You've got to have a sticker on the windshield." The general said, "Drive on!" The sentry said, "Hold it! You really can't come through. I have orders to shoot if you try driving in without a sticker." The general repeated, "I'm telling you, son, drive on!" The sentry walked up to the rear window and said, "General, I'm new at this. *Do i shoot you or the driver?*"
