English (US) Joke

A priest is doing confessional and really has to go to the bathroom. While he's in between people, he notices the janitor outside the confessional booth. **Priest:** "Hey John, come sit in here for me for a second while I use the restroom, please." **John the janitor:** "Yes Father, no problem." As the janitor is waiting for the priest to return, a woman enters the confessional booth. **Woman:** "Forgive me father, for I have sinned." The janitor nervously proceeds, "What did you do?" **Woman:** "I gave a blow job to a married man." As the janitor is struggling to come up with how to respond, he sees an altar boy walking through the church. **John the janitor:** "Hey Timmy, what does Father Angelo normally give for a blow job?" **Timmy:** "A bag of chips and a coke."
