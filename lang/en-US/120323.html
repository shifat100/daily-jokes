English (US) Joke

Freddy, the social type of fish, was doing his normal morning swim. He greets and gets greeted from all sides, “hi Freddy, whaddup! Howya doin’, Top of the morning to ya…” When suddenly he spots a whale. *Well damn, first time I see a whale, better say hello* “Hello mr. Whale!” No response. *Hmm… maybe he didn’t hear me…* “Hello mister Whale” Nothing… *Maybe he can’t hear too well.* Freddy swims to his ear. “HELLO MISTER WHALE?” Still nothing. *Must be that whale noise* “IIIHEEEEEELLLLEEEEEEUUUUUWWWWW MIIIIIIISSSSTEEEEERR WHHHHAAAALLLLEEEEEE” The Whale doesn’t respond. Freddy swims in front of him. “MISTER WHALE, YOU ARE GETTING RUDE, I KEEP SAYING HI. IT WOULD BE POLITE TO ANSWER.” Freddy starts swimming away “…fuck all that shit” when slowly the whale turns around: “It’s Misses Whale!”
