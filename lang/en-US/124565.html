Then the chemist tells his idea: - I would find some chemicals that added to her food would cause a heart-atack. She is old so no one would even get suspicious. - And how would you do it? - they ask their friend who studied agriculture - Oh I know exactly how! I would buy a loooot o aspirin, put it all in my mouth, chew it a little bit and form a huge pill of aspirin. Then I would put it on the table and wait for her to come see it. She be like: - Wow! that is a huge aspirin pill! - and then I would smash that bitch`s head with an axe!
