English (US) Joke

Man wakes up in the hospital... ...after and accident and doctor is by his bed and starts explaining his situation. Doc: Sad to say i have some bad news and good news. which one would you like first? So the guy wants to hear bad news so good news can cheer him afterwards Doc: You have been in a terrible accident and you have lost both your legs Patient: So what is the good news then? Doc: The rich guy, in bed next to you, really likes your slippers
