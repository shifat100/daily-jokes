English (US) Joke

Ironic A Jewish family has a reunion to celebrate Hanukkah. During the reunion, a boy has a conversation with his grandfather, a Holocaust survivor. Boy:"Grandpa, isn't it ironic how Hitler used the swastika, a symbol of peace, as the symbol for the Nazi party? Grandfather: No, that's not ironic. Boy: Oh, yeah? Then what is? Grandfather: Your father's Volkswagen.
