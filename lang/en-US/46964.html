English (US) Joke

A black kid wants to join a father-son lookalike contest But he doesn't have a father. However, he still shows up to one at a local auditorium. The attendants ask him where his father is, and he tells them he doesn't have one. The attendants let him go on, as he is very young. The spotlight pansb over the contestants. When they get to him the judges stop. The judges announce him winner, saying the man behind him in the spotlight looks just like him.
