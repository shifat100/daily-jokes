English (US) Joke

Daddy longlegs! A little girl was playing in the garden when she spotted two spiders mating. Daddy, what are those two spiders doing? she asked . They're mating, her father replied . What do you call the spider on top? she asked. That's a daddy longlegs, he replied. So, the other one is a mommy longlegs? the little girl asked. No, both of them are daddy longlegs, he replied. The little girl thought for a moment, then took her foot and stamped them flat, and said. Well, we`re not having THAT sort of shit in our garden.
