English (US) Joke

A man phones home from his business trip... His 9yo son answers and says hey. "Hey buddy, it's dad! Wheres youre mom?" "I will check", replies the son as he walks to his parents' bedroom and sees his mom in bed with uncle Jim. "She is playing in bed with uncle Jim" "What?! Uncle Jim?! Tell them I will be right over!" the man fumes. "Mom, dad says he'll be right over", says the kid. His mom starts panicking and shouting, uncle Jim jumps quickly from the window and falls in the empty pool and cracks his head. The mom rolls in sheets, exits the room and falls down the stairs and cracks her head. The kid looks around and starts crying. "What happened son?" "Mom fell down the stairs abd uncle Jim jumped into the empty pool and died" he squeals. "The pool?" Asks the man. "Is this the Goldberg house??"
