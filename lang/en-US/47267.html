English (US) Joke

A guy wanted to celebrate and throw a party So he went to a bakery to get a cake for his party. But when he got there, there was a huge line. He finally got through and went to a pizza place to get a couple pizzas for his party. But there was a huge line there too. He finally got through and he gets home and starts to set up the party. The party is going great and he's having a great time. He then gets thirsty and goes to the punch bowl. And when he gets there, there was no punch line.
