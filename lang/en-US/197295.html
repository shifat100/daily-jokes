English (US) Joke

There were three fathers to be in a... There were three fathers to be in a hospital waiting room, waiting for their babies to be born. The nurse came out and told the first father, "Congratulations, you're the father of twins!" "It figures," the first father said. "I'm the manager of the Minnesota Twins." Five minutes later the nurse came out and told the second father, "Congratulations you're the father of triplets!" "It figures," the second father said. "I work for 3M." At that the third father fainted dead away. The second father turned to the first and asked, "What's the matter with him?" The first father replied, "He works for 7-Up!"
