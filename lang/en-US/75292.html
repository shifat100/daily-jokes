English (US) Joke

A man travels to an island... A person travelling remarks on how healthy the locals look, and an attractive man says, "Yes, it's the island. When I first arrived I was bald, didn't have teeth, and couldn't walk -- but now look at me." The traveler: "Wow... That's amazing. So where are you from?"
