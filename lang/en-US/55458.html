English (US) Joke

Lines With No Punch Two guys are hanging out drinking in their garage. One said to the other.. "I bet this month's rent that I can break through that wall with no physical force." The other replied, "HA! I'll take your money if you're really willing!" The first guy chuckled a little then looked straight at the wall and said, "We're both part of a lame joke with no punchline."
