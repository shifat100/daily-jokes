English (US) Joke

A student isn't paying attention in a Chemistry class. The teacher notices this. "JOSEPH!" She shouts. Joseph, the student, snaps his head up, to look at the teacher. "Have you even heard a WORD I've said?!" she yells. Joseph nods. "Oh REALLY?! Then, I hope you won't mind telling me and the rest of the class the 116th element on the periodic table!" Joseph looks at her blankly and goes, "Uuh..."
