English (US) Joke

Polar bear cub... A Polar bear cub is running around the igloo; his mama bear tells him to go outside and play. After a few minutes the cub comes inside and says, "mom, am i a Polar bear?" Mama bear says, "yes, now go play!" And the cub goes outside. A few minutes later the cub comes back in. "Mom, are you sure I'm a Polar bear?" "Yes, now go outside and play!" The cub leaves and comes back a few minutes later. He says,"mom, you're sure I'm FULL Polar bear?" Mama bear says, "yes honey, you are FULL Polar bear. Your dad is a Polar bear, I'm a Polar bear, so you are 100% Polar bear. Why do you keep asking?" The cub says, "because I'm freezing my BALLS OFF out there!"
