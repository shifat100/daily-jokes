English (US) Joke

The kid is not Esus Although he was born years ago in Bethlehem. He was not a miraculous child. Weighing a mere 10 pounds when born, his birth was not celebrated. His father left the family almost immediately. Skin as pink as Dahlia, he began crawling within a day. He drank milk and ate vegetables within the second day. By the third, he attempted to form words with his tiny tongue, but they hardly resembled the spoken language. Within a week, hair had grown all over his body and he ran faster than any child in town. By the second week, he stood 2 feet tall and proud. He didn't live beyond the third week. A lion devoured him. He was not missed because he wasn't a human child. He was a goat.
