A Candid Evaluation of the British Navy Once upon a time, in the 1800s, a bureaucrat of the British Empire was assigned the task of interviewing sailors to get a candid evaluation of the level of contentedness of sailors with their captain. As he was opening his folder, he asked the first sailor that he was interviewing, "Where are you from originally?" "Charleston." "And how has the way things are run on board this ship struck you so far?" "I'm impressed."
