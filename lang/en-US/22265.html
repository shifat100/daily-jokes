English (US) Joke

My first blind date. My friend set me up on a blind date and all I had was the phone number of the guy I was supposed to meet. So I call him up and ask how will I recognize him? "I am 175cm tall and weigh 75kg and I will be standing in the corner. What about you?" I replied, "Well, I guess I will be the one with a tape measure and a bathroom scale..."
