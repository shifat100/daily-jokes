Apparently, each one of them was boasting about their vampiric abilities to one another.Vampire A said, "Look at that mansion over there! I can finish all of its inhabitants within 10 minutes!" And so he flew to the mansion, and 9 minutes later, he came back with his mouth filled with blood, looking satisfied with himself.Vampire B then said, "Bah! Look at that village over there! Give me 5 minutes!"He flew to the village, returned at the next 5 minutes, with his mouth filled with dripping red blood.Vampire C then yelled, "Pffft! Look at the TOWN over there! Give me 3 minutes!"He flew to the direction of the town, and one minute later, he returned with his mouth filled with blood.Vampire A and B stared at him with amazement, then immediately asked, "How come you have such speed, friend?"Vampire C pointed at a direction, then asked them, "Do you see a building over there?""Yes!" answered A and B."Well I DON'T!!!"
