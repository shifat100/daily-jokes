English (US) Joke

A girl invites a guy over to smoke weed and chill at her place... ...they are already pretty high, when the girl leaves the room to use the bathroom. As soon as she shuts the door the guy realizes he needs to take a dump real bad. He sees a dog sitting in the corner and quickly comes up with a cunning plan - he decides that he will shit on the floor and then simply blame the dog for it when she comes back. He does just that. She comes back and freaks out - "why is there a shit on my carpet?!" "It was the dog, it got up and took a shit on a carpet, nothing I could have done to prevent it" he claims. She looks at him in disbelieve and replies: "That's a stuffed dog you retard!"
