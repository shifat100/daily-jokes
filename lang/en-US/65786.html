English (US) Joke

I had just landed from a long haul flight extremely tired and flagged a taxi down to take me home... As the journey began i informed the driver that I may fall asleep and to wake me upon arrival. I snoozed off and 5 minutes into the journey the car took a big BUMP! and shocked me awake. 'What the hell was that?' I asked. 'Sorry pal, I just ran over nigger!' he replied. 'In my tired state I barely registered and fell back to sleep. But then 5 minutes later: BUMP ! BUMP! 'What the hell?!' Once again being rudely awoken. 'Sorry pal.' He replied. 'I had to go up the kerb to get that one!'
