English (US) Joke

The ventriloquist... ...and his dummy were getting big laughs with their repertoire of blonde jokes. Midway through the act, a blonde woman in the audience stood up and yelled, "This is offensive! Is it right to stereotype people by their race? No! Is it right to stereotype people by their religion? No! So why is it okay to stereotype women by their hair color? I'm a blonde, and I'M not stupid!" "I'm sorry, Miss," said the ventriloquist. "I certainly didn't mean any offense." "You stay out of this, buddy," said the blonde. "I'm talking to that little smartass on your knee!"
