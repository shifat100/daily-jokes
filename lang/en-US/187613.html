English (US) Joke

A Man at a Bar orders a nice Strong shot The bartender gives him one, and the man gulps it down with ease and asks for another one. The Bartender is happy to oblige. After ten shots, the bartender says, "You're really big on drinkin' today. Is it a special occasion?" The man says, "Yes. I'm celebrating my first Blowjob." The bartender replies, "Well, then, how about another shot on the house?" The half-drunken man tells him, "No thanks. The tenth one got the taste out of my mouth."
