English (US) Joke

A woman is fed up with receiving lame birthday presents from her husband... So, two weeks before her birthday, she tells her husband "You always get me the worst presents when my birthday rolls around. Well, this year had better be different. When I wake up in two weeks, there had better be something in the driveway that will go from O to 200 in less than 60 seconds!" &nbsp; A week and 6 days pass, and the woman goes to bed, trembling with excitement as she imagines what the her husband has gotten her. The next morning, she wakes up early, and notices her husband is already missing from the bed. So, she rushes downstairs. &nbsp; Her husband is already at the door, holding it open for her, a wide smile upon his face. She squeals with excitement, runs out the door, straight to the driveway.... &nbsp; Where she finds a bathroom scale, complete with a bow on top. &nbsp; Their divorce was finalized 3 months later.
