English (US) Joke

Silly Billy (Its a translation of a popular joke from my country. My English is a bit rusty. ) So Silly Billy was driving and he had a door on the roof of his car. A Police Officer pulled him over. Police Officer - Where are you taking this door? Billy - Oh, I am taking it to the locksmith. Police - What?.... Why? Billy - Oh I lost my key and I need to unlock the lock. Officer laughed a bit and asked Police - What if a theif gets into your house? Billy - No one can get in my house. I got the door right here.
