English (US) Joke

Guys Goes to a Hardware Store to Buy a Hammer A man walks into a hardware store to buy a hammer. When he gets to the register the attendant says: Attendant: "That will be $10." Guy: "But across the street they have hammers for $5" Attendant: "Then why don't you buy a hammer across the street?" Guy: "Well, they ran out of Hammers." Attendant: "Interestingly enough, our hammers are $5 also when we don't have any."
