English (US) Joke

Everything is about confidence. Many Engineering teachers were invited to a flight, after they all took seat they were told that the plane they were in was entirely built by their students, Every one of the teachers quickly panicked got up and tried to exit the plane desperately but one of them was quiet and appeased he said: "I Know exactly what my students can be capable of, so im confident as hell this shit won't even start".
