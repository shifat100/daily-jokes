English (US) Joke

Moron of the year. Every year a group of people meet. No one know how long this has been happening, it just does. The moron of the year from the previous year stands on a stage and holds up an item. He describes it, then anyone from the crowed may take a guess as to what this item is. The one who guesses correctly wins. that person is the new Moron of the Year. One year the item is held up, it has two wheels and you can ride it. After many guesses someone yell out, A Bicycle! TaaaaDaaaa! Moron of the year. The next year the same, it has little beads in it, you can sit on it as it forms to your shape. Some one yells out, A Bean Bag! TaaaaDaaaa! Moron of the year. Today the item is held out, it comes in a tube you squeeze it out of the tube and you clean your teeth with it.
