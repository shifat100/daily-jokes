my first self-made joke attempt: "A Super Friends Orgy" Wonder Woman decided to take a photo to remember the epic orgy her and the other Super Friends were taking part in that night at the Hall of Justice. She got her iPhone out, told everybody to get it on, and took the picture. When she looked at the photo, she frowned and said, “There must be something wrong. This picture turned out like crap!” Batman grabbed the phone, looked at the photo, and said, “How ‘bout next time you turn the Flash on first.”
