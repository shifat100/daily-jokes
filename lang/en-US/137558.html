English (US) Joke

I asked my grandma what people use to think of democrats and republicans over 70 years ago *Watching the news with my grandma* Me: Grammy, when you were really young, did they talk about democrats and republicans, like they do today? Grandma: What do you mean? Me: Were they always hostile towards one another, like this lady on the news. Grandma: Oh yeah, that's one thing that has never changed over the years. Me: Well, what do you remember people saying about democrats and republicans when you were young. Me: What is the first thing you remember about it? Grandma: Well, I always heard the older people say the same thing Grandma: "Republicans are for the rich, and democrats are for the poor." Me: What did they say about everyone in the middle? Grandma: I don't know, they always just talked about getting screwed.
