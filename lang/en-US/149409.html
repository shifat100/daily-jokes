How did I get my name? A young Native American boy goes to his father and asks "father how did brother get his name" Father looks at his youngest son and says "when he was born we heard a wolf sing out to the moon, so we call him Singing Wolf" The young boy then asks "Father how did sister get her name" Father looks at him again and says "well when your sister was born a majestic eagle soared over head, so we named her Soaring Eagle" Then curious the Father looks at his youngest son and asks "why do you wish to know this Two Dogs Afuking"
