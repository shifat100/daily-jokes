English (US) Joke

It was time to name Canada All the 4 founding hosiers were sitting around and no one could agree on a name. They finally decided to put a bunch of letters in a hat and 3 people would draw one out at a time while the last transcribed the name. The first guy drew and read, "C, eh?" The next drew, "N, eh?" Finally the last maple head drew and said, "D, eh?"
