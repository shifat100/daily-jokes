English (US) Joke

The pick up line... Walked up to a nice girl at the bar last night and asked her if there were 20 letters in the alphabet. She said, "no" , looking at me like I had two heads. "There's, 26." "Oh," I said, "you're right! U-R-A-Q-T!" Smiling, she giggled and said, "You're forgetting one more..." Leaning in towards her, I whispered, "No I'm not. You'll get the D later."
