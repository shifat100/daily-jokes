English (US) Joke

A CNN Reporter gets a story (got shown this one) A CNN reporter goes to a foreign country to interview a man. This man has been praying to a Religious wall. He has been doing this for 60 years. The reporter asks what are you praying for? The man replies "To end world hunger and disease." "To help the innocent and protect the weak" "For Christians, Jews and Muslims to get along" "For Presidents and big business to stop lying" "And to end human pain" To this the Reporter replies "Wow. Do you think your God is making a difference? The man replies "No. I feel like I am praying to a Fucking Wall"
