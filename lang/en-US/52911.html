English (US) Joke

A buffalo is ruminating in the middle of the great plain... as he observes a small white rabbit getting nearer and nearer. The rabbit stops right in front of his face. *"get the hell out of my way, you big ass imbecile!"* the little rabbit yells. The buffalo just ignores him. The rabbit goes on yelling. *"are you stupid, other than ugly? I'm going to kick your ass so hard that you will feel pain for the rest of your useless life!"* The buffalo snorts. *"Oh my God, I can't believe how dumb you are, giant walking pile of crap! Just get out of my face, son of a stupid cow!"* At that point the buffalo loses his cool, turns and unload a huge pile of crap all over the little bunny and goes away. After a while the rabbit manages to get out of the dump, looks at the buffalo going away and yells at him: *"Yeah, run away, you big coward! I made you shit yourself, didn't I?"*
