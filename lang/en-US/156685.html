English (US) Joke

A man offers a woman at a bar a million dollars... to have sex with him, after not much debate the woman agrees and says "absolutely". The man with a smile on his face then says "will you have sex with me for $100". The woman snarls back at him "What kind of woman do you think I am?" The man gingerly replies "We have already determined that, we are just discussing price now."
