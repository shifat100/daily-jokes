English (US) Joke

Not a joke, just a story with a reminder to be careful when telling jokes... I heard a joke a few weeks ago that went, "What do you do when an epileptic is having a fit in the bath? Throw your washing in." I decided to repeat the joke in work today to a few of my customers (I work in a pub) and when I finished, one guy got really mad at me. He screamed that I shouldn't tell jokes like that because his brother died in the bath as a kid while having an epileptic fit. I asked, "Did he drown?" The guy was furious and said, "No, he choked on a sock!"
