English (US) Joke

4 teens get arrested in a prostitute's den They spend the night in the lockup and in the morning their parents come to bail them out. The Japanese kid bows his head as his father comes and walks out without saying a word. The Asian kid hugs his mother and starts venting out about how horrible it was in the lockup. The European kid says a "Thank you dad". The American kid walks out and while leaving says "Wow! Thanks for nothing dad! That's all I needed. You think you own the world. Guess what you don't own me, you didn't own me earlier and you will never own me in the future either. Will you please stop doing all this!?". Edit: The joke is based on how spoiled, stupid and thankless American kids are portrayed in the media (Ex. Dexter - Astor, loads of American movies and TV shows).
