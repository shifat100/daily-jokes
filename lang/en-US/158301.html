English (US) Joke

A man is getting ready to go on a business trip... He has been suspicious of his wife cheating on him. So he places under his bed a spoon attached to a string that hangs on the metal frame with a bowl of milk under it. He measures the weight of his wife in bed alone to make sure it is not in the milk unless there is more weight. The man leaves and comes home after a few days and looks under his bed to find a bowl of butter.
