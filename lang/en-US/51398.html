English (US) Joke

a man is sitting in a bar and a very big man sits next to him and says "did you know that if you take ten shots and jump off of the 10th floor of this building, you'll be perfectly unharmed?" the man responded very skeptically "I'm not falling for that". the big man says "no I'm completely serious, watch I'll do it first". so the big man takes 10 shots, goes up to the 10th floor, jumps off, hits the ground hard, and stands right back up. the man says "WOW! I've gotta try this" and proceeds to take ten shots. he goes up to the 10th floor, jumps off, hits the ground hard, and dies. the big man begins to chuckle, walks back into the bar and sits down. the bartender looks at him disappointed and says "Superman, you're an asshole"
