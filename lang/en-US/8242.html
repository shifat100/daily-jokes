English (US) Joke

A couple is walking in St. Petersburg Square on Christmas Eve when they feel a slight precipitation. "I think it's raining" says the man "No, It's snowing" replies the woman "How about we ask this Communist Officer here? He is always right!" exclaims the man."Officer Rudolph is it raining or snowing?" "Definitely raining" Officer Rudolph replies before walking off. The man turns to his wife with a smile and says "See, Rudolph the Red knows rain, dear."
