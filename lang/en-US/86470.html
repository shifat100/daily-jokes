4 friends in their 20s.... Four friends in their 20s go to a new restaurant, Sands, because they've never been there before. 10 years later, they reunite and go back because the waitresses were so pretty. In their 40s, they decide to go again because of the amazing wine selection. Another 10 years pass and they once again go because of the delicious food. In their 60s, they go again because the quiet atmosphere is perfect for a low conversation. You guessed it, in their 70s they go again, this time for the amazing sunset over the late. Ten years later, they decide to go to Sands, because they've never been there before.
