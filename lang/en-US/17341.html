English (US) Joke

My wife and son were talking about his nut allergy when we dropped him off at summer camp... Wife: "We talked to your counsellors about your allergies. They said they will bring up the topic during the first cabin meeting." Son: "Mom! Geeeeez. So embarrassing!" Wife: "It's no big deal. They are going to make sure the other kids will wash their hands if they eat nuts." Son: "Mom, you know I'm good with my surroundings. I will just stay away from them." Wife: "It doesn't hurt if they know. You can't be so quiet about it. You need to be more vocal. Just wait, in a few years you will be on a first date with a possible girlfriend and you'll have to say, 'look, I like you and want to kiss you right now but did you eat nuts today?'" Me: "wtf"
