English (US) Joke

He Didn't Take Off Two old pilot friends in the Air Force were talking about the day's activities: Jenkins (first pilot): Did you hear? Captain Smith jumped out of a B-1 bomber, without a parachute, and he wasn't hurt! Randy (Second Pilot): That's impossible! The fall would have killed him! Jenkins (shakes his head): The bomber hadn't taken off yet.
