English (US) Joke

Bacon tree Two soldiers are sat on top of a hill looking for targets. One says to the other "Hey bob, is that...is that a bacon tree?" "I don't know Jim, go have a look while I stay here and cover our backs" Jim goes for a closer look and Bob stays on the hill eating his lunch. After a while Jim returns with about ten arrows in his chest. "My god what happened? Was it a bacon tree?" "No Bob, turned out to be a ham bush"
