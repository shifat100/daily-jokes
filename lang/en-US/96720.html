English (US) Joke

A woman gives birth to twins and then goes into a coma When she wakes up, she realises that she is yet to name her child. Doctor: Oh, don't worry about that miss. Your brother was here while you were unconscious. He named your children. Mother: What!? My brother is a complete idiot! Oh God, what did he name my daughter? Doctor: Denise Mother: Oh, I guess that's not too bad. What a relief. And what did he name my son? Doctor: ....Denephew
