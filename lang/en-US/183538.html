English (US) Joke

A woman goes into her orthodontist's office. She then began to strip, and eventually got naked. When her orthodontist walked in, he exclaimed, obviously embarrassed, "Oh, my! I'm sorry, you must've walked in the wrong building. The doctor's is next door." She assured him she was in the right building, pointed to her vagina, and said, "You put my husband's retainer in, you can get it out."
