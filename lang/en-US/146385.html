My Car Won't Drive At Night The service department of a BMW dealership took a call. The customer stated that his car, a 380i, will not drive at night. The advisor, flummoxed at this, asks for more detail. "You see," the owner replies, "when it is daytime, I put the car in 'D' and it drives like a dream." "But when I put it in 'N' for Nighttime....'"
