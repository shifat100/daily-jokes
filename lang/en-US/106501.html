English (US) Joke

A pregnant woman A pregnant woman from Washington D.C., gets in a car accident and falls into a deep coma. Asleep for nearly six months, she wakes up to find that she is no longer pregnant. Frantically she asks the doctor about her baby. The doctor replies, "Ma'am, you had twins: a boy and a girl! Your brother from Maryland came in and named them" The woman thinks to herself, "No, not my brother! He's an idiot!" She asks the doctor, "Well, what's the girl's name?" "Denise." replied the doctor. "Wow that's not a bad name. I like it! What's the boy's name?" "Denephew."
