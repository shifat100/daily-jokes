English (US) Joke

A man traveling into the city One evening, a man who is traveling into the city encounters to a stop light with one of those new traffic cameras installed. As he approaches the light it is green, and then turns yellow as soon as he is passing, but as he passes he notices the camera flash and take his picture all before the light turns red. Confused, he decides to turn around and approach the light again, this time crossing as it is still green, and like before, it flashes as soon as he crosses. He gets annoyed and devises a plan. He decides to go a third time while it is red, stop, wait for it to turn green, then slowly pass the light. As he does this the camera flashes a 3rd time and takes his picture. He then goes on his way and prepares himself to fight the charges and anxiously awaits his ticket for his day in court. Finally, after a couple of weeks he receives a letter from the big city with 3 pictures attached accompanied by 3 tickets... for driving without a seatbelt.
