English (US) Joke

There once was a women who had a hundred children.. There once was a woman who had a hundred children. She named each of them after numbers, in the order they were born. All of them died except for Ninety. Ninety went off to have some children of her own. Her kids were very kind, and one day they found an injured dog. They took the dog home but never told their mother, fearing she would kick the dog out. In fact, they never told anyone about the dog. To further keep the puppy secret from the world, they named the dog “This,” so that its name could be used in conversation without arousing suspicion. One day the dog ran away, and they never saw This again. But nobody else knew about This. Nobody knew that a dog named This had even existed. Only Ninety’s kids will remember This.
