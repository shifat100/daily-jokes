Life Mini-game "Tip the Janitor" So I play a game and I'm pretty sure it's original and no one else plays it yet. You could be next! I have amassed almost $2.50 as my highest while at an all night drinking session with friends. Sometimes the quarters are gone when I know no one cleaned the urinal and it makes me laugh my ass off to think of some customer fishing my pee-soaked quarter out of the urinal. Well that's it, remember to always have fun no matter where you are.
