True story I went today down the street. I look the granny siting on a bench, holds heart. I run to it, ran to a drugstore bought acpirine and brought to hospital. While sat in the accident ward, I noticed in a window suspicious persons near one car. Probably want to steal. I ran out from hospital. They run in the car from me. I jumped so in my car. I go behind them. I called police. Generally together with police we them caught up. Went to policeoffice. While sat in office I look out of the window, the house burns there. I run there. In a window on the third floor the child shouts. I saved him. His parents set fire to the house and escaped. Generally now we collect documents for adoption.
