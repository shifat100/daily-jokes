English (US) Joke

Jesus was on a walk in town one day... When he crossed paths with a Roman soldier. Feeling particularly cool, Jesus put his hand up for a hi-five while passing by. The Roman soldier, having ill feelings towards Jesus' teachings and following, simply grunted and ignored Jesus. Jesus was a little disappointed so he then turned around and said, "Aww come on man, don't leave me hanging!"
