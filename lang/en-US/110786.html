English (US) Joke

A trans woman is about to win $1,000,000 on a game show... if she can answer the final question correctly. The game show host smiles at the camera and asks, "Alright, Theresa. What is the world's largest mammal?" The contestant, Theresa, overcome with the excitement of winning such a large sum of cash, shouts her answer immediately: "Elephant!" "Ooooooh, I'm sorry," says the host. "That's incorrect." Crestfallen at going home with no money, Theresa looks down and shakes her head and mumbles, "Whale then..."
