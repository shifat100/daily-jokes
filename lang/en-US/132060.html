English (US) Joke

First prize in the synagogue lottery Rabbi announces 3rd prize in the synagogue lottery goes to Mr. Schwartz - an all-expense paid trip to Hawaii. Applause all around for Mr. Schwartz. Rabbi announces 2nd prize goes to Mr. Litvak - a chocolate cake. Well, Litvak goes nuts - says WTF - for 3rd prize Schwartz goes to Hawaii and for 2nd I get a cake? Rabbis says - You don't understand - this cake was baked with loving care by the rebbitzen [wife of the rabbi] Litvak says: "Fuck the rebbitzen" Rabbi says - No, that's first prize.
