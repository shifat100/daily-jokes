English (US) Joke

Jesus and Buddha's favorite pastime Jesus: Hey Bruddha I'm bored, wanna play rock paper scissors...but with a twist? Every time a player loses, He gets a flick to the head. Buddha: Interesting. Ok. *proceeds to lose every single round, audible yelps of pain ensue* Jesus: Hey you alright? Tough luck man let's take a break. *5 minutes later* Jesus: Okay I'm bored again, wanna play that game again? Buddha: No way, I always lose and my head is still aching! Jesus: Fine...Hmm how about for this round, the loser gets a flick to the balls instead then? Buddha: Challenge accepted. And the rest is history. Even today, Buddha can be seen with bruises all over his head, with both hands in a ready-to-flick gesture...meanwhile Jesus is always on the lookout because he doesn't want Jesus Jr. hurt; even when he was crucified he kept his legs crossed Edit : Formatting.
