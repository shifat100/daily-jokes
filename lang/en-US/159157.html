English (US) Joke

My African-American pastor was not amused Man is in a hospital room with his wife who is giving birth. After all the pain and pushing, she gives birth to a healthy baby boy. A half-black baby boy. The white father is outraged, and runs out of the room, knocking things down and shouting on his away down the hall. The doctor who delivered the baby rushes to him and asks him what is wrong. Man: That baby is half-black. I am white. Don't you see a problem here!? Doctor: Sir, I still don't see the problem. Man: She clearly cheated on me with a black man! Doctor: Sir, sir, your wife is black. Man: Oh, yeah.
