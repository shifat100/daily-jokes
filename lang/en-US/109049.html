English (US) Joke

I went to the Doctor's last week... I went to the Doctor's last week because I found some lettuce growing out of my anus. The Doctor said 'Ok, let's have a look, drop your trousers'. So I drop my trousers, and he says 'Ah yes, I can see some leaves poking out...' I said 'That's nothing -- that's just the tip of the iceberg!'
