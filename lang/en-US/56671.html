English (US) Joke

5 friends new to the business world asked a consultant of what to do with their money. The consultant told them to buy a taxi as it is a good business and since they are 5 they can have it run for many hours and days. The 5 friends bought a taxi and started the work. A month later they had really bad numbers. So they decided to go back to the consultant again. They asked him: why are we not gaining money; is it the place we operate in? Is it the time? The consultant asked them: how are you managing the taxi? So one guy said: two in front and three at the back.
