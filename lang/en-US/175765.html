English (US) Joke

A brunette, a farmer and a sheep... On her day off work, a young brunette decided to take her new convertible car for a drive through a farming community. After an hour of driving she had to stop while a farmer shepherded his sheep across the road. The brunette realises a rare opportunity and asks the farmer "if I can guess how many sheep you have, can I have one?" "Sure" replies the farmer after thinking a bit, "that wouldn't be easy" "326" the girl says out of nowhere.. "Correct!" said the stunned farmer and never the less gives her a sheep. The brunette is extatic by her efforts, but just before she pulls away the farmer stops her and asks "How about another deal? If I can guess what color your hair was before you dyed it brunette, can I have my dog back?"
