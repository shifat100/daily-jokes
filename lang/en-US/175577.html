A mom, her child and a policeman are waiting at the bus station... A mom, her child and a policeman are waiting at the bus station, and the bus is late. Child: "Mommy, when's that bus coming?" Mom: "Shhhh baby, it's on it way". After 5 minutes, the bus is still not coming and the child is getting anxious: Child: "Mommy, where's that bus already?" Mom: "Oh, baby, I think it's just around the corner. It'll be here in no time." Couple of minutes more pass: Child: "Mommy where is the bus now?" Mom: "It had a flat tire, my child. It'll be here as soon as they change a spare one." 15 minutes later: Child: "Mummy, where is the bus already?" Mom: "Oh, it got dirty after all the repairs, they just went to the car wash, they'll be right here soon enou..." Cop: "Why the hell did they need to wash it now, I'm late as it is!"
