English (US) Joke

Jesus was set in charge of pearly gates of St. Peter for a few minutes. One day Jesus was walking by the pearly gates when St Peter asked him to watch the gates for a few minutes. Jesus agrees and in a few minutes he sees an old old man approach. The old man walked very slowly, had a halting gait, long white hair and a beard. When Jesus asked if he could help, the old man said that he was looking for his son. Jesus wanted to help but didn't think he could because there were millions of people there. "I know I can identify him," said the old man, "because he has holes in his hands and feet!" Jesus looks at him in shock and says, "Father? Is that you?" The old man looks at Jesus and says, "Pinnochio?"
