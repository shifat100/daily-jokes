English (US) Joke

Priceless Picture A man sat in his attorney's office. "Do you want the bad news first or the terrible news?" the lawyer asked. "Give me the bad news first." "Your wife found a picture worth a half-million dollars." "That's the bad news?" asked the man, incredulously. "I can't wait to hear the terrible news." "The terrible news is that it's of you and your secretary."
