Do you like The Pussycat Dolls? A woman is in a bar and sees a man she is attracted to so she tries to start a conversation with him, she isn't good at pick up lines so she tries to think of something she can ask him. A Pussycat Dolls song is playing so she thinks to herself that she should ask this man if he likes the pussycat dolls. She asks "Do you like *the* pussycat..." Out of nowehere, the man starts kissing her and he doesn't let her finish her sentence. He then takes her to his place and they do it. After they're done she asks him "This is what I wanted, but how did you know that I wanted this?" The man asks "How did you know that my name was Cat?"
