English (US) Joke

Vegans these days.. So my sister is vegan and one night we went out to get some dinner. After we sat down we noticed the table next to us ordered 5 rare steaks, one for each family member. My sister became upset and decided to only order a water. When the steaks arrived she lost it and began to cry. In the midst of the crying I noticed she stopped drinking her water and had asked the waiter to take it away. When i asked her why she said my tears fell into my water so now I can't drink it because my tears were a product of animal suffering... vegans these days.
