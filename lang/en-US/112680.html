English (US) Joke

A boy is asked if he knows the first 4 letters of the alphabet... So, he goes home and asks his mum what the first letter of the alphabet is - her reply being: ***"Shut up!"*** He later visits his brother, who is watching batman, and asks him what the second letter is. ***"NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA BATMAN!"*** After that, he asks his dart-player-dad what the third is. He yells: ***"180!"*** For some reason, he then thinks that his little sister might know the 4th: ***IN MY LITTLE-RED NODDY-CAR BEEPBEEP*** So, the next day, he tells his teacher that he knows the first **4** letters of the alphabet! So she asks what they are. ***"SHUT UP!"*** ***"I'm sorry mister, who exactly do you think you are?"*** ***"NANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANANA BATMAN!"*** ***"Tell me, how many detentions do you want?"*** ***"180!"*** ***"How do you think you will get away with this?"*** ***"IN MY LITTLE-RED NODDY-CAR BEEPBEEP!"***
