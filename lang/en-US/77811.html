English (US) Joke

A fat manchild, who is completely obsessed with pokémon, sneaks into the vents above the women's bathroom... He slowly creeps along the vents, armed with an iPhone, take shots of the girls from above. The stranger thing is, he's looking for a very specific girl. An Asian woman called "Chu Watagashi". As this happens, the vents are starting to creak and break under his weight. They give way and he falls into one of the stalls he was about to take a creepshot of. The girl inside screams as the ball of fat plops down from above. He pauses for a moment, looking at the terrified woman and then takes a photo of her from below. She slaps him and screams "WHY THE FUCK WOULD YOU DO THAT?" He adjusts his fedora and then says "I just wanted to take a Peek-at-chu"
