English (US) Joke

A blonde comes into work crying... As she sets down at her desk, her boss comes over and asks her whats wrong. Blonde: my mother just died. Boss: Please, go home, you of course can take time to grieve and take care of anything. Blonde: No I want to distract myself today. Later that day the boss walks past the blonde's desk and sees her crying again. Boss: OK, I am tell you to go home, your mother dying is serious, go take care of yourself please. Blonde: This isn't about my mother, I just talked to my sister and HER mother died too!
