Blonde's Bad Dream A blonde keeps having the same weird dream, so she goes to her psychologist. Psychologist: What is your dream about? Blonde: I am being chased by a vampire... Psychologist: So, where are you in this dream? Blonde: I am running in a hallway. Psychologist: Then what happens? Blonde: Well, that's the weird thing. In every single dream, the same thing happens. I always come to a door, but I can't open it. I keep pushing the door and pushing the door, but it won't budge! Psychologist: Does the door have any letters on it? Blonde: Yes. Psychologist: And what do these letter spell? Blonde: "P-U-L-L."
