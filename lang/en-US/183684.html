English (US) Joke

Catching an elephant is so easy anyone can do it! Step One: Know that elephants *love* peas. Step Two: Dig a (big) hole and fill it with branches and sticks and burn them. After it cools it will be a soft bed of ash. Step Three: Camouflage the hole and cover it with peas. Step Four: When an elephant comes to take a pea kick it in the ash hole! Congratulations you've caught an elephant!
