English (US) Joke

Kevin is woken up for school. Kevin is woken up by his mother. "Rise and shine, Kevin! Time to go to school!" "But mom, I don't want to get up." "No, you're getting up now, no excuses." "Name me two good reasons for why I should get up now and go to school.." "First of all: You're 54. And second of all, you're the principal!" *Source: Hamburger Abendblatt issue #124*
