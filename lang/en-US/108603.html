English (US) Joke

A young soldier is sent to a military base in the middle of a desert. After a few weeks he starts to get extremely horny, so he goes to the captain to ask him what to do. "Chief, how can you withstand such long times without women?" "You get used to it soon, but if you really miss them, there's a camel in the stable that you can use." He is disgusted by the thought at first, but after a long time he goes there and does his business. At last, dirty and tired to death, he is done. On his way back he meets the captain. "Boss, it was so hard, how do others do it?" "Normally, we sit on the camel and ride to the city." (Sorry for bad English)
