New Baby Sibling When a woman discovered that she was pregnant, her four-year-old son overheard his parents' conversation. He didn't say anything until a week later when a family friend asked him if he was excited about the prospect of a new brother or sister. "Yes," said the boy, "and I know what we're going to name it. If it's a girl, we're going to call her Emily, and if it's another boy we're going to call it Quits." [Source](http://smile.xonaki.com/Joke/EN?categoryCode=EN&jokeId=122#mainBody)
