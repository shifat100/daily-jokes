English (US) Joke

A man with a big camera and a crew of people with microphones behind him knocked on the door of Wayne Manor. Alfred Pennyworth walks quickly to the door and opens up. "Does Caitlyn Wayne live here?" asked the man. "No," says Pennyworth, "I believe you're mistaken. It's Bruce Wayne." The man snickered. "Looks like someone didn't finish season six."
