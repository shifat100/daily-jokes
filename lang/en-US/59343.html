English (US) Joke

An Irish priest, a Scottish priest, and an English priest are sitting and enjoying pints after Sunday mass. A fly lands in the English priest's beer and he dumps the pint in disgust. A fly lands in the Scottish priest's beer and he picks out the fly and resumes drinking the beer. A fly lands in the Irish priest's beer, he picks up the fly, holds it up to his face and yells, "Give it back ya bastard!"
