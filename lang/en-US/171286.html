English (US) Joke

Two Blonds at a Job Interview Both were in the reception waiting to be called in for an interview. The first blond looks at the second blond and says, "Are you here for the interview too?" The second blond says, "Why, yes I am." The first blond says, "Do you know IBM?" The second blond says, "Oh! Me too, especially after eating oatmeal!"
