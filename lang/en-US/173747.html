English (US) Joke

Married in the arctic circle After 30 years of unfulfilling matrimony a crotchety old Alaskan couple finally decide to seek marriage counseling. Upon the first meeting with their therapist they both sit down awkwardly on the couch, and pull back their Anorak hoods only to realize that they've been married to the WRONG person for the past 30 years. The wife sighs, looks at the doctor and exclaims "It's like I've been trying to tell him doctor, I'm just not that Inuit."
