English (US) Joke

the widow Boudreaux Three Cajuns were at work when one (named Boudreaux) suddenly died of a heart attack.  One of the other Cajuns said, "I know where he lived, but I wasn't personally acquainted with his family. How are we going to break the news?"  The other Cajun replied, "Don't worry. I am plumb full of tact and know what to do."  So the two Cajuns went to Boudreaux's house and rang the doorbell. When his wife answered, the other Cajun said, "Hello, are you the widow Boudreaux?"  She replied, "My name's Boudreaux, but I'm not a widow."  "The hell you ain't."
