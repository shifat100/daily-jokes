English (US) Joke

A man meets a Genie and gets three wishes However any of his wishes that are granted, also come true for all the politicians in the world times two. **Genie:** What is your first wish? **Man:** I want a million dollars. **Genie:** You now have a million dollars and all the worlds politician now receive two million. **Genie:** What is your second wish? **Man:** I want a new Mercedes. **Genie:** You now have a new Mercedes and all the worlds politician now receive two of them. **Genie:** What is your third and final wish? **Man:** I want to donate a kidney.
