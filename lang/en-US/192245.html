Tapeworms in a cow Time for another terrible joke. So, there are these two tapeworms in a cow. They are talking and just generally gossiping a bunch. One of them tells the other something shocking. The other tapeworm says. "Where did you ever hear that" The first tapeworm replies. "I heard it through the bovine." I'll show myself out now.
