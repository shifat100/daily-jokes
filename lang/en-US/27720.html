English (US) Joke

Hemp in the Hotel. Mary Juana owned a hotel called Hero Inn. This hotel had 420 rooms, each costing $420. This particular hotel had one rule, and one rule only: No Drugs. A man is on a business trip with his buddies from Caine Co. He finds Hero Inn and decided to stay there for the night. There is only one room that is not booked, and that is... Room 420. He enters the room, sets down his luggage, showers, and lies down on his bed. However, he is unable to sleep because of a foul odor in the room. He checks the drawers, closets, cabinets, anything for this stench. He finally looks under his bed, and discovers a huge package of cannabis. He is well aware of the rule of no drugs, so he decides to report to Mary. After hearing this, Mary calls all the previous inhabitants of Room 420. After careful investigation by the police, they catch the man who brought drugs to Hero Inn. After all this, Mary would like to reward the businessman. She offers him money, and he accepts. She states "Thank you for helping us. I'm grateful that you helped us weed him out."
