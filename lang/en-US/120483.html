English (US) Joke

Some bored kids were throwing bricks off an overpass... ...and hitting cars on the roof as they went by. Eventually however, they ran out of bricks, so they tied a string to one, threw it, and then pulled it back up. Well, as they were pulling to back up, an eighteen wheeler changed lanes right into the brick, which was hanging above the cars. Because the eighteen wheeler was taller tho, it smacked it right in the windshield and got tangled on the mirror. The kid freaked out and tried to pull back, but the truck was obviously too much and pulled his arm off. After the ambulance and the police came, the kid was taken to the hospital. Then, the police started to arrest the truck driver. "What the hell did I do??" "Sir, you're under arrest for arm robbery"
