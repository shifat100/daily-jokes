English (US) Joke

The first explorers of Canada When the first explorers of the new world stepped foot on this great land they were presented the task of naming it. Since no-one had any previous experience naming a new land they came up with the compromise of putting all the letter of the alphabet into a hat, whatever the hat spelled they would name the land that. *The first letter was pulled* "It's a 'C', eh." *The second letter was pulled* "It's an 'N', eh." *Finally the last letter was pulled* "It's an 'D', eh." The listeners jotted down the new world's name and proclaimed **The New Land shall be called CANADA!**
