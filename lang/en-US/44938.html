English (US) Joke

So foreigners are on a boat... Several foreigners from all around the world are on a boat. The boat begins to sink and the passengers must drop some of their cargo: The American drops his Iphone, Japanese: "Why'd you drop your Iphone?" American: "Don't worry, we've got many Iphones in America!" The Japanese man drops his laptop, American: "And why'd you drop you laptop?" Japanese: "Ha! We've got many computers in Japan!" Everyone on the boat now looks at each other trying to figure out what to drop. The Filipino, not knowing what to drop, pushes a Korean off the boat. Everyone now looks at the Filipino, astonished. "Don't worry! We've got many Koreans in the Philippines!"
