English (US) Joke

Your Mother... - Yo mama is hairy, she shaves her legs with a weed wacker. - Yo mama is so fat, when she jumped in the air she got stuck. - Yo mama is so stupid, she tripped over a cordless phone. - Yo mama is so poor, when came over I stepped on a cigarette butt she said "who turned off the heat". - Yo mama is so fat when I walked around her I got lost. - Yo mama is so fat and stupid, when I said it was chilly out, she ran outside with a bowl. - Yo mama is so dumb she tried to drown a fish. - Yo mama is ugly, when she goes shopping they ask *her* for a bag. - Yo mama is so ugly, she got fired from the zoo. - You mama is so fat, when she jumped in front of my car, I swerved and ran out of gas. Happy Mothers day everybody!
