English (US) Joke

Was in town last night... I somehow managed to pull this absolute stunner of a lassie. Long story short, turns out she was a lady boy. But I mean, she looked like a woman, spoke like a woman, kissed like a woman... It was only when she drove us back to her place and managed to reverse the car perfectly into the garage that I stopped and thought "wait, hang on a minute!"
