English (US) Joke

A guy is standing in the street shouting out "I am God. I am God." The police call a social worker who comes over to see if he can help, Walking up to the guy he asks "What is your name?" "I am God." the guy replies getting agitated.. The social worker says "Calm down. Why don't we go into this coffee shop, sit down and have a talk. As soon as they enter the shop the barista looks at the guy and exclaims "Oh God. Not you again.?
