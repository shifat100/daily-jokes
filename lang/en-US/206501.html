Ring - No Ring As the Broadway showgirls were dressing for a performance, one of them noticed her friend was no longer sporting a flashy engagement ring. "What happened, Lily?" she asked, pointing to the bare finger. "The wedding off?" "Yeah," Lily admitted. "I saw him in a bathing suit last week, and he looked so different without his wallet."
