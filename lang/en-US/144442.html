English (US) Joke

Black Panties? Two older gentlemen were sitting on a park bench just passing the time. One of them exclaimed, "Look at that young woman sitting over there wearing the short skirt. She has her legs spread so far I can see her black panties!" The other man looked and said, "I see her but you're wrong. She doesn't even have on panties, she just has a black bush!" As they looked and argued a small boy came by on a bicycle and they called him over. They explained their argument and offered the boy $5 to ride by the woman, take a quick look, come back and settle the argument. When the boy returned they gave him his money and asked who was right. The boy replied, "You're both wrong, it's flies!"
