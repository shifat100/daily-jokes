English (US) Joke

A man worked for a road crew. One day he woke up ill... ...with a touch of laryngitis - but being a dedicated employee he went to work. The boss felt rather sorry for him and didn't want him to do any physical labor - as they were repairing a part of the freeway. He says, "Why don't you go down the road and tell people to slow down going through the construction?" The worker is glad for the easy day. He stops the first vehicle: "Sir," he whispers, his throat feeling worse, "please slow down, there's a road crew up ahead." "Okay," the driver whispers back, "I'll try not to wake them."
