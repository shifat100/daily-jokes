English (US) Joke

A man is sitting at a courthouse... A man is sitting at a courthouse waiting for his trial after calling the Queen a swine. They finally call him, the queen is sitting at her throne, and three officials are in front of him and the man asks: -"So I can't call the Queen a swine anymore?" One of the officials says: -"No you can't, and you will have to pay a fine." The man replies: -"Ok. But let me ask you something: If I want to call a swine "queen", is that ok ?" -"Yes... I suppose." Says the confused official The man looks up to the queen's throne -"Ok then. Goodbye, Queen!"
