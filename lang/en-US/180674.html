English (US) Joke

A man enters a variety show With his miniature companion, a small foot tall man and a miniature piano, not more than 20 inches across. The mini man sits at his mini stool and plays the piano perfectly-- a classic Motzart concerto. He wins the $5,000 top prize and the emcee asks, "So how did you come across a miniature man that plays the piano so well!?" "Well, I found an old oil lamp and freed a Genie inside, so he granted me one wish-- unfortunately he was hard of hearing." "How's that?" The emcee asked. "You Think I wished for a 12 inch PIANIST?!"
