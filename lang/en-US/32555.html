English (US) Joke

A Professor Calls "Pencils Down" A professor calls pencils down and one students keeps writing. When the student goes to turn in his exam, the professor tells him "l'm not going to accept this, you didn't put your pencil down when I said to." "Do you have any idea who I am?" The student says, snobbily. "I don't have the slightest idea who you are and I don't care," the professor retorted. "Good." The student replied as he slipped his exam into the middle of the stack and walked away.
