English (US) Joke

A white guy, a black guy, an Asian guy, and a Mexican are on a tall roof They are told by Satan to either kill themselves or rot in hell for all of eternity. Flustered the Mexican jumps off and says "This is for my people" The Asian trying to be honorable jumps off and again says "This is for my people" Then it is the black guys turn he looks down and says "This is for my people" And throws the white guy off. Satan being amused lets the black guy go to heaven finally racist people get rewarded.
