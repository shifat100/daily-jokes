English (US) Joke

The Cowardly Lion, Aslan, and Cecil are having drinks at a bar ... They all look worn out so the bartender asks them why they look so beat. The Cowardly Lion goes first and says, "Man, you have no idea what I went through just to get courage." Aslan then chimes in quickly after that and says, "Nonsense, you have no idea what I went through just to get Narnia united." There's a pause so they all look at Cecil. He raises his eyes from his drink and quietly says, "Oh yeah? You have no idea what I went through just to get a cavity filled."
