English (US) Joke

In General A blonde is taking an interview, and the examiner is asking some general questions. "What is the boiling temperature of water?" "I am not sure." "Miss, either you know it or you don't know it." "No, I am still not sure." "The boiling temperature of water is 100 degrees!" "Ok, so it's the right angle that boils at 90 degrees?"
