English (US) Joke

A professor of a class... ...is giving a lecture to a very diverse group of students: an African student, a Haitian student, a Chinese student, and an American student. He says to the class, "Today we are going to have an open discussion about the relief of world hunger in other countries." The African student says, "Hunger? What's that?" The Haitian student says, "Relief? What's that?" The Chinese student says "Open discussion? What's that?" The American student says "There are other countries?"
