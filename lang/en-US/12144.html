English (US) Joke

A woman go to see her Doctor " Doctor, , remember last week you gave me some tablets to help my husband to spark up his love life, I want to make a complaint" "why's that", said the Doctor, didn't they work?" "well yes they did, I put them in his food at dinner, we drank a little wine, and then he swept the dishes off the table and we made passionate love". "but Mrs. Jones", said the Doctor, Why are you complaining, isn't that what you wanted, aren't you pleased?" "Yes I'm pleased, but the manager of the local Chinese restaurant wasn't too happy".
