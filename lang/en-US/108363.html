English (US) Joke

Which of us were you talking about? A young woman married the love of her life. They raised a beautiful family of six children. They lived a happy life until the father passed away unexpectedly. Years passed and the woman met another fine man and found love again. They, too, we're married and started having children. Coincidentally, she birthed six more children. Life was good again, until several years later, husband number two also passed. Shortly thereafter the woman succumbed to the heartache of finding and then losing true love twice and she passes away. During the eulogy at her funeral, the pastor spoke the phrase, "finally, they are together again." Curious, all twelve of the woman's children approached the pastor after the service. Speaking for the entire group, the eldest child asked, "Reverend, when you said, 'they are together again,' did you mom was with our father or theirs?" "Neither," he replied, "I was talking about her knees."
