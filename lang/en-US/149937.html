English (US) Joke

A leper is sitting at a lunch counter eating soup. Suddenly the tip of his nose falls off, *plunk*, right in his bowl. Yet he keeps on eating. The guy to his right starts gagging. Then one of the leper's eyelids falls off, *plunk*. Again he goes on slurping. The guy on his left starts dry heaving. Suddenly the leper stops and says, "I'm sorry gentlemen, am I making you sick by eating here?" "It's not that," replies one of the fellows. "It's the guy behind you dipping chips in your back!"
