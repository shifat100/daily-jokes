English (US) Joke

Collection of band jokes: I used to be in a Jazz band and there were a bunch of band jokes our conductor made, here are the ones I can remember: How do you know if a drummer is at your door? He never knows when to come in. How many bass players does it take to change a light bulb? None, the piano player can do it with his left hand. What did the drummer get on his IQ test? Drool. What do you call a guitarist without a girlfriend? Homeless. Why's is bad to have a singer for a roommate? They always lose track of the key. Yeah so these are the only ones I can remember, my conductor played trombone, like me, and as such tended to rip into the rhythm section a bit.
