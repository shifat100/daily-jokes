Being a die-hard Catholic, the man squeals. *Oh my gosh! The Pope is on this very train!* He then looks up and sees that the Pope is actually in the same car as him. *Wow! He's in the same car as me! Boy, would it be awesome if he sat right ne-* He was cut off when someone sat next to him, and, coincidentally, it was the Pope.
