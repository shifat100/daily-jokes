English (US) Joke

Peanuts A tour bus driver is driving with a bus load of seniors down a highway. A little old lady tapped on his shoulder and offers a handful of raw **peanuts**, which he gratefully munches up. After about 15 minutes, she taps him on his shoulder again and she hands him another handful of peanuts. She repeats this gesture about five more times. When she is about to hand him another batch again he asks the little old lady, *"Why then don't you eat the peanuts yourself?"* "We can't chew them because we've no teeth" she replied. The puzzled driver asks, "Why do you buy them then?" The old lady replied, "We just love the chocolate around them."
