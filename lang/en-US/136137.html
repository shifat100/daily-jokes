English (US) Joke

New medical students A group of doctors in training and their teacher are standing in a circle in the lab around a cadaver. The teacher tells his students: "it's very important that you feel completely at ease with the corpse", so he puts his finger up the ass of the corpse, takes it out and licks it. "Your turn now" he tells his students. And they do it, one after another they all put their finger up the ass of the corpse and lick it. When the last student has done it, the teacher has one final thing to say: "it's also important that you observe carefully what I do. I inserted my index finger and licked my MIDDLE finger. Try to pay attention next time..."
