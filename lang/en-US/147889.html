English (US) Joke

Elderly couple is driving down the road and get pulled over. The officer says "sir. You were doing 65 in a 45". Old lady in the passenger seat says "What did he say?" Man says "he said I was speeding" Officer says "I'm gonna have to issue you a citation." Old lady, "what did he say?" Man "he's giving me a ticket" As the officer is filling out his paperwork he notices the couple is from Cornhusker, Iowa. Officer says "I've been there. Worst piece of ass I ever had, I got there." Old lady, "what did he say?" Old man "He thinks he knows you."
