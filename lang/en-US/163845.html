Two girlfriends meet each other on the street. Two girlfriends meet each other on the street. A: How is your married life? B: For the last couple of weeks my husband has been helping me a lot around the house: watching kids, cooking, groceries, cleaning, doing laundry… A: How did you convince him to do that? B: He read an article in one magazine that if a woman is less tired at the end of the day, then she is much more active in bed. A: And, did it help him? B: I don't know yet. He falls asleep as soon as he hits bed.. =/
