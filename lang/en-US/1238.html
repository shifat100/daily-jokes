English (US) Joke

A rabbi and a priest A rabbi Asked a priest: >Could you ever be promoted in your church? The priest answered, >Well, I could become a bishop The Rabbi asked, >And then? The preist said, >Well, I suppose I could even be an archbishop The rabbi said, >And if you were to be promoted again? The priest answered, >Well, then I would be a cardinal The rabbi inquired once again, to which the priest answered, >I could become the Pope The rabbi asked, >And then? The priest threw up his hands and said, >what more could I become? God himself?! The rabbi calmly replied, >One of our boys made it.
