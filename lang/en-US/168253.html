English (US) Joke

So a man walks into the bar..... he sits down and immediately starts to cry. So the bartender asked, "Hey man are you okay? What happened?" after this, the man immediately pulls out a little man playing a piano. Then the bartender exclaims, "That's amazing. But still why are you crying?" then the man sobs, "Well, I met this genie and she gave me this little man on the piano." The bartender still didn't know why the man was sad and before he could say anything else the man said, "Do you think I asked for a 12-inched pianist?"
