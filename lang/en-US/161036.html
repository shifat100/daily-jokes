English (US) Joke

So Rob Ford went to the cancer clinic... ...at one o'clock, and signed in with the receptionist. She told him he'd be waiting a while, since his appointment wasn't until two. "Is this some kind of joke?" He asked, "Can't I get in now?" "No joke, Mr. Mayor" she said, "it's too soon."
