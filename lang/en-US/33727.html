English (US) Joke

Two nuns are remodeling a new complex for the blind. They are painting and get really hot and decide to take off their habits. A little bit later due to the heat they decide to just get completely naked and paint. Later that afternoon there is a knock on the door, in a panic the nuns ask "who is it?" Man replies "blind guy". They breath a sigh of relief and let him in. He walks in and says " hey nice tits where do you want the blinds?"
