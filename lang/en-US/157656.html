Three Nuns Three nuns died in a car accident. On the way to heaven, there was a man who said "To go to heaven you must each answer a question." So the first nuns question was who was the first man in earth. She replied "Oh, that's easy the first man on earth was Adam." The second nuns question was who was the first woman on earth. She replied "Oh, that's simple the first women on earth was Eve." The last nuns question was what was the first thing that Eve said to Adam. Her reply was "Oh, that's a HARD one." Hahaha
