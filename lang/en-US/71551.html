Tithes A man was praying saying Lord if I pick up 10 dollars I'll pay 1 dollar as tithes. If I pick up 100 dollars I'll pay 10 dollars as tithes. If I pick up 1000 dollars I'll pay 100 dollars as tithes. 2 weeks later he picked up 900 and he said "The Lord doesn't trust me, he has already taken his 100 for tithes"
