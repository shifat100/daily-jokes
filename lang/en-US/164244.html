English (US) Joke

A mother tries to convince her son to go to school Early one morning a mother went to her sleeping son and woke him up, “Wake up, son. It’s time to go to school.” Son: “Awww Mom! I don’t want to go to school.” Mom: “Give me two reasons why you don’t want to go to school.” Son: “One, all the children hate me. Two, all the teachers hate me.” Mom: “Oh, that’s not a reason. Come on, you have to go to school.” Son: “ Okay, you give me two good reasons why I should go to school!" Mom: One, you are FIFTY-TWO years old, And should understand your responsibilities. Two, You're the principal of the school! "
