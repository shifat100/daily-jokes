English (US) Joke

A hot woman was standing in a bus. A kid saw her standing, got up and said, "hey lady, you can sit here. I've vacated my place for you." At this, woman got real livid and slapped the kid. "These days you can't even be nice to anybody", said the boy and went back to sit on his father's lap.
