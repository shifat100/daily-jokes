English (US) Joke

Three religious leaders are golfing. After the fourth hole, they are told that they need to wait to continue in order to accommodate a group of blind golfers. [One religious leader] opines, "To see people overcoming their disabilities to do something they love truly demonstrates the compassion of [diety]." "It's wonderful to see that they have a chance to experience this wonderful day that [other diety] has created," answers [next religious leader]. [Leader of religion to be deprecated] responds, "Ah, fuck 'em, why don't they golf at night?"
