English (US) Joke

Three fetuses were talking about their futures... "I'm gonna be a doctor when I grow up, and bring babies into the world" said the first one. "I'm gonna be a preacher, and baptize babies!" said the next. "I'm gonna be a murderer!" said the last. "A murderer!" exclaimed the first two. "Why?" "I'm gonna kill the guy who's poking me in the back every night!"
