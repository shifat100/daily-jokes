They decide they're going to escape! So, like, they get up onto the roof, and there, just across this narrow gap, they see the rooftops of town, stretching away in the moonlight... stretching away to freedom. Now, the first guy, he jumps right across with no problem. But his friend, his friend daredn't make the leap. Y'see... Y'see, he's afraid of falling. So then, the first guy has an idea... He says "Wh-what do you think I am? Crazy?" "You'd turn it off when I was halfway across!"
