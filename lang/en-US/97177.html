English (US) Joke

Computer trouble I was having trouble with my computer. So I called Joseph, the 14 year old next door whose bedroom looks like Mission Control, and asked him to come over. Joseph clicked a couple of buttons and solved the problem. As he was walking away, I called after him, "So, what was wrong?" He replied, "It was an ID ten T error." I didn't want to appear stupid, but nonetheless inquired, "An ID Ten T error? What's that? In case I need to fix it again." Joseph grinned, "Haven't you ever heard of an ID ten T error before?" "No", I replied. "Write it down," he said, "and I think you'll figure it out." So I wrote down: I D 1 0 T I used to like that little boy.
