English (US) Joke

Daddy where do people come from? Young boy is curious and asked his father where people come from dad says "well son it goes way back to Adam and eve and they were mother and father to everyone" Young boy still confused asks his mother "Mommy where do people come from?" She responds with "well after years of evolution we evolved from monkeys and gorillas" The young boy even more confused now goes back to his father for and explanation and he replys "well son your mother was talking about her side of the family"
