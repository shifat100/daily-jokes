English (US) Joke

Decision A Man had 4 Girlfriends. Yara, Samantha, Jasmin and Lollie. One day he makes up his mind to get married and get settled. But the problem was he couldn't decide which one to marry. After pondering for couple of days taking advice from his friends and family he is still left indecisive. So he comes up his own version of test. He give each of these 4 girls $1000.00 and ask them to spend on anything they like. After few days he ask each one of them what did they do with the money? - Yara said, she invested the money for growth. So they can have a better future together. - Samantha said, she wanted to look more beautiful for her man so she spend it on cosmetic surgery. - Jasmin said, She bought more jewelry. - Lollie said, She planned a perfect trip.
