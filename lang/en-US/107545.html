English (US) Joke

The new barman is black The new barman in the pub is black, so I said to him, "Beer please, nigger." He hit the roof and said, "Why don't we swap places, let's see how you like it." So I went round the bar, he walked out then came back in and said, "Beer please, honkey." I said, "Sorry, mate, we don't serve niggers in here."
