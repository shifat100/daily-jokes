A man walks in a restaurant and orders a soup. The waiter brings the soup over and says: Waiter: Enjoy your soup. Will there be anything else? Man: Actually, yes. There appears to be a fly in my soup. Waiter: Oh, no sir. That is mearly artistic representation of a fly on the bowl. Man: But...It's moving. Waiter: It's a GIF, sir. Before the man could protest the fly manages to reach the top of the bowl and fly out. Waiter: 3D!!
