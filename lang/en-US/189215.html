English (US) Joke

The bitch A man walks into a bar with a St. Bernard and asks for a martini. The dog requests a double martini. The bartender says to the man "So you're a ventriloquist. Big deal. We don't serve dogs in here." The man gets up to go to the men's room, and the dog again requests his drink. The bartender is amazed. "Good God, you can talk. Will you do a favor for me?" The dog replies, "What's in it for me?" The bartender goes to the cash register and takes out a $20 bill. "Here's $20. Go across the street to Riley's bar and tell the guys in there that they are a bunch of wimps and that our softball team will whip their asses when we play them this weekend." The dog goes out. His owner returns and asks "Where's Rex?" The bartender says "He went across the street to do me a favor." The owner is visibly upset and says "I don't let Rex out alone!" The dog's owner leaves immediately to retrieve Rex, but Rex is not across the street. He is in the gutter in front of the bar going at it fast and hard with an Irish Setter. The owner is shocked. "Rex, what's come over you? You've never done anything like this before." Rex responds, "I didn't have the money before."
