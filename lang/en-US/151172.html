Its about time we started swearing A 5 year old and a 3 year old are upstairs in their bedroom ‘You know what?’ says the 5 year old, ‘I think it’s about time we started swearing.’ The 3 year old nods his head in approval, so the 5 year old says, ‘When we go downstairs for breakfast I’m gonna swear first, then you swear after me, ok?’ ‘Ok’ the 3 year old, agrees with enthusiasm. The mother walks into the kitchen and asks the 5 year old what he wants for breakfast. ‘Sh*t mum, I don’t know, I suppose I’ll have some Fruit Loops ‘ WHACK…she spanks him He flew out of his chair, tumbled across the kitchen floor, got up, and ran upstairs crying his eyes out.
