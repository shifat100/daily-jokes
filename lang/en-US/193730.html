English (US) Joke

A Guy is Walking Through a Marina He looks over and sees a Boat with AMB on the prow. As he's thinking about it.... American ships are called USS...United States Ship British are called HMS...Her Majesty's Ship For the life of him he could not place what AMB would stand for. So he calls down to the gentleman standing on the boat: "Excuse me but what does the label "AMB" stand for?" The guy looks up and yells, "ATSA MY BOAT!!!"
