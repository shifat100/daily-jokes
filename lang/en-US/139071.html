English (US) Joke

A bucket goes to the doctor The bucket was feeling ill, and decided to go to a physician. The doctor, seeing as this was a new patient, asked him, "tell me about yourself first." "Well, I can hold about 1/2 a gallon of liquid. I'm 3 years old, and I have to tell you, I feel pretty under the weather." The doctor replied, "I can tell. You seem to be a little pail."
