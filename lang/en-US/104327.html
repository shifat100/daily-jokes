Sikh There was a sikh who worked in an IT shop. He had few colleagues working with him. There was a female colleague in the office he used to like. One day, he went up to her and started talking and it looked like the girl actually fancies him. So all of a sudden he asks the girl, " There will be no one at my house tonight. You wanna come?" The girl feels shocked first but she too likes the guy and says " Yea sure id love to" The sikh then says his goodbye and leaves. The girl was very excited abt it and around 8'o clock she finally leaves her house. Once she has arrived, she knocks on the door and nothing. She goes around the house and knocks on the back door and nothing. There was no one at the house.
