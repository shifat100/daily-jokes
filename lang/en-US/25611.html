English (US) Joke

A guy has just moved in with his girlfriend. "Will you go shoe shopping with me tomorrow?" Girlfriend asks after a particularly debauched session of lovemaking. Not wanting to put her off, the man replies: "Sure. Where do you want to go?" The girlfriend slowly plays out the line, "First the discount store on Broadway, and then I wanted to stop by the big shopping center downtown." "Won't need to do the second one," the boyfriend quickly observes. The girlfriend, having anticipated his reluctance immediately challenges: "Why not?" "Because once you've seen one shoe store, you've seen the mall."
