English (US) Joke

Getting older Two middle-aged brothers were chatting about getting older and life in general. The elder brother says, “Jeez – it seems like you’ve gotten a little grayer on top just since the last time I’ve seen you.” Without missing a beat the younger brother turns toward his balding elder brother and says, “I hadn’t noticed – why don’t you nod your head for me so I can take a look?”
