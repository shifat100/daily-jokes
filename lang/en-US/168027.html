English (US) Joke

A mathematician and a farmer are sitting in a train... Suddenly, the train comes to a stop. The mathematician looks out the window and sees a herd of sheep. - Let's see if we can count how many sheep are in that herd. The mathematician busts out a calculator, a pen and a notebook and starts calculating furiously. The farmer just stares out the window. After a few minutes, the train starts moving again. The mathematician says: - I couldn't count them all. I bet you didn't either. The farmer gives the mathematician a blank stare before replying: - There were 176 sheep in the herd. The mathematician's eyes grow wide. He is completely puzzled. After a few seconds, he asks the farmer how did he manage to count all the sheep. The farmer replies: - It's simple, son! You count all the legs and divide by four!
