English (US) Joke

Airplane passengers watch nervously As two men wearing pilots' uniforms and dark glasses use canes to feel their way into the cockpit. &nbsp; The plane starts barreling down the runway, and the passengers begin to get scared as the water at the end of the airstrip nears. With only a few yards left, everyone screams, but the plane lifts off just in time. The passengers think it was all a joke, while in the cockpit, the pilots high five. &nbsp; "You know," says one pilot to the other, "one day they're gonna scream too late, and we're gonna die."
