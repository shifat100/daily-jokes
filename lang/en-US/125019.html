English (US) Joke

Two Amish boys are walking one day They got a goat in a wagon and are pulling it along. They go down a hill and the boy with the wagon loses his grip. The wagon is speeding down the hill with the goat in it while the boys chase after it. The wagon reaches the bottom and one of the front wheels hits a rock sticking out of the ground sending the goat flying into a fence near the hill. The boys catch up with their goat and sees that its head is stuck in the fence. The first boy says to the second: "What do we do?" the second boy looks around to see if anyone is close by then goes over to the goat, drops his pants, and starts fucking it. He finishes and turns to the other boy and asks: "You want a turn?" the other boy replies: "Do I have to stick my head in the fence?"
