English (US) Joke

Definition of Service I became confused when I heard the word "Service" being used with these agencies: 1. Internal Revenue "Service" 2. U.S. Postal "Service" 3. Telephone "Service" 4. Cable T.V. "Service" 5. Civil "Service" 6. State, City, County & Public "Service" 7. Customer "Service" This is not what I thought "Service" meant. But today I overheard two farmers talking, and one of them said he had hired a bull to "Service" a few cows. ……….BAM!.......It all came into focus for me.
