English (US) Joke

Breakfast Wife (Overheard at work) I was eating breakfast at a dinner with the old lady when the man at the next booth says to his wife, "Please pass the sugar, sugar". A short time later at the booth on the opposite side of me, the man says to his wife, "Please pass the honey, honey". Annoyed, my wife says to me "How come you never talk sweet to me like that?" "Ok", I say, "Please pass the bacon, pig."
