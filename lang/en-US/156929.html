English (US) Joke

A man had send his youngest daughter off to college... and without his wife, was experiencing 'empty nest syndrome.' He got online and visited some chat rooms, where his screen name was "WhiteKnight." He found a kindred spirit, BlueButterfly.A lady who seemed to have grown up with him. A woman with whom he shared everything. They decided to meet at a hotel. The night finally came; WhiteKnight knocked on the hotel door... Blue Butterfly opened it, looked out with wide eyes, and said.... "DADDY?????"
