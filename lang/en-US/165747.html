English (US) Joke

A joke from my Philosophy textbook The employer introduced himself to his new gardener. "I am a professor of logic," the employer said. "Oh. What's that?" the gardener asked. "I shall give you a demonstration," announced the professor. "Do you own a wheelbarrow?" "Yes," replied the gardener. "Then I infer you are a hard worker," the professor continued. "And from that I infer you have a family. And from that I infer you are conscientious and responsible. And from that I infer you are a conservative. Am I right?" "Wow!" exclaimed the gardener. "That's right! So that's logic?" "That's logic," preened the professor. Later the gardener met up with one of his buddies and told him he had a job with a professor of logic. "Logic?" his friend asked. "What's that?" "I'll show you," the gardener said. "Do you own a wheelbarrow?" "No." "Stupid liberal."
