English (US) Joke

Little Johnny came home from school. "I failed math today." He told his father. "What?! How did you fail math?!" His father asked. Johnny explained, "Well, she asked me what three times two is. I said six." His father nodded, "Yeah, that's the right answer. And...?" "Then she asked me what two times three is." "What the fuck is the difference?" Johnny exclaimed, "That's what I said!"
