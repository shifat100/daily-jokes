English (US) Joke

Mickey's Piss Mickey was having a great day, enjoying the end of a snow storm when he decided it was time to go and shovel the driveway. When he got outside, he noticed something that infuriated him. Goofy's name was written in piss on his side walk. Mickey went from angry to sad when he realized not only was it Goofy's piss, but it was in Minnie's handwriting.
