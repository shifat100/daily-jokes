English (US) Joke

Soviet propaganda short fim titled: Mercifulness of our Great Leader Stalin Two kids run into Stalin's study, saying: "Uncle Stalin, uncle Stalin, give us some sweets, please!" Stalin, distracted, looks angrily at the kids and shouts: "Out of my study, right now!" Children scared off and ran back immediately. Voiceover concludes: "Yet he could kill them."
