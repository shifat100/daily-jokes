Here's why: The population of this country is 7 million. 1 million are retired. That leaves 6 million to do the work. Of this there are 1/2 million employed by the government, leaving 1 million to do the work. 1/2 million are in the armed forces, which leaves 1/2 million to do the work. Take from the total the 400,000 people who are unemployed, and that leaves 100,000 to do the work.
