English (US) Joke

Yet another lady goes to the pet shop and sees a parrot in a cage... The lady admires the bird for a moment or two before noticing that the parrot had a ribbon tied to either leg. One was red, and one was green. The lady signals the owner of the pet store, curious about her discovery. "Why does this parrot have ribbons tied to its legs?" The lady asked. "Oh, you see," the pet order enthusiastically responds, "what we have here is a very special, very talented parrot. If you were to pull the red ribbon, she would recite the United State's Pledge of Allegiance, and if you were to pull the green ribbon, she would beautifully sing the song *Greensleeves.*" "That's very fascinating, but..." the woman inquired, "what would happen if I were to pull both ribbons?" The parrot squawked. *"I'd fall off of my perch, dumbass."*
