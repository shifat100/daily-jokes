English (US) Joke

A kindergarten teacher asks her students what animals provide us... She said, "What does a chicken give us?" and the students replied, "Eggs". She then asked, "What does a pig give us?" and the students replied a joyous "Bacon". Finally she asked "What does a cow give us?" and before anyone could answer little Johnny said "Homework". Joke provided by my ten year old son.
