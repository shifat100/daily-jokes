English (US) Joke

Six weeks ago, my brother was deployed by Afghanistan... And today he called home. He asked me how things were back here, and so I took a deep breath and told him the truth. "Your cat died." My brother was aghast, especially at how coldly I said it. "Joe, that's not how you break bad news." Miles away, I could hear the frustration in his voice. He sighed and explained: "You should've started with something innocent and then gone from there. Don't open with the worst news. Say something like 'Well, the cat got on the roof... we tried to get her... then she slipped and we couldn't save her'. Understand?" I told him I understood and thanked him for the advice. "Alright," he said, "How's mom doing?" "Well, she got on the roof..."
