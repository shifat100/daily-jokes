speeding.

A woman was speeding down the motorway one evening, when a police officer on a bike pulled her over. He started writing out a ticket and asked... "ma'm, do you know how fast you were going? " "Oh, I'm terribly sorry officer, I guess it was over the limit, I didn't notice, I'm in such a hurry" "and where are you hurrying to tonight?" "Well actually my father was a policeman, and I'm late for the annual secret Policeman's ball" The officer get's a bit angry, that she's clearly telling a lie to get out of a ticket, so he responds "Policemen don't have Balls!" at that he tares up the ticket, walks back to his bike and never speaks about the incident again.
