English (US) Joke

There's a woman who gets dropped off at my building for work every day by the husband. And every day the husband will come out and go to the passenger side to open the car door for her. Seeing so much love from the husband I decided to ask her whether this has always been the way. She replied no, it only started two years ago. I asked "What made him change?" The woman replied "Change? the inside door handle stopped working two years ago. He didn't want to change it."
