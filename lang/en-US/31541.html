English (US) Joke

Bobo the Clown A man goes to his psychiatrist and says he is depressed. The psychiatrist says the circus is in town and he should go see the funniest clown in the world, "Bobo the Clown," to cheer him up. The man replies, "but doctor, I *am* Bobo the clown."
