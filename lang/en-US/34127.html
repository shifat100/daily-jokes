English (US) Joke

A guy on a train is sitting next to a nun who is doing a crossword puzzle. The nun is stuck on a clue that doesn't fit with some other answers that she's already written down. She asks the man for help. "Let's backtrack, and double check that your other answers are right. That usually helps me," he says. "Good idea," the nun says. "How about this one: A four-letter word, ending in 'U-N-T,' and the clue is 'Something you would call a woman.'" "Aunt?" The man says. "Oh, right..." the nun says. "Say, do you have an eraser?"
