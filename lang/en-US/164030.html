English (US) Joke

Strap in folks. I've got a hipster joke for ya. Several years ago a friend and I strolled into a bar called the "Jollie Rouge" in Asheville, NC, grabbed a couple stools and ordered some beer. A little later, a bald man not dissimilar in appearance to Michael Stipe sat down on the stool between my friend and I, and bellied up to the bar. My friend shot me a knowing look. This guy is Michael Stipe. Athens, GA is not too far from here and being in Asheville.. this situation is not a long shot. So, I summoned my courage, guzzled the last of my beverage and audibly swallowed past the lump in my throat. "I know I don't have to ask this, but I'm going to anyway." "Go ahead. I'm used to it," the man replied. "If you knew Kurt Cobain was going to kill himself to 'Automatic For The People,' would you have made it any better?" The bald man furrowed his brows and birthed a tense, pregnant silence. Finally, after averting his gaze from the side of my head and aiming it down at the bar in front of him, he answered.. "No, I would have made it sooner."
