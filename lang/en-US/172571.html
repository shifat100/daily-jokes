English (US) Joke

Son asks father the difference between Theory & Reality... Father replies, "You want to know the difference between theory & reality, huh... Ok, go upstairs and ask your mother if she would sleep with a stranger for a million dollars." So the son goes upstairs and a few minutes later comes back down. "Dad, Mom said Yes, she would sleep with a stranger for a million dollars." Dad says, "Well there ya go son. In theory we'd be millionaires... In reality, your mother's a whore!"
