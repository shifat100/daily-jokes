English (US) Joke

A guy runs into a saloon and yells "everybody clear out, Big John's comin' to town!" A couple minutes later a mountain of a man rides into town on an ox and he's dragging a mountain lion on a chain behind him. He gets down and punches the ox and slams the mountain lion and says "You guys stay here." He walks into the saloon, ripping the doors off the hinges. Walks up to the bartender grabs him by the shirt and says "Give me a bottle of beer." Bartender does, guy bites the top right off, chugs it down in one gulp and slams it down on the bar. Bartender asks, "Ca-ca-can I getcha another?" Guy says "Naw. I gotta get out of here. Big John's coming to town."
