English (US) Joke

A man goes golfing and finds another man crying... > A veteran golfer is playing on a new course with some very difficult holes. He arrives at a 700 yard par 5 only to find another golfer sitting at the tee pad openly weeping. Before he can ask the crying man what's wrong, the man says "My ball... it went in." >The veteran golfer is awestruck. "You got a hole in one! That's amazing. Tell me how it happened!" >The man on the ground replies. "I hooked my drive in to the lake and got mad. So I tried to kick the tee, but instead I slipped and fell on it. My ball... it went in..."
