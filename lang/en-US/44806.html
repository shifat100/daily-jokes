English (US) Joke

3 guys get stranded on an island 3 guys (for this joke we will call them Joe, Ben and Eric) get stranded on an island inhabited by natives. The natives take the trio to their village to meet the chief of the village. They are told to find 10 of 1 fruit and to bring the fruit back to the village. Joe comes back with 10 apples and is told to put all the apples up his butt without making any facial expressions or he will be killed. He puts the first apple up with no problems but flinches on the second apple and is therefore killed. Ben comes back with 10 cherries and is told to do the same thing. He finds it really easy with cherries and manages to get up to 8 cherries before he starts laughing uncontrollably and therefore killed. Up in heaven, Joe asks Ben why he started laughing, Ben replied saying, because I saw Eric coming back with pineapples.
