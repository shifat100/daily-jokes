English (US) Joke

fləkCHəˈwāSH(ə)n This old Asian woman moves to America and goes to the bank to exchange money. She gives the teller 100 Yen and he gives her $120 dollars. The next day she returns and gives the teller another 100 Yen and he gives her $80 dollars. She gets upset and says to the teller, "what you do to me...what you do to me?" He looks at her with misunderstanding and says "what?" She again says "what you do to me? yesterday to give me one hundred twenty dollar, today you give me eighty dollar!" The teller says "fluctuation". The old woman takes a step back and says "well fuck you white guys too!"
