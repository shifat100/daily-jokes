English (US) Joke

10 blondes walk into a bar... they say to the bartender, "We'll have the most expensive bottle of champagne you have! We're celebrating." They sit down and crack open the bottle and raise their glasses and they all say "23" and drink. The bartender is curious and goes to their table and asks, "What are you celebrating?" They point to a framed puzzle and say, "We'll we were sick of all the negative stereotypes of blondes, so we decided to do this puzzle, it took us only 23 days, but on the box it says 2-3 years!"
