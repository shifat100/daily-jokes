English (US) Joke

There is a shortage of butter in North Korea .. .. Kim Jong Un orders a team of scientists to do something about it urgently. They are tasked with a project of converting shit to butter. After a few months of work, the supreme leader demands a progress report. "Well, the project is almost complete!" "What do you mean by almost?" "We can already spread it!"
