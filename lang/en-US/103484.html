English (US) Joke

A man meets an older woman... A man meets an older woman in the bar of a nice little hotel. It turns out the woman is already over 55, but she's still really hot. After a few drinks, the woman asks the man: "Did you ever try a threesome? What about a mother-daughter combination?" "Never, but I won't say no to it" says the man heatedly. "Then it's your lucky night! Follow me." They go upstairs to the woman's room. She opens the door, turns on the light and says: "Mum, look who I brought!"
