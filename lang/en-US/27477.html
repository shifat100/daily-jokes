English (US) Joke

A guy walks up to a woman at a bar... He flirts with her. He makes small talk but the woman insists she isn't going to go home with him. The guy says, *"What if I offer you a million dollars to sleep with me?"* The woman has never had a million dollars in her life. She stops and considers the offer very seriously. Guy changes his mind, says, *"What if I change my offer to a dollar instead?"* The woman is aghast, "What kind of woman do you think I am?" *"We'd already figured that out. Now we're just negotiating"*
