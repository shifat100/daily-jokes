English (US) Joke

New whore house in town. In town opens new whore house but only with men inside. Women’s from town come to see what is offered. On entrance they get information that on entry of each door is description of size and shape of dicks inside. On each next floor dick are getting better. * First floor. First floor: Small and short. They decide to go up. * Second floor: Long and thin. They say better, but let’s see what is on next floor. * Third floor: Thick and short. This is better, but let see what is on next floor. * Fort floor: Long and thick. Oh very good, but let’s see what is on next floor. * Fifth floor: There is nothing here; this is only proof that women’s are never satisfied.
