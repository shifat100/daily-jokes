English (US) Joke

Job interview Interviewee is doing pretty well when the interviewer asks the classic interview question, "What would you say is your worst quality as an employee." Guy thinks for a minute, sez, "I guess it would have to be honesty." Interviewer sez, "I don't think that's such a bad thing; in fact, I think honesty is a very positive quality." Guy sez, "I don't really give a shit what you think."
