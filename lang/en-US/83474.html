English (US) Joke

Four Engineers get into a car. The car won't start. The mechanical engineer says: "There must be a problem with the gear box." The electrical engineer says: "It must have a broken starter." The chemical engineer says: "There must be something wrong with the fuel" The IT engineer says: "Hey, let's all get out of the car and get back in."
