English (US) Joke

Early in the morning, an East German man named Honecker arrives at his office and opens his window. He greets the Sun, saying: "Good morning, dear Sun!" "Good morning, dear Honecker" the sun replies. Honecker works, and then at noon he heads to the window and says: "Good day, dear Sun!" "Good day, dear Honecker!" the sun replies. In the evening, Honecker calls it a day, and heads once more to the window, and says: "Good evening, dear Sun!" There is no response. Hearing nothing, Honecker says again: "Good evening, dear Sun! What's the matter?" The sun retorts: "Fuck you. I'm in the West now!"
