English (US) Joke

A lawyer, priest, and social worker are on a ship that hits an iceberg... The captain comes over the intercom: "Everyone please make your way to the lifeboats". The social worker yells out: "Women & children first!" The lawyer starts pushing his way towards the boats: "Screw the children!" The priest responds: "Do we have time?"
