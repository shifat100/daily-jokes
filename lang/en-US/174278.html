English (US) Joke

An attorney called the governor just after midnight,... ...insisting that he talk to him urgently. An aide eventually agreed to wake up the governor. "So, what is it?" grumbled the governor. "Judge Jones has just died," said the attorney, "and I want to take his place." Replied the governor: "Well, it's OK with me if it's OK with the undertaker."
