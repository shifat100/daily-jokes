Tom asks 'What happened?" Charlie replies " I decided Id go out and have some drinks. I had way too many and to top it off i had some pretty strong shots with a girl I was talking to." " I wound up getting her back to my place but she fell asleep shortly after we got there. About ten minutes later I started blowing chunks." Tom says " Well? other than the hangover and having thrown up thats not too bad." Charlie looks down and starts sobbing. "Chunks is my dog."
