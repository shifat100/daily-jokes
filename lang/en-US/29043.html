English (US) Joke

Plane lullabies Folks complain about sitting near a crying baby on the plane. But what they need to do is think of the right lullaby to sing to help out the little tyke. *Rock-a-bye baby, up in the sky* *When the plane crashes, Baby will die.* *When the wings break, the cabin will drop,* *And down will come Baby, Mommy, and Pop!* *Rock-a-bye baby, up in the air.* *Where the plane goes, the Devil may care.* *Captain's asleep, the passengers too,* *All the air leaked from a hole in the loo.* *Rock-a-bye baby, ready to nod.* *In arms of good Ahmed, servant of God.* *Laugh at the shiny, glittering knife,* *He's flying the plane for the rest of your life.*
