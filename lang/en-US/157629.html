English (US) Joke

A guy thinks his wife is cheating on him. So he waits for her to leave that night and jumps in a cab to follow her. By following her he finds out she works in a whorehouse. The guy says to the cabbie, ‘Wanna make $100?’ The cabbie says, ‘Sure, what do I have to do?’ The guy replies and says all the cabbie has to do is to go inside the whorehouse, grab the guy’s wife, put her in the back of the cab, and take them home. The cabbie goes in. A couple minutes later the whore house’s doors get kicked open, and the cabbie drags this woman out who is kicking, biting, punching, and fighting all the way to the cab. The cabbie opens the door to the cab, throws the girl inside, and tells the man, ‘Here, hold her!’ The man looks down at the girl and says to the cabbie, ‘THIS ISN’T MY WIFE!’ The cabbie replies, ‘I KNOW, IT'S MINE; I'M GOING BACK IN FOR YOURS!!’ Shamelessly stolen from /u/Zegopher in [this thread](http://www.reddit.com/r/videos/comments/2lvhr1/girl_gets_caught_cheating_by_best_man_story_in/clym8e6).
