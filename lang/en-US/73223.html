English (US) Joke

A man rushes to the hospital after his wife gets in a severe car accident. (one of my favorite jokes, I believe originally from Gilbert Gottfried) Upon arrival the surgeon rushes out to see him. **Surgeon:** Sir, I'm afraid I have terrible news. Your wife was in a severe accident and she is paralyzed from the neck down. From here on out, you'll have to do everything for her: feed her, bath her, clothe her. Your life is going to be very different. **Man:** *(stricken with grief)* This is terrible news! I don't know what I'm going to do! **Surgeon:** Haha! Just joking! She died.
