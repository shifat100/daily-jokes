English (US) Joke

Dimension Dummies The zero(th), first, second, third, and fourth dimension go to a party. A guy comes over and says "can I tell you guys a joke?" The fourth dimension says "No, I don't have time for that." The second dimension says "Chill, bro, you're acting so plain." The first dimension says "Hey guys, you're crossing the line." The third dimension says "Yeah, give him some space." "You've got a point," adds the zero(th) dimension. "Damn dimensions," says the guy "always plotting something."
