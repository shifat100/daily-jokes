English (US) Joke

Inside Knowledge A man joined a big Multi National Company as a trainee. On his first day, he dialed the kitchen and shouted into the phone: "Get me an fucking cup of coffee, quickly!" The voice from the other side responded, "You fool, you've dialed the wrong extension! Do you know who you're talking to?" "No," replied the trainee. "It's the Managing Director of the company, idiot!" The trainee shouted back, "And do you know who YOU are fucking talking to, you idiot?" "No!" replied the Managing Director indignantly. "Thanks for that!" replied the trainee and put down the phone.
