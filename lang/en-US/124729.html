English (US) Joke

Three men are talking in a bar... So one of them says "I once went to a restaurant in Africa. I didn't speak the language, so I had no idea what I had ordered. When the waiter arrived, he held a plate of dog poop! It was the most disgusting meal in my life. So the second man, quite stupid, says "Wow! Really?" And the third man says "I can't believe you actually bought that shit."
