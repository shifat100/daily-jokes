English (US) Joke

So a woman walks into an ice-cream shop.. This dumb blonde bitch walks into an ice-cream shop and asks for a gallon of chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry. Man says "we don't have any chocolate". "Well then just give me half a gallon of chocolate, strawberry, and vanilla." "Ma'am, we don't have any chocolate" "Okay, give me a quart of chocolate, vanilla, and strawberry". He sighs, "Ma'am".. spell the 'van' in 'vanilla'." "v-a-n". "Now spell the 'straw' in 'strawberry'." "s-t-r-a-w." "Now spell the 'fuck' in 'chocolate'." "Well, there ain't no fuck in chocolate!" "That's what I've been trying to tell you!"
