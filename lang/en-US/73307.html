Four guys in a boat, fishing Four guys are out in a boat, fishing, when one of them falls into the water. Two of the other guys jumps into the water to pick him up. After a short while they come back with a lifeless body. They pull him aboard and starts resuscitation. While one of the guys give him mouth-to-mouth, one of the other guys says: - Guys, I think you got the wrong guy. This one's wearing ice skates
