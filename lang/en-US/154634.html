Best Golf Balls in the world (old) At Schvetti, we believe we are the world's best manufacturer of Golf Clubs, Golf Bags and training videos, but where we really excel is our balls. Yes, at Schvetti, we think we've got the best balls in the world, and we think after holding our balls, you'll agree. Headquartered in Stockholm, Sweden, Schvetti recently became one of the largest Golf Ball manufacturers in the world, with two major acquisitions in one month: the Japan-based Ichi Golf Corporation, and the German Haree company. So whether you've got Schvetti balls, Ichi balls or Haree balls, you'll agree, when you hold our balls in your hand, you've got the best balls in the world.
