English (US) Joke

A shepherd and his dog In a sunny day in the fields, there is a shepherd and his dog herding their sheep's. The shepherd asked his dog to round up the sheep to the fields. The dog went out and started herding the sheep's to the field. After a while the dog returned and told the shepherd that he already rounded up 20 sheep's on the field. The shepherd was a bit confused because he did not have that many sheep's so he head out to the fields and counted his sheep. The shepherd counted 15 sheep's and wondered why his dog said 20. He approached his dog and asked "there is only 15 sheep's in the field. Why did you say there were 20?" The dog turns toward the shepherd and said "i know, but i rounded them up"
