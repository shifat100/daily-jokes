What "being a man" is about A little boy asks his father: "Dad, what does it mean to be a man". The father replies: "well son, being a man means that you're the person in control of the situation, you're the one who takes all the important decisions". "Well" - the kid answers - "then I hope to be a great man when I grow up, just like mom is".
