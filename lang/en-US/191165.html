English (US) Joke

A woman is going through labour... ...but there is a blackout and her car isn't working. Her husband attempts to deliver the baby. Their young daughter is asked to hold a torch so that her father can see. After a long and stressful procedure, the baby boy is born. The man spanks the newly born child and the baby starts crying. The mother asks the daughter about what she just saw. "Smack him again, he shouldn't of crawled up there in the first place."
