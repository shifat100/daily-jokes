English (US) Joke

A man decides to venture into the south seas to claim his red wings... and even though his wife is on her period, he insists. Mid act the door bell rings and then man goes to answer the door. "I'm sorry, I was just eating a jam sandwich and got some on my face" he explains to the post man. The post man replies "It looks like you also got some peanut butter on your chin."
