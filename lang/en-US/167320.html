English (US) Joke

A woman has twins and gives them up for adoption... One of them goes to a family in Egypt and is named “Ahmal”. The other goes to a family in Spain; they name him “Juan”. Years later, Juan sends a picture of himself to his birth mother. Upon receiving the picture, she tells her husband that she wishes that she also had a picture of Ahmal. Her husband responds “They’re twins! If you’ve seen Juan, you’ve seen Ahmal.”
