English (US) Joke

Sean and Seamus open a pub... ...but it's not very successful. In the first week they'd only gotten three customers. By Saturday night and looking at an empty bar, Sean turns to Seamus and says, "See? I told you we should have opened a brothel." Seamus says, "That's ridiculous. If they're not coming in for beer, they certainly wouldn't come in for broth!"
