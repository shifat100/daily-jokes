English (US) Joke

A comeback in class. . . I was in class the other day and the teacher was talking about books and movies about books. Someone in the class said, "Well, I think that movies are better because you get more feelings than reading a book!" The teacher agreed with her and started to write it on the board. Then I blurted out "Well, I would beg to differ! I think that books are quite solid enough."
