Poor Billy and His Parents Bedroom

A little boy is walking down the hallway of his house... There is Screaming in his Parents bedroom. Opens the door Boy's Dad is dressed in Shabs and his mother dressed in a Cheer-Leading outfit with nothing else on! Billy says Daddy whats going on? His father responds Just hold on son, Ill be there to tuck you in in 20 minutes 20 minutes later the father is walking down the hall and he hears screaming in the boys bedroom... He walks in and sees the boy having sex with his Grandmother. Father says Billy whats going on?! Billy: Its not so funny when its your mother is it?
