English (US) Joke

A young priest... A freshly ordained young priest was walking to his newly assigned parish in the inner city. As he walked down the street, a prostitute stopped him and said, "Hey father, $20 for a blow job." The priest had never heard of such a thing and hurried away from the prostitute. When he arrived at the church he was greeted by the Mother Superior who showed him around the place and guided him to his room. Before she left the priest alone in his room, she asked if he had any questions. The young priest said, "I do have one question, Mother Superior, what is a blow job?" And the old nun says, "Twenty bucks, same as on the street."
