English (US) Joke

Tyrone' s 1st day in the first grade, he comes home crying... When his mother asks why, he replies, "The teacher told us to say our ABC's and all the little white boys could say them and I could only get to "E" why is that?" Mom says, "Cause you black and they white." Next day Tyrone is crying again . "What's wrong today Tyrone?" His mother asks. Tyrone said, "Teacher told us to count to 100 and all the little white boys did, but I could only get to 10. Why is that?" Mom says, "Cause u black and they white." Next day he comes home smiling. "What happened today Tyrone?" Tyrone says to his mama, "We went to the bathroom and my thing was biggest of all. Is that cause I'm black and they white?" Mama says, "No Tyrone. It's cause u 17 and they 6."
