English (US) Joke

There were a pair of twin skunks... One was named in, the other was named out. When out was out, in was in and when in was in out was out. One day, out was in and in was out so mama told out to go out and bring in in, so out went out and brought in in, and mama asked "how did you find in so quickly?" He replied, "In-stinked!"
