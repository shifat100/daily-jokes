English (US) Joke

So a man has to go to a gay doctor. A man has to go to the doctor and finds out he is gay. He tells his wife about it. "Honey, just because he is gay doesn't make him any less of a doctor or anything to be afraid of." Against his better judgement the man goes to the appointment for his physical. The doctor tells him he needs to undress and put on the hospital gown. "Doctor I have to tell you, I know you are gay and I am not comfortable with undressing in front of you." The doctor replies "I will turn the light off for you. Will that make you more comfortable?' The man replies "Yes" and the doctor turns the light off. He undresses and asks the doctor where he should put his clothes at. The doctor replies "Just toss them in the corner of the room with mine."
