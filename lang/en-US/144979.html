English (US) Joke

Obama was trying to buy Michelle a really great gift for her, on their Anniversary, so he sought out to create a new perfume for her... Secret Service was enlisted into the task of rounding up the best perfumers to combine their efforts to create the best scent in the world. They easily create Obama's wish, and present him with the finished product. Obama agrees this is the most amazing scent he has ever seen. He presents Michelle with her gift, and she gives it a sniff..."This is WONDERFUL! WHAT DO YOU CALL IT!!!!!!!!! Obama says- "I call it "COME TO ME" Michelle replies with a second sniff..."It doesn't smell like come to me..."
