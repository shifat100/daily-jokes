English (US) Joke

Surely people with the middle name 'Will' are the most nominally determined people possible? And if they they have quite a will, surely they are even more nominally determined, and surely if they are only slightly quite willful, surely they are ever more nominally determined, and in fact, because of this, the amount they are nominally determined is so much that they surely their name causes their life to be determined by their name so much that they cannot escape from their (W/w)ill. Hell, I guess you can tell by this long paragraph that nominal determinism is, in fact, my middle name.
