English (US) Joke

The worm sermon A minister decided that a visual demonstration would add emphasis to his Sunday sermon. Four worms were placed into four separate jars. The first worm was put into a container of alcohol. The second worm was put into a container of cigarette smoke. The third worm was put into a container of chocolate syrup. The fourth worm was put into a container of good clean soil. At the conclusion of the sermon, the Minister reported the following results: The first worm in alcohol: Dead. The second worm in cigarette smoke: Dead. Third worm in chocolate syrup: Dead. Fourth worm in good clean soil: Alive! So the Minister asked the congregation, "So, my friends? What did you learn from this demonstration?" Maxine, sitting in the back, quickly raised her hand and said . . . "As long as you drink, smoke and eat chocolate, you won't have worms!"
