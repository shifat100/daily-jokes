English (US) Joke

"Who's that over there talking to himself?" I asked the barman in the pub. "That's Bill," He replied. "He comes in every dinner time and sits and has a drink with his imaginary wife." "Is he a loony?" I asked. "What makes you say that?" He laughed. "Why else would you have an imaginary wife?" I replied, "an imaginary girlfriend would be far less hassle, and you'd still get regular sex."
