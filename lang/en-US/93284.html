English (US) Joke

A group of buddies, all in their 50's , discussed where they should meet for lunch. Finally, it was agreed that they would meet at Hooters because all the waitresses were young and good looking. Ten years later at 60+, they discussed where they would meet for lunch and they decided at Hooters because the food was good and they had plenty of parking. Ten years later at 70+, They again discussed where they would meet for lunch and they decided on Hooters because it was handicap accessible and had a toilet for the disabled. Ten years later at 80+ they discussed where they would meet for lunch and they decided on Hooters because they had never been there before.
