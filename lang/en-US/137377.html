English (US) Joke

difference between courtesy and tact In these days of coed dorms, one must treat the other students with courtesy, and more than that, tact. I'll explain just what I mean. If you should walk into the shower room and see a woman standing there naked, you must always close the curtain quickly and say "Excuse me sir, I didn't me to interrupt". The "Excuse Me" is common courtesy, but the "Sir", that is tact.
