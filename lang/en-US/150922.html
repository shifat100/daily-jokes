English (US) Joke

Drummer jokes! Q: How can you tell the drummer is at the door? A: He doesn't know when to come in. Q: How many drummers to screw in a lightbulb? A: 5. One for the lightbulb, and 4 to describe how much better Neil Peart would have done it. Q: How do you know there's a drummer walking behind you? A: You can hear his knuckles dragging. Q: Which car belongs to the drummer? A: The one with the pizza sign on top.
