English (US) Joke

Two men are hunting together in a tree stand. One leans over to his friend and says "Hey I can see your house from up here! I can see through the window, it looks like your wife is cheating on you with some guy." The husband in disbelief says to the other, "Well if you can, shoot her in the head and him in the nuts. That'll teach them a lesson." His buddy giggles as he raises his gun and says, "I can get that in one shot!"
