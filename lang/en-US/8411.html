Atheist Boyfriend A boy and a girl were dating and the results are bumpy. So, they decided to a date to decide the outcome.... During the date, the girl bursts out: Do you love me? The boy replies: Sure, I love you as much as God himself! The girl bursts out into tears.... The boy continues: I'm an atheist. The girl storms off.
