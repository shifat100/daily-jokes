English (US) Joke

Ellen In a poll for the audience, Ellen asks for a show of hands how often they have sex with their partners. She asks, "Two or three times a week?" Some hands go up Then, "Once a week?" Some other hands go up. To be facetious, "Once a year?" A gentlemen in the back shoots up and chants, "Me! Me!" Ellen resonds, "Once a year? You seem pretty excited about that!" The man shouts, "Yeah! And tonight's the night!" (*Quoted out of context. Heard from a guest at my work, but had to share. Had me rollin.*)
