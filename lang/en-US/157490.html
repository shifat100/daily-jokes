Inside thoughts of a doctor A doctor after furiously having sex with his patient in his office, lays down and starts to morally think what has he done. Oh my God, I shouldn’t have sex with a patient! I have broken a line of trust and compromise. Then a little evil appears in his mind and says “come on, every doctor has done it at least once in their lifes, it is totally normal!” The doctor fills a relief inside of him while listening to that voice and smiles away. Then a little angel appears in his mind and says “yeah, some doctors have sex with their patients, but you are a pediatrician!!!” [source](http://www.yoaca.com/health-medical/inside-thoughts-of-a-doctor/)
