English (US) Joke

A redneck walks into a gay bar... he walks up to the bartender and says, "Give me a Schlitz, fag." The bartender says, "That's not cool. You can't come in here and say that to me." The redneck just smiles from ear to ear. "Give me a Schlitz, fag." The bartender asks, "How would you like it if *you* were behind the bar and I came up and said something like that to you?" The redneck shrugs, "I wouldn't care." So he hops off his stool and positions himself behind the bar, the bartender goes outside, walks in and says, "Give me a vodka Red Bull, asshole!" The redneck replies, "I'm sorry, we don't serve fags in here."
