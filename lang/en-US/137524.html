UK Fire Insurance A man and his wife moved back home to Cork, from London. The wife had a wooden leg and to insure it in Britain was £2000.00 a year! When they arrived in Cork, they went to an Insurance agency to see how much it would cost to insure the wooden leg. The agent looked it up on the computer and said to the couple that the price would be €39.00. The husband was shocked and asked why it was so cheap here in Ireland to insure, because it cost £2000.00 in England! The agent turned his computer screen to the couple and said, 'Well, here it is on the screen, it says that any wooden structure, with a sprinkler system over it, is €39.00.
