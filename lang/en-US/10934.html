English (US) Joke

A teacher wants to make sure non of her students feel stupid... So she walks to the front of class and says " if any of you feel dumb please stand up." A minute goes by of silence before little Johnny stands up "Why do you think you are stupid?" To which little Johnny replies " I don't feel stupid I just felt bad that you were the only one standing up"
