"Woman, who's watching the fuckin' TV? You or me?" Part 2: The woman is standing in front of an off stove, when her husband comes in. "What are you doing?" asks the husband. "Cooking!" replies the husband. "But the TV is off," noticed the husband. "Man, who's fuckin' cooking here? You or me?" "But there are no weights," noticed the father. "Parents, who is lifting fuckin' weights here? You, or me?"
