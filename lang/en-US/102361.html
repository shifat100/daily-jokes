English (US) Joke

An backwoods bride was crying... She came to the breakfast table in the shack where they lived with her granny. Granny ask her why she was crying the morning after her wedding night. "Granny we din't have relations on our weddin' night, and he ner' terched me. He read a book all night". Granny assured her it would be O.K. The next morning the new bride came to breakfast sobbing again and told granny the same story about him reading and ignoring her. Granny snapped her boney fingers and told her to come upstairs. She followed and Granny led her to an old dusty chest. Granny opened the chest, dug deeply and pulled out the most beautiful negligée. "This is what I wore when your grandpappy wasn't feelin' like lovin'... it never failed". So, that evening she went down to the creek and bathed, brushed her hair a hundred strokes, put on the perfume her mama gave to her years ago and slipped on the silky negligee granny had given her. That night shewent into the room with her new husband and slid into bed beside him. Granny, curious as she was, quietly looked on through a knothole in the wall. It wasn't long before her husband slid his hand between her thighs and touched her twat. Granny smiled and went to bed. The new wife ran down to breakfast the next morning crying harder than ever. Granny said, "What's wrong now honey, I saw him terch your twat"..."He did granny, but he only wet his finger to turn the page!'...
