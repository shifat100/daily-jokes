English (US) Joke

Tensions in the middle East have reached an all time high.... ...to the point that the US government is forced to institute a draft forcing many people to join the military. Lucy Linus and Charlie Brown are all together when they open their letters from the government. Lucy opens hers and says "Uuugh! I have to go to Afghanistan!" Linus opens his and says "lookth like I got Thaudi Arabia." Charlie Brown opens his with a mopey face and says "I got Iraq."
