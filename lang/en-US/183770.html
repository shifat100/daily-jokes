English (US) Joke

A black man, white man, and Asian man are standing on a cliff... A genie appears and tells the men that when they jump off the cliff, they will land in a giant pile of whatever they yell while falling. The black man runs and jumps and yells "BITCHES" and lands in a crowd of sexy women. The white man jumps off and yells "MONEY" and lands in stacks upon stacks of hundred dollar bills. The Asian man is running and as he approaches the edge of the cliff, trips on a rock and yells "SHIT".
