English (US) Joke

The world's leading scientists build a lie-detecting machine... ...so powerful that if you are detected to be lying, it you immediately drop dead. They bring around three test subjects. The first one, an Irishman, is hooked to the machine. He says, "I think I don't drink" and the next moment, he's dead. Next, it's the Asian's turn. She says, "I think I'm a good driver" and what do you know! She too is killed instantly. Then the blonde walks up and confidently begins,"I think—" and drops dead.
