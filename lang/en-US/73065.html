English (US) Joke

A woman calls her butler into the bedroom A woman calls her butler into the bedroom and says: "Jeeves, take off my gloves." Jeeves replies: "Yes my lady." The lady then continues by saying: "Now Jeeves, I want you take off my dress and corset." Jeeves replies: "Of course, my lady." The lady then says: "Jeeves, now I want you to take off my undergarments." Jeeves obliges the request and says: "As you wish. Will there be anything else my lady?" The lady replies: "Yes Jeeves, please don't ever wear my clothes again."
