The hells angels ride again... That's a real talent you're wasting there, Sugar Shorts. You could be famous if you rode with me. Why are you committing suicide?" She explained, "My parents don't like me laughing at dead trans people on Reddit.com." It's still unclear whether she jumped or was pushed.
