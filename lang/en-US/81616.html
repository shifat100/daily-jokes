English (US) Joke

The Catholic church has been making quite a bit of money... ...however, almost none of the funds have been recorded for taxes. Eventually the IRS catches wind and sends a man over to meet with the church leaders. The IRS agent knocks on the church door and a priest opens up the door. The IRS agents rudely blurts out, "I have some questions for you!" "Ah, come on in I'll be with you in a second," replies the priest. The IRS agent does so and sees the priest cleaning up some cracker crumbs and spilled wine from last communion. The IRS agent says,” boy there sure are a lot of crumbs what do you do with all of them and how do you clean up the wine?" The priest responds with, "Oh we send them to the cracker company and get a full box for free. And as for the wine we mop up the spills and get free box from the wine company." "Hmm," says the IRS agent.” Well um, I have one more question. It's a bit strange, but when you perform circumcision, what do you do with the extra skin?" The priest smiles and gently responds with,” we send it to the IRS and they send us a complete dick."
