I am making the money for the family so I am "capitalism". Mom and me take care of you, that makes you "The People". The nanny we hired is the "working class" and your 2 year old brother, he is the "future". Son: Wow, thats kind of complicated. Let me sleep over this for a night. You can test me tomorrow. All go to bed and in the middle of the night the baby wakes up due to dirty diaper. Son gets up and goes to the parents bedroom. Tries to wake up his mom, but she is passed out, no way to wake her.
