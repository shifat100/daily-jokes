English (US) Joke

A teacher catches her student listening to music in her class... and walks over to him. She threatens to take his phone away, but he says he wasn't listening to any music. Doubtful, she puts an earbud in her ear and hears Lil' Wayne music. "Yep... sorry you're right. You weren't listening to any music."
