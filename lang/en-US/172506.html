English (US) Joke

Gimp and the drumstick... Gimp walks into the music store to return one of his drumsticks. He slams the stick on the counter and shouts to the shopkeeper: "This drumstick sounds terrible" Bewildered, the shopkeeper asks: "And how does this drumstick, or any other drumstick, exactly sound terrible?" To which the gimp replies: "Just look at the fucking splinters!!!" *(now google "sounding")*
