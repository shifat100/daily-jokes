Cheap as chips The other evening I was trying to nap before my night shift. My girlfriend walked in my room started kissing one thing led to another and she started begging for sex. I said I'm really tired and just want to nap. She offered to buy me chips. Baffled I asked if she thinks I'm as cheap as chips. That was the end of our relationship.
