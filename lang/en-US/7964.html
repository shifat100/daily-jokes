English (US) Joke

A Husband came home late at night from the office one day and realised he forgot his Wife's birthday... "How would you feel if you don't see me for next few days?" His wife said He couldn't believe his luck. He replied at once. "Wowww.....That would be great!'' Monday passed & he didn't see her.... Tuesday he didn't see her... and Wednesday passed too... On Thursday the swelling was better as he caught a glimpse of her from the corner of his left eye.
