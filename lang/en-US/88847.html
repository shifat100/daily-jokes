English (US) Joke

Blatant racial discrimination! Teacher asks the kids what they done at recess. *Teacher:* "Timmy, what did you do?" *Timmy:* "I played with the ball" *Teacher:* "Very good Timmy. You come up to the board and write 'ball' ". *Teacher:* "Sam, what did you do at recess?" *Sam:* "I played with Timmy, beside the tree." *Teacher:* "Very good, come up to the board and write 'tree' " *Teacher:* "Jamal, what did you do at recess?" *Jamal:* "I wanted to play with Sam and Timmy, but they wouldn't let me." *Teacher:* "That's terrible. Do you know what that sounds like to me? Blatant racial discrimination! Jamal, you come up to the board and write 'Blatant racial discrimination'. "
