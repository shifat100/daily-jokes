English (US) Joke

A man is going down on his wife when he suddenly becomes violently ill. He goes to the doctor, who asks him to describe what he was doing leading up to the sudden bout of illness. "Well," says the man, "my wife and I were just drinking some Budweisers, and then I started to give her oral sex-" "Ah, well you should've seen this coming," the doctor says. "How so?" asks the man. "Surely you know the saying - beer before lick 'er, never been sicker."
