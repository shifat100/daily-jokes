English (US) Joke

The Funeral A man arrives at his laywer's funeral and and was very dismayed by the presence of this one person. He turns to the people around him. 'Why are you all at this man's funeral?' A man turns towards him and says, 'We're all clients.' 'And you ALL came to pay your respects? How touching.' 'Huh? No, we came to make sure he was dead.'
