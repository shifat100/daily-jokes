English (US) Joke

A man goes to his Rabbi "Rabbi, I think my wife is trying to poison me!" "Very interesting," says the Rabbi. "Let me go and talk to her and see what I can find out." A few hours later the Rabbi calls the man up. "I've spent several hours speaking with your wife," he says. "What should I do?" the man asked. "Take the poison."
