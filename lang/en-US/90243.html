Duckling and Baby Skunk There was a mama duck and her duckling walking with a mama Skunk and her baby. They came up to a road, the mamas decide to cross the street first, they both get hit by a car The baby duck starts crying and saying my mama just died and I do not know what I am. The baby Skunk says, well you are yellow, have webbed feet and wings you must be a duck. The duckling thanks the baby skunk. The baby Skunk starts crying now, my mama just died and I do not know what I am. The duck thinks for a minute and says, we'll you are not white, you are not black, and you stink, You must be a mexican!
