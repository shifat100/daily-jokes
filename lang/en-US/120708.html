English (US) Joke

She said to take off her shirt... So I took off her shirt. Then she said, "Take off my skirt." So I took off her skirt. "Take off my shoes," she said, so I did. "Now my bra and panties." I took them off and she looked at me and said, "I don't want to catch you wearing my things again."
