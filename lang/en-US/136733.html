English (US) Joke

A beggar in Afghanistan An Afghan man walks across a busy street when an old lady says ''Please my son, give me $1 in charity.'' ''I don't have any.'' ''If you don't have that just give a tiny little bit. Praise God, look at your youth, your tie, your strength and beauty. May I be sacrificed for you, just give a tiny little bit.'' ''I don't have any.'' ''Really just even $0.01 would be enough.'' ''I don't have any, really, not even $0.01.'' ''Well why are you just standing there then? Come sit and beg like I do.''
