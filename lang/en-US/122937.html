Singing competition. Sam and David were good friends. Once upon a time, David entered into a singing competition. In the competition, they had to make their original songs and sing them. David penned down his song, which he titled Esfore. It was a song on a mythical woman by that name, whose affection many men pined for. The song was amazing and when David sung it before Sam, Sam was moved beyond tears. On the day of the competition, David was a nervous wreck. He said to Sam, "Mate, I can't do this! I'm a nervous wreck! You know how bad my singing gets when I am nervous!" Sam replied, "Are you sure? It's your song! Won't you be hurt if I get credit for your efforts?" David grasped Sam by the shoulders and said, "I don't care! I want the people to hear this song! If you care so much, then just let the crowd know that I wrote it."
