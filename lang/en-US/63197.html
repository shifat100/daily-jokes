'tis a shem...' - says paddy. 'aye... tis a shem' - says seamus.. 'aye- tis a shem..' say's seamus. 'that a man of faith, whatever that is, should be corrupted.' a few pints later, and they notice bishop brennan knock on the door of the brothel and go in. 'tis a shem...' says paddy. 'aye.. tis a shem...' says seamus. 'one of tha poor garls must be dyin'. RIP Jack Kelly- if you haven't watched 'Father Ted' the series, i recommend.
