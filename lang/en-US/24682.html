English (US) Joke

A Blind Man walks into an all women's bar He sits down and orders a shot. After the shot he asks, "Does anybody want to hear a blonde joke?" The lady next to him leans over. She says, "Hey buddy, so I'm gonna tell you this because it seems like you don't know any better. I'm a blonde coal miner, that woman over at the bar is a blonde professional boxer, the bartender is a blonde cage fighter, that woman at the pool table is a blonde steel mill worker, and shes a blonde bouncer. So buddy, are you sure you still want to tell that blonde joke?" To which the blind man responds, "Well no, not if I've got to explain it five times."
