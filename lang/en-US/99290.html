English (US) Joke

A man is getting his haircut at the barbershop... A kid walks in and the barber says to his customer, "this is the dumbest kid in the world, look I'll prove it to you" The barber takes out a dollar bill in one hand and 2 quarters in the other and proceeds to ask the kid, "Young man, which of these would you like?" The kid thinks for a second and then grabs the 2 quarters and leaves. The barber turns to his customer and says, "See I told you! He is an idiot!" After leaving the barbershop, the man sees the kid across the street coming out of the ice cream store. He walks over and asks, "Hey kid, how come you took the quarter and not the dollar?!?" The kid responds, "Once I take the dollar, that idiot's game will end"
