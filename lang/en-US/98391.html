English (US) Joke

3rd grade students are learning about animals. The teacher is displaying photos of various animals on a projector. Teacher: Shows a picture of a hyena. "Johnnie, what's this animal?" Johnny: "Uhm..." Teacher: "This animal laughs a lot." Johnny: "Oh! A hyena!" Teacher: "Correct! Sarah, what is this animal?" She shows a picture of a cheetah. Sarah: "A leopard?" Teacher: "No. This animal is on the Cheetos commercials." Sarah: "Oh! A cheetah!" Teacher: "Correct! Hmmm. Kevin, what is this animal?" She shows a picture of a deer. Kevin: "Uhhh... I don't know." Teacher: "It's something that your mom sometimes calls your dad." Kevin: "Oh! A horny little bastard!"
