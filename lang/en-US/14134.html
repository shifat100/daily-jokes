English (US) Joke

A man and a woman are preparing for their romantic evening. Two very overworked individuals, Richard and Jessica, prepare for their Friday evening date. Richard makes sure he has a clean shave, and Jessica goes to touch up the color in her hair. Suddenly, to her surprise, she sees that she is out of her hair dye! No matter how hard she looks, she just can't seem to find that perfect shade of red that she needed. Richard sees that she is in peril and approaches her. "What is it, honey" he asks? "Oh, I just can't seem to find the right hair dye for tonight," she replies. "Let me go get some for you darling," Dick graciously replies. "What color specifically do you need," he adds? "It's my ombre," Jessica calmly answers. So then Richard leaves to get Jessica's hair color. I guess you could say......... Dick's out for her ombre.
