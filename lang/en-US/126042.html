English (US) Joke

Memory trick Two very elderly couples bump into each other in the street, Jack says " hi there George, how are you?" George says " Great! we've just been to that new memory clinic, they teach you how to remember things using word association, it's really good" Jack asks " really? what's the name of the clinic?" George thinks for a moment and then says " let me see, eh, what do you call that flower with a thorny stem?" Jack says "A rose"? "Ah yes that's it" George turns to his wife...."Rose, what's the name of that clinic"?
