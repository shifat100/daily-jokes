English (US) Joke

A bunch of bad jokes. Why does a man carry a brick with him in a plane? To throw it out. How do you get an elephant into a fridge? You open the door and put the elephant in. How do you get a giraffe into a fridge? You open the door, get the elephant out and put the giraffe in the fridge. How do you die by doing nothing? By getting hit with the brick from the first joke.
