English (US) Joke

A Canadian woman living near the border A Canadian woman lives with her family in a forest near the border with the US. One autumn morning, her son comes home from town holding a letter. He approaches the woman and says: "According to this letter, the United States wants to consider this area as part of Montana. The Canadian government agrees, but says that since we're the only family living here, they want our permission to sign this land over to the US." The woman jumps out of her chair and exclaims, "Where do I sign? I don't think I can stand another Canadian winter!"
