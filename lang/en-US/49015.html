Marriage Anniversary Wife: Honey today is our marriage anniversary. It's 14 years now Husband: Oh that is great Wife: What shall we do celebrate it in a grand style? Husband: How about fasting and prayers? Wife: Just that? Husband: Yes, to thank God because for the first time, He might be surprised that we are still living together despite our troubles.
