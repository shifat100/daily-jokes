English (US) Joke

A woman visits her doctor because she isn't feeling well. "What seems to be the problem", the Dr. asks. "Well", the woman says, "I have very little energy and just don't feel all that well". The Dr. gives her a prescription for testosterone and says, "take one of these every day for 6 mos. and I think you'll feel much better. Set an appointment for 6 mos from now and we'll see how you feel". In 6 mos. the woman comes back and says, "Doctor you were right. I feel so much better. The only thing is that I'm growing hair where I've never grown hair before". The doctor says, "Yes, well we talked about side effects and that hair growth is the most common one. So, where are you growing hair that you've never grown hair before"? The woman answers, "It's on my balls doctor, on my balls".
