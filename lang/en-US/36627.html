English (US) Joke

The Engineer's Trainee So one day at a coin mint, an engineer who works there is taking a trainee for a tour and shows him how everything works. They approach the dollar-making machine. After explaining it, the Engineer says: "Well, what do you think? Complicated?" And the Trainee says: "No, I completely understand it; it makes sense." To which the Engineer replies: "No, it makes dollars."
