English (US) Joke

Shooting the wife An Army Sniper goes to Rifle shop to buy new scope for his Gun. Manager takes out one & says: ''This scope is so good, U can see my house 1 km up on that hill". Sniper looks through the scope & laughs :- ''I see a naked man & a naked woman in your house''. Manager looks in the scope & gives 2 bullets to sniper :- ''I'll give U this scope free, If U shoot my wife's head off & the guy's Dick". Sniper looks again in the scope :- ''Well ! Seems like I can do that with one bullet."
