English (US) Joke

Once an adventurer came across a tribe of men They welcomed him, fed him and invited him to stay for the night. Being inquisitive he wondered how the men got along without women. *"Pardon my frankness"* he said, *"how can you live with out women? I imagine it's frustrating to not get any relief"* They told him they'll show him in the morning how they do it. The next day they all went down to the river bank where an ass was standing. They told him to go first as he was their guest. Now he didn't want to be rude after all their hospitality, so he approached the ass, unzipped his pants and began doing her. After 10 minutes he finished, patted the ass and turned to the tribe. *"About time"* they said, *"we need the ass to take us to the other side where the tribe of women live"*
