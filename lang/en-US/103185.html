English (US) Joke

In high school, I was very bad at math. I still am, but was bad then as well. I'd score between 2% and 8%. Now, to shame us into doing better, the results used to be announced out publicly from the lowest to the highest marks, and I would always be the 1st Or 2nd to be called out. One day, the Math final papers were being released and my name wasn't among the first to be called out. The teacher got to 30s, 40s, 50s, 60s & 70s. Still my paper had not been called out. Everyone kept looking at me! And the teacher went on to the 80s when he got to 88%, and he had one exam paper in his hand. Could it be? Proof that I was a misunderstood genius all along. Finally the teacher looked up and said, "There is a an idiot that did not write his name on the paper and scored 0%. If you have not received your paper come and get it now...
