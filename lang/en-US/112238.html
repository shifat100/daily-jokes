English (US) Joke

Toke-Ville This one time, a couple of political science majors were smoking weed as they discussed US politics. A new guy in the group hogs the bong, taking repeated puffs before a senior member exclaimed: Listen here, Tocqueville is all about democracy! Let everyone get their turn. They all stared blankly at him. "Wait, was my humor too high?" They burst out laughing.
