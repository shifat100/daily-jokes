English (US) Joke

A woman sits down at a bar and asks for a drink. "I hear you have a drink that is guaranteed to be as good as an orgasm in my mouth or my money back, I'll have one of those." The Barkeep mixes, stirs, shakes and pours and serves the young lady the drink. The yound lady takes a sip and spits the drink on the floor. "Ugh, this is horrible. It's tastes warm, salty, and slimy, I can't believe I put that in my mouth." "That'll be $10" says the bartender.
