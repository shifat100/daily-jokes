English (US) Joke

The Depression A San Francisco Bay Area denizen was discussing the drastic rise in the cost of living with his neighbor. "It's getting really hard to even buy groceries nowadays due to the increase in commodity prices from all these high-paying tech firms and their employees living here currently." "I know - I used to live in a gated community across the bay, but I was compelled to move". "Oh really? Which one?" "Yes. San Quentin".
