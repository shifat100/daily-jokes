English (US) Joke

King decided to free the prisoners Once a king was in a very good mood and decided to free many prisoners. There was an elderly man among those prisoners. The king caught sight of him and inquired the old man: "For how long you are in the prison?" Old Man replied : "Since the time of your father" King became very sad and with tears in his eyes said: "Put him back in the prison, as he is the remembrance of my father"
