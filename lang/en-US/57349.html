Car accident After a car crash, there are two white and two black people who are injured. When the ambulance arrives, they start to treat the white people. The two black guy start to shout: - Hey! What about us? - Sir. Do you know the colours? - Of course. - And what colour is our van? - White. - See? The black one will arrive soon!
