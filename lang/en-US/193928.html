English (US) Joke

Little Johnny's Grammar Lesson One day, during a lesson on proper grammar, the teacher asked for a show of hands for who could use the word "beautiful" in the same sentence twice. First, she called on little Mary, who responded with, "My father bought my mother a beautiful dress and she looked beautiful in it." "Very good, Mary," replied the teacher. She then called on little Steven. "My mommy planned a beautiful dinner and it turned out beautifully," he said. "Excellent, Steven!" Then, the teacher called on little Johnny. "Last night, at the dinner table, my sister told my father that she was pregnant, and he said, 'Beautiful, fucking beautiful!'"
