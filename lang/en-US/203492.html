M&M Peas A mom, Bri, and a son, Jorge, were eating dinner at the dinner table. Jorge pushes his plate away and says, "I'm done." Bri glances at Jorge's plate and notices he still has a pile of peas on it, "No you don't. Not untill you eat all of your food." "I don't like peas." "They are not peas they are green M&M's but the trick is you can't taste the candy untill you swallow them." Jorge gets excited and sticks a spoon full of peas in his mouth. Then he grabs his glass of milk and takes a drink. Jorge says, "your right mom they do taste like M&M's. Why don't you try?" "Who do you think you're kidding?," replies Bri and gives Jorge a sly look. "Who do YOU think you're kidding?," Jorge says grabbing his glass of milk and pouring the peas out of it onto his plate.
