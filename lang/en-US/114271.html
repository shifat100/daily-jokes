English (US) Joke

A group of church ministers start a bowling team. What do they call themselves? I was walking across the street at work today and an old man grabbed my arm, somewhat suddenly. I immediately thought he needed helped crossing the street, but he appeared to be moving fine, regardless of his cane. We kept walking as he held tightly to my arm. He started speaking, "a group of church ministers..." He stuttered. My thought at this point was that he was part of and/or simply looking for that group of people and that he was lost. But then he continued, "a group of church ministers... start their own bowling team. What do they call themselves?" I realize it's a joke at this point, "I don't know. What?" "They call themselves 'The Holy Rollers'." To which I muster up a fake laugh, smile and say, "That's a good one, sir!" This was quite possibly the worst joke I've ever heard, but the man seemed to enjoy telling it, so I was happy to listen.
