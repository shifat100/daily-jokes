English (US) Joke

A man goes to confession He enters the confession, sits down and says "Forgive me Father for I have sinned". Then starts saying all the sins he has done. The priest listens and at the and he asks the man: "Do you feel bad about all of this?". "Yes", the man replies. Then the priest says: "Good. Do you renounce Satan?" The man says: "I would, it's just that I already have two children with her!"
