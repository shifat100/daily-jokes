English (US) Joke

The Restaurant and the Blond One day a blonde went into a restaurant and decided she was going to order the free birthday dessert, even though it wasn't her birthday. A month later on her real birthday she went back to the restaurant with her family, and they had the same waiter. She ordered the dessert again and the waiter recognized her. This was her excuse: "Oh that wasn't me that was my twin sister." Let's just say she wasn't going back anytime soon.
