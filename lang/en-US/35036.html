English (US) Joke

Two converts set off to go join ISIS... Hasan and Hussein set off to go join ISIS. Hasan flew to Istanbul first class, but Hussein was on the no fly list and had to stow away on an empty oil tanker. But Hasan gifted Hussein a heavy backpack of food and cigarettes to make the trip more bearable. But when they got to Syria, the ISIS folks found those cigarettes in the backpack and brought them both to a high tower in Raqqa. The judge said, "Hussein, you're pardoned. But Hasan, we throw you off the tower." "But Your Honor," Hasan says, "I'm not even the tobacco junkie!" "Yeah," the judge replies, "but *he's* on the no fly list."
