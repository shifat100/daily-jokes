English (US) Joke

Two men were walking home when suddenly a portal opened up right infront of them... The portal seemed to be a gateway between earth and this whole different universe. "Should we go in?" Said the first guy "It wouldn't hurt would it...." The two men eventually jump through the portal and enter this disgusting, filthy world filled with horrific stench and garbage everywhere the eye can see. A two men see a man walking by and ask them what this place is. "Excuse me! Over here!" "Yes?" replied the wandering man "Where are we?" "None other then Neverland of course" "This place is so disgusting. Doee anything ever get done here?" "Never"
