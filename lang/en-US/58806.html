English (US) Joke

A Boy approaches his Dad... The Boy asks, "Dad, what is sex?" The Dad ponders for awhile and figures that now is a good time for his 10 year old boy to finally understand the true facts about where babies come from. He then elaborates on every single detail, from putting penises into vaginas, and even shares about his sex life with the Boy's mother. The Boy, visually astounded, takes a long pause to absorb all the information. He then whips out a piece of paper. "So Dad... Do I circle the M or the F?"
