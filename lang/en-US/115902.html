English (US) Joke

White Horse A white horse walks into a bar, and orders a beer. He moves aside, minds his own business; but the barkeep hasn't had such an unusual patron for a long time. He's curious. He ransacks his brain, and can't come up with a better line than... "you know, there's a whiskey named after you?" And the white horse replies: "What? Eric?"
