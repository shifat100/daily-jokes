English (US) Joke

The night's winding down, and all the customers have left except for this one drunk. Bartender: Hey man, you should get going, go back home and get some sleep. The drunk leaves through the back door of the establishment and, as the bartender is cleaning up, comes back in through the front 10 minutes later. Bartender: Hey man, I just kicked you out, we're closing for the night! The drunk leaves through the back, and not five minutes after the bartender locks the front door, the drunk comes and knocks. Bartender:Dammit, didn't I just tell you- Drunk:Hey, barkeep! How many bars do you work at anyway?
