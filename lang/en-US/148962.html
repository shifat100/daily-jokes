English (US) Joke

Three engineers There are three engineers heading to their college reunion in a car; an electrical engineer, a chemical engineer and a Microsoft engineer. The car breaks down along the way for seemingly no reason. The electrical engineer suggests testing the electronics of the car and attempt to find out if a fault might have occurred. The chemical engineer, suggests that maybe the fuel is becoming emulsified and getting stopped up. Then, the Microsoft engineer, comes up with a "Microsoft Solution": Close all the windows, get out, get back in, then open the windows again, then he's positive it will work! Source: One of my old professors but, it's probably somewhere on the internet.
