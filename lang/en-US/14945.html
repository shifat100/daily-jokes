English (US) Joke

A man has been unemployed for a long time... finally, with the release of Stephen King's remade 'It', he gets a job posting huge billboards around the city. After some weeks however the movie was slumping, so in a desperate effort for publicity, the advertisers sent the man back out with crimson paint and a paint brush and told to give all the clowns a 'bleeding face' effect. Mid-job, covered in paint, and late into the evening, the police spot him and surround him with guns drawn. "No, No" he screams, "you don't understand! I'm a red It poster!"
