English (US) Joke

So a blonde died and went to Heaven. God was at the gate and said to her, "Before entering, you will see 10 angels, and each one of them will tell you a joke. If you manage to not laugh at all, you may enter." The blonde walked up to the first angel, listened to the joke and did not laugh. She then went to the second and again did not laugh. This happened every time and she finally reached to the last angel. When the last angel was at the middle of the joke, the blonde started laughing REALLY HARD. The angel then asked, "Woah, is my joke really that funny?" The Blonde, laughing non-stop, said, "No... I JUST GOT THE FIRST JOKE!"
