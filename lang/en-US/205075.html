You Know You're From Oklahoma You know you're from Oklahoma if: a tornado warning siren is your signal to go out in the yard and look for a funnel. your idea of a traffic jam is ten cars waiting to pass a tractor on the highway. you ever had to switch from heat to A/C in the same day. you see people wear bib overalls at funerals. little smokies are something you serve only for "special occasions". you refer to the capital of Oklahoma as "The City". you know cowpies are not made from beef. someone you know has used a football schedule to plan their wedding date. you have know someone who has had one belt buckle bigger than your fist. you aren't surprised to find movie rental, ammunition, and bait all in the same store. your "place at the lake" has wheels under it. you learned to shoot a 12 guage shot gun before you learned to multiply. And finally, you are 100% Oklahoman if you have ever heard or been a part of this conversation: "Ya wanna coke?" "Yeah." "What kind?"
