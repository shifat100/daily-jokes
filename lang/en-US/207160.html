English (US) Joke

My Plates or Yours? A professional photographer, at a friend's house for dinner, was asked to show his portfolio from a recent overseas trip. His friends were quite impressed. "What wonderful photos!" said the host's wife. "You must have a very expensive camera." The photographer just smiled and waited until after dinner, when he said, "Thank you for the delicious meal. You must have very *expensive* pans!"
