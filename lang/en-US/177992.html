English (US) Joke

Two girls were playing chess Two girls were playing chess Girl 1-I think we have played enough, I am getting bored.. Girl 2:You're right. Anyway, you are just left with a rook & I am with a knight.. Then a boy comes there.. Boy:Hey girls, Let's play some chess. Both girls:No, You will beat us easily.... Boy:Ok, then. I will play against you two combined.. Both girls :Even then you will beat us.. Boy :Okay, I will play left handed.... Both girls :Sounds fair. Ok. The girls, obviously, lose & the boy leaves. Girl 1: This is so contemptible. He beat us even left-handed. Girl 2 (after thinking for a bit ):OMG, he befooled us.... Girl1:How? Girl 2: He must have been left-handed...
