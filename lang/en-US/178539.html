English (US) Joke

An old married couple are sitting on their porch one morning... ...when suddenly the old lady lashes out with her cane and hits her husband on the knee. "Ow! What'd ya do that for?" He asked. "That's for 60 years of bad sex!" He stewed in silence a few moments, rubbing his knee. All of a sudden he took his cane and thumped his wife on her knee. "Ow! What's that for?" "That's for knowing the difference."
