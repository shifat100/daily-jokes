English (US) Joke

A brunette woman goes into the dr.'... A brunette woman goes into the dr.'s office. She tells the Dr. : "It hurts all over my body." He says: "point to where it hurts". She points to her shoulder and yells "OUCH!". She then points to her hip and yells "OUCH!". Finally she points to her knee and screams in pain "OUCH!!!". The Dr. asks her "Are you a true blonde and dyed your hair brown?" She says: "yes, how did you know"? He answers: "YOU HAVE A BROKEN FINGER!!!".
