English (US) Joke

Babies! When the second grader arrived home from school, she excitedly ran up to her mom and said, "Guess what we learned today, mommy? How to make babies." Her mother was more than surprised, but did her best to remain calm. She knew that this day would come, but she had hoped it wouldn't have been so soon. "How interesting dear," her mother said. "How do you make babies?" "It's really simple," replied the little girl. "All you have to do is change the 'y' to 'i' and add 'es'."
