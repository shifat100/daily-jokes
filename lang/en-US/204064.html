English (US) Joke

PARTY!! My 4 year old brother just had a birthday. At his birthday party he invited all his friends and a clown for the entertainment. The clown didn't have too many options for entertaining 4 year olds, so he figured a nice game of Simon Says would be fun. "Simon says, 'Point to your nose.'" The children all do it and he continues. "Simon says, 'Point to your head.'" Again they all do. "Sit down." All those who sat down are out. "Simon says, 'Point to your lip.'" Trying to end this silly game and go on with his pathetic life, "Point to your eye." "OWWWWW!!!" Yep. They lost.
