English (US) Joke

*Girl bumps into a guy at the bar* Girl: Oh sorry about that, hi. Guy: Oh! I shouldn't be talking to you. Girl: Why not? Guy: Because when I talk to a pretty girl I always make a fool of myself *The girl smiles, taking this as a compliment* Girl: You're not making a fool of yourself. *The guy looks the girl up and down* Guy: Oh yeah, you're right.
