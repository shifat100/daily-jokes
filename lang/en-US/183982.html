English (US) Joke

Two Mothers Are Sitting Together At An Outdoor Café In Baghdad... **Mother 1**: [*pulls out picture from purse*] "This is my son Abdullah. He would have been 25 now, but, alas... he became a martyr." **Mother 2**: "Ah, yes I remember when he lost his first tooth..." [*sighs*] **Mother 1**: [*pulls out another picture*] "This is my son Hussein. He would have been 22 now, but, alas... he became a martyr." **Mother 2**: "Ah, yes. I remember when he took his first steps..." [*deep sigh*] **Mother 1**: [*pulls out yet another picture*] "This is my son Muhammed. He would have been 18 now, but, alas... he became a martyr." **Mother 2**: "Ah, yes. I remember when he was born..." [*deepest sigh*] "You know, they blow up so fast!"
