English (US) Joke

The lab results are in! Phone rings. — Hello? Is this Miss Smedley? — Yes? Who is this? — This is the hospital. The lab results are in for your husband. But there is a problem. — Oh? — There was a mix-up in the lab, and we’re not sure whether your husband has AIDS or alzheimer’s. — But that’s terrible? What should we do? — Send him on a walk, and if he comes back, do not have sex with him.
