English (US) Joke

Your Star Wars Name To get your Star Wars name, do the following: 1) Start with the 1st 3 letters of your last name 2) Add the first two letters of your first name 3) Add the first two letters of your mother's maiden name 4) Add the first two letters of the city in which you were born 5) Then, if you want, you can remove one letter to make it sound cool.
