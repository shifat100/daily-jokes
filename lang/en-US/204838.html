12 Days of Christmas, A Letter Miss Agnes McHolstein 69 Cash Avenue Beaver Meadow, Col. December 14 Dearest John: I went to the door today and the postman delivered a Partridge in a Pear Tree. What a thoroughly delightful gift! I couldn't have been more surprised. With deepest love and devotion, Agnes ------------------------------------------------------------ Miss Agnes McHolstein 69 Cash Avenue Beaver Meadow, Col.
