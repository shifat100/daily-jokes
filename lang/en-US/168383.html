Free shoes

A very beautiful brunette with long hair, long legs and a petite figure decided to go shopping for shoes. Her friends warned her that the old, lonely man who worked at the shoe-store would try to look up her skirt to see her panties. She really wanted a pair of shoes but was dismayed that the shoe salesman would try to look at her panties. She thought for a while, and then went shoe shopping. She found the perfect pair of shoes, and the shoe salesman gave them to her for free, saying it was the least he could do. Her friends were all jealous that she got the shoes for free. They asked her, did he try to look up your skirt to see your panties? She said yes, but I outsmarted him. When her friends asked how she had outsmarted him she said, I didn't wear any panties, so he couldn't see them even if he wanted to.
