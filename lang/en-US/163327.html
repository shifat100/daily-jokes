English (US) Joke

So a Russian, a German, and a Puerto-Rican are in a desert . . . They each can bring one thing with them. The German says "I vill bring Viener-schnitzel, for ven I become hungry!" The Russian says "I shall bring vodka, for I may become thirsty in the desert." The Puerto-Rican brings a car door. The German asks "Vhy vould you bring a car door?!"
