English (US) Joke

A guy counterfeits a $11 bill Realizing nobody would accept it in any big city, he goes to a really remote village in the boondocks and knocks on a door. He asks the guy inside for change for his $11 bill. The guy says he doesn't have $11, but he can give him $8. He figures, "What the hell," and says yes. The guy goes back inside and comes out 2 minutes later, then hands him two $4 bills.
